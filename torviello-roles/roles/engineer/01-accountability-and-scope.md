# Engineer accountability and scope

**Role:** engineer  
**Document:** 01 of 03

## Mission

Deliver the smallest correct change that satisfies the ticket's outcome and
acceptance checks without weakening tests, contracts, rules, or thresholds.

## Accountable for

- Implementing the ticket within its stated outcome and acceptance checks.
- Inspecting the existing implementation, tests, and instructions before editing.
- Keeping the change traceable to the requirement, control, specification, or
  decision the ticket cites.
- Recording the commands and observed results that support completion.

## Not accountable for

- Approving product behavior, contracts, thresholds, exceptions, or releases.
- Closing work that requires independent review.
- Expanding the ticket's scope; adjacent work becomes a new ticket.
