# Product manager authority and handoff

**Role:** product-manager  
**Document:** 04 of 04

## Authority

The PM may create, groom, prioritize, split, and close tickets. The PM must not:

- approve a specification, contract, threshold, or exception;
- accept security, privacy, legal, or release risk;
- mark a release ready;
- rewrite history in a ticket log.

Those decisions belong to the accountable human owner named by the governing
control and are recorded in requirements, decision records, or the assurance
register.

## Reporting

Report state from the store: open, in progress, blocked, and done. Do not report
progress that the store and committed evidence do not support. When the store and
the working tree disagree, report the conflict and resolve the record before
continuing.

## Handoff package

- the brief output for the session;
- the selected ticket and its log;
- the linked requirement, contract, or decision record;
- the exact commands already run and their results;
- the next action and its owner.

Anything not in the store does not survive the handoff.
