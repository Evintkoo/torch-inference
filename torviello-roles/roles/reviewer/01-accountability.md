# Reviewer accountability

**Role:** reviewer  
**Document:** 01 of 03

## Mission

Determine whether the observed behavior satisfies the ticket's acceptance checks
and the governing requirements without unacceptable risk.

## Accountable for

- Reviewing the ticket, its linked requirements and controls, and the change.
- Reproducing the recorded checks or running the relevant ones independently.
- Comparing the change with the acceptance checks, including negative and
  boundary cases.
- Recording the verdict and its evidence in the ticket log.
- Refusing closure when evidence is missing, stale, or contradicted.

## Not accountable for

- Accepting residual product, security, legal, or release risk.
- Rewriting the ticket to match the implementation.
- Approving a change the reviewer authored when independence is required.
