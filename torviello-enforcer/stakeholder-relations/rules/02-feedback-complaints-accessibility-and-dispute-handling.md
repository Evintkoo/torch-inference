# Feedback Complaints Accessibility and Dispute Handling

**Control family:** stakeholder-relations  
**Control document:** EXT-02  
**Requirement trace:** ORG-005, ORG-031–ORG-032, ORG-042, ED-033  
**Status:** Normative project control

## Control objective

Complaints and feedback are controlled signals about unmet expectations, harm, defects, accessibility barriers, and trust. The process must be easy to find, impartial, timely, reviewable, and linked to improvement while respecting customer rights and privacy.

## Applicability

This control applies whenever a person, team, contractor, coding agent, automated agent, or delegated function performs an activity in scope. Responsibilities are functional roles, not mandated job titles or headcount; one person may hold multiple roles when conflicts and approval-separation risks are controlled. Apply jurisdiction-specific employment, safety, privacy, consumer, and contract rules through an accountable qualified owner.

## Normative requirements

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" are interpreted as described by IETF BCP 14 only when they appear in all capitals.

### EXT-02.01 — Publish how users, customers, and partners can…

**Requirement:** Publish how users, customers, and partners can provide feedback or complain, what information is useful, expected acknowledgment/update, available accessibility alternatives, escalation, and any external dispute or regulator route required by applicable terms/law.

### EXT-02.02 — On receipt, assign an owner, case ID…

**Requirement:** On receipt, assign an owner, case ID, acknowledgment, category, severity, affected people, urgency, and next update. Assess actual or potential harm, privacy/security, accessibility, financial impact, repeated pattern, and whether the complainant needs an immediate protective action.

### EXT-02.03 — The investigator MUST be independent of the disputed decision…

**Requirement:** The investigator MUST be independent of the disputed decision where practical, preserve relevant records, hear the complainant and affected parties fairly, distinguish facts from assumptions, and identify applicable product, policy, contract, and legal requirements.

### EXT-02.04 — The decision-maker MUST state findings, rationale, corrective…

**Requirement:** The decision-maker MUST state findings, rationale, corrective action or remedy, owner, timeline, and review/appeal route. A responder MUST not promise refund, service credit, admission, legal position, or roadmap change outside delegated authority.

### EXT-02.05 — Provide status and outcome within the stated timeframe…

**Requirement:** Provide status and outcome within the stated timeframe and confidentiality limits. If delayed, explain the reason, owner, next update date, and escalation route; do not leave the complainant to repeatedly chase an unowned case.

### EXT-02.06 — When a complaint concerns legal rights, safety…

**Requirement:** When a complaint concerns legal rights, safety, discrimination, security, privacy, or a high-impact product decision, route it to the appropriate specialist process. Internal complaint handling MUST not block a user from applicable external rights or remedy.

### EXT-02.07 — Review recurring themes by product, version, user…

**Requirement:** Review recurring themes by product, version, user group, workflow, and impact. Assign corrective action to root causes; verify later whether complaint recurrence, defect, or barrier decreased, and communicate material product changes.

## Prohibited conditions

- Do not require a user to use inaccessible channels or disclose unnecessary data to complain.
- Do not allow the team whose decision is challenged to be the only appeal authority for a material dispute.
- Do not treat a low complaint count as evidence that users have no problem.

## Decision procedure

Choose severity, investigator, decision authority, and response time using impact, repetition, vulnerability of affected users, legal rights, accessibility, and conflict-of-interest risk. Preserve a route for independent appeal or external escalation for serious cases.

## Assessment procedure

### Examine

- Sample a complete instance of the feedback complaints accessibility and dispute handling workflow from trigger through owner, action, approval, record, user/stakeholder outcome, and closure.
- Inspect the current role map, procedure, access/records, exception, and supporting evidence; confirm the actual workflow matches the written one.

### Interview

- Interview the person performing the work and the accountable owner separately where practical. Ask how they know they are authorized, where to escalate, what happens on failure, and where the durable record lives.

### Test

Trace complaints of a product defect, denied accessibility need, billing dispute, and high-impact safety concern from receipt to remedy and effectiveness review. Test acknowledgment clocks, independence, privacy, appeal, and trend analysis.

## Assessment determination

Pass requires every applicable MUST to be followed and supported by current evidence. Fail applies when authority is unclear, an action bypasses required review, a person cannot identify the safe next step, a required record or safeguard is absent, or evidence is stale. Not applicable requires a stated condition and owner rationale.

## Required evidence

- Workflow record identifying applicable EXT-02 controls, functional owners, approvals, affected requirements, and outcome.
- Relevant access, training, decision, communication, support, audit, or corrective-action evidence, minimized to the data needed to assess the control.
- Approved exception with owner, risk, compensating control, expiry/review date, and revalidation plan when the standard cannot be met.

## Exceptions and nonconformance

A MUST deviation requires approval by an owner authorized for the affected risk, with independent review where practical. Record scope, rationale, impact, interim protection, remediation owner, and expiry or review trigger. Applicable law, contractual duties, safety obligations, and user rights cannot be waived by an internal exception.
