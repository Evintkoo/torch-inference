# ORG-013 — Work practices shall protect dignity, safety, fair treatment, accessibility, and freedom from retaliation, subject to applicable law and approved policy

**Requirement ID:** ORG-013  
**Priority:** MUST

**Status:** Active requirement; no implementation or evaluation evidence is recorded in this repository.

**Acceptance checks:** None recorded  
**Open decisions:** None recorded

## 1. Normative requirement

Work practices shall protect dignity, safety, fair treatment, accessibility, and freedom from retaliation, subject to applicable law and approved policy.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

This requirement is part of **Workforce lifecycle and capability** in [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md). It is traced to 5 rule control(s): PPL-03, PPL-05, PPL-06, PPL-07, SEC-13. Those controls state the following purpose:

- **PPL-03 — Respectful Accessible and Safe Work:** Work practices must protect people’s safety, dignity, accessibility, and ability to raise concerns. The organization must maintain qualified, jurisdiction-aware policies and fair response processes instead of relying on informal norms or individual goodwill.
- **PPL-05 — Work Time Availability Leave and Expense Integrity:** People must follow approved work-time, availability, leave, expense, and resource-use policies with accurate records and safe escalation. These controls require a policy owner and lawful, transparent procedures; they do not define a universal schedule, compensation model, or employment arrangement.
- **PPL-06 — Recruitment Selection and Hiring Decisions:** Recruitment and selection decisions must be job-related, fair, authorized, privacy-preserving, and supported by consistent evidence. The hiring process must identify the capability needed and select for that capability without promising terms or collecting information beyond lawful business need.
- **PPL-07 — Performance Development Recognition and Corrective Action:** People and teams need clear expectations, useful feedback, development support, and fair accountability. Performance processes must improve outcomes and capability without reducing complex work to a single metric or bypassing approved employment policies.
- **SEC-13 — Physical Workspace Remote Work and Asset Protection:** People must protect devices, conversations, records, and physical access wherever work occurs. Controls must match the sensitivity of information and actual threat, remain usable for the workforce, and respect jurisdictional privacy and workplace rules.

Acceptance is not satisfied by citing these controls. The implementation must demonstrate the observable result in section 6, and the mapped rule clauses in section 5 define what must hold at the enforcement boundary.

## 3. Priority and interpretation

This requirement has **MUST** priority under the repository's requirement conventions. A MUST requirement is needed for the stated product direction or a safe release. ## 5. Detailed obligations

Each subsection below is the complete normative content of a mapped control. A requirement is not satisfied by selecting one convenient clause; every applicable clause must hold, including its negative and boundary behavior.

### PPL-03 — Respectful Accessible and Safe Work

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [03-respectful-accessible-and-safe-work.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/03-respectful-accessible-and-safe-work.md)

**Control objective.** Work practices must protect people’s safety, dignity, accessibility, and ability to raise concerns. The organization must maintain qualified, jurisdiction-aware policies and fair response processes instead of relying on informal norms or individual goodwill.

#### PPL-03.01 — A designated owner MUST maintain current, accessible policies…

**Requirement:** A designated owner MUST maintain current, accessible policies for respectful conduct, discrimination/harassment prevention, accessibility accommodations, workplace health/safety relevant to the work, acceptable technology use, and non-retaliation. Policy scope, reporting routes, responsible handlers, and applicable jurisdictions MUST be stated.

#### PPL-03.02 — People MUST have an accessible reporting route for unsafe…

**Requirement:** People MUST have an accessible reporting route for unsafe, discriminatory, harassing, retaliatory, or coercive behavior and an alternative when the normal manager or handler is implicated. Emergency or immediate physical danger MUST have a direct urgent response route.

#### PPL-03.03 — Reports MUST be acknowledged, risk-triaged, assigned…

**Requirement:** Reports MUST be acknowledged, risk-triaged, assigned to a trained impartial handler, and handled with access limited to need-to-know. The process MUST state confidentiality limits, evidence preservation, protective measures, fair response, escalation, and closure.

#### PPL-03.04 — The organization MUST prohibit retaliation for good-faith reporting…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST prohibit retaliation for good-faith reporting, requesting accommodation, participating in an investigation, or raising a safety/quality concern. Monitor for adverse treatment after reports and provide a separate escalation path.

#### PPL-03.05 — Work tools, documentation, meetings, training…

**Requirement:** Work tools, documentation, meetings, training, and reporting channels SHOULD be usable by people with differing access needs. When an accommodation or accessibility barrier is raised, assign an owner, assess confidentiality and safety, and provide an effective alternative while resolving the barrier.

#### PPL-03.06 — Investigations and performance decisions MUST distinguish observed facts…

**Requirement:** Investigations and performance decisions MUST distinguish observed facts, inferences, and unverified claims; provide affected people a fair opportunity to respond when appropriate; and follow qualified HR/legal advice for regulated or sensitive cases.

#### PPL-03.07 — Aggregate trends in reports, safety issues, accommodations…

**Requirement:** Aggregate trends in reports, safety issues, accommodations, and recurring barriers to improve systems. Share only the minimum personal detail needed for action; protect source identity and records under approved retention and legal-hold rules.

### PPL-05 — Work Time Availability Leave and Expense Integrity

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [05-work-time-availability-leave-and-expense-integrity.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/05-work-time-availability-leave-and-expense-integrity.md)

**Control objective.** People must follow approved work-time, availability, leave, expense, and resource-use policies with accurate records and safe escalation. These controls require a policy owner and lawful, transparent procedures; they do not define a universal schedule, compensation model, or employment arrangement.

#### PPL-05.01 — Each role MUST have documented expectations for availability…

**Requirement:** Each role MUST have documented expectations for availability, on-call duty, response windows, work location, time recording if required, and escalation. Expectations MUST be consistent with the applicable agreement, service promise, and jurisdiction-specific policy; informal messages MUST NOT create hidden permanent obligations.

#### PPL-05.02 — Where time or activity records are required…

**Requirement:** Where time or activity records are required, the person MUST report actual work, leave, on-call, or expense information accurately and promptly. Managers MUST correct process or workload issues rather than pressure people to falsify records or work off the record.

#### PPL-05.03 — Leave and absence MUST use the approved confidential process…

**Requirement:** Leave and absence MUST use the approved confidential process, identify only information required by policy, and trigger appropriate duty coverage and access/delegation review. Managers MUST not disclose sensitive leave information to coworkers beyond operational need.

#### PPL-05.04 — A person requesting reimbursement or using organizational funds MUST…

**Requirement:** A person requesting reimbursement or using organizational funds MUST state business purpose, amount, date, recipient, and evidence required by policy, disclose conflicts, and use approved categories. Approvers MUST verify legitimacy, policy, budget, and separation-of-duties controls.

#### PPL-05.05 — Cards, accounts, credits, cloud budgets…

**Requirement:** Cards, accounts, credits, cloud budgets, travel booking, and subscriptions MUST be assigned to an authorized person, purpose, limit, and reconciliation owner. Lost instruments, suspicious charges, duplicate claims, and out-of-policy purchases MUST be reported promptly.

#### PPL-05.06 — People MUST be able to report fatigue, excessive…

**Requirement:** People MUST be able to report fatigue, excessive workload, unsafe work hours, conflicting on-call assignments, or inability to meet response commitments without retaliation. The owner MUST rebalance work, change coverage, or reduce the service commitment rather than conceal the risk.

#### PPL-05.07 — Time, leave, payroll, reimbursement…

**Requirement:** Time, leave, payroll, reimbursement, and expense evidence MUST be access-controlled and retained only according to approved legal, financial, employment, and audit requirements. Product or engineering repositories MUST NOT become shadow HR or finance databases.

### PPL-06 — Recruitment Selection and Hiring Decisions

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [06-recruitment-selection-and-hiring-decisions.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/06-recruitment-selection-and-hiring-decisions.md)

**Control objective.** Recruitment and selection decisions must be job-related, fair, authorized, privacy-preserving, and supported by consistent evidence. The hiring process must identify the capability needed and select for that capability without promising terms or collecting information beyond lawful business need.

#### PPL-06.01 — Before recruitment begins, an accountable owner MUST approve…

**Requirement:** Before recruitment begins, an accountable owner MUST approve the role purpose, outcomes, essential competencies, reporting/delegation boundary, budget, employment or contractor form, and decision authority. Do not recruit for an undefined title or imply a role grants unapproved product, spending, or access authority.

#### PPL-06.02 — Selection criteria and interview/assessment methods MUST relate to actual…

**Requirement:** Selection criteria and interview/assessment methods MUST relate to actual role outcomes and be consistently applied to candidates. Define essential versus trainable capabilities, accessible participation, scoring or evidence method, and decision threshold before evaluating applicants.

#### PPL-06.03 — Interviewers MUST disclose personal, financial, or other…

**Requirement:** Interviewers MUST disclose personal, financial, or other conflicts and recuse or use independent review where appropriate. Hiring decisions MUST be based on documented job-related evidence, not protected characteristics, affinity, prestige, personal similarity, or undisclosed criteria, subject to applicable law.

#### PPL-06.04 — Collect only candidate information needed for the approved process…

**Requirement:** Collect only candidate information needed for the approved process; provide required notices and consent where applicable; restrict access; secure interview notes and assessments; set retention/deletion triggers; and use approved systems. Sensitive screening MUST be lawful, proportionate, and performed by authorized roles.

#### PPL-06.05 — Reference checks, background checks, work samples…

**Requirement:** Reference checks, background checks, work samples, psychometric tests, and automated ranking MUST have an authorized purpose, valid criteria, proportionality, transparency where required, appropriate human review, and a challenge/correction path. An automated score MUST NOT be the sole basis for a consequential decision without an approved lawful process.

#### PPL-06.06 — Only an authorized role MAY make or change…

**Requirement:** Only an authorized role MAY make or change an offer, compensation term, work location, start date, confidentiality/IP term, or contractual commitment. Candidate communications MUST be timely, accurate, respectful, accessible, and consistent with approved terms.

#### PPL-06.07 — The owner MUST record the selection rationale and approvals…

**Requirement:** The owner MUST record the selection rationale and approvals in protected records, notify candidates appropriately, dispose of data under retention rules, and hand the approved role, duties, conditions, training, and access requirements to the onboarding owner.

### PPL-07 — Performance Development Recognition and Corrective Action

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [07-performance-development-recognition-and-corrective-action.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/07-performance-development-recognition-and-corrective-action.md)

**Control objective.** People and teams need clear expectations, useful feedback, development support, and fair accountability. Performance processes must improve outcomes and capability without reducing complex work to a single metric or bypassing approved employment policies.

#### PPL-07.01 — Each role MUST have current expectations tied to responsibilities…

**Requirement:** Each role MUST have current expectations tied to responsibilities, authority, quality, collaboration, safety, and outcome measures relevant to the work. Expectations MUST be communicated before an evaluation period and revised when scope changes.

#### PPL-07.02 — Managers or designated reviewers MUST provide feedback based on…

**Requirement:** Managers or designated reviewers MUST provide feedback based on observed work and agreed expectations, explain evidence and impact, invite context, and identify actionable next steps. Material feedback MUST not be delivered for the first time as an undocumented consequential decision.

#### PPL-07.03 — Evaluation MUST consider outcome quality, user and team…

**Requirement:** Evaluation MUST consider outcome quality, user and team impact, role scope, constraints, collaboration, incident learning, and opportunities provided. Metrics MUST be contextual and MUST NOT be the sole basis for pay, promotion, discipline, or separation decisions.

#### PPL-07.04 — Before attributing failure to an individual, review workload…

**Requirement:** Before attributing failure to an individual, review workload, unclear authority, missing training, unsafe incentives, tools, dependencies, staffing, and process design. People remain accountable for intentional or negligent conduct under policy, but systemic contributors MUST be addressed as well.

#### PPL-07.05 — When capability gaps are remediable, define support…

**Requirement:** When capability gaps are remediable, define support, mentoring/training, time, expected improvement evidence, and review date. Promotion and recognition criteria MUST be role-related, consistently applied, conflict-reviewed, and accessible under applicable policy.

#### PPL-07.06 — Compensation, promotion, discipline, performance plans…

**Requirement:** Compensation, promotion, discipline, performance plans, termination, or other consequential employment decisions MUST be made by authorized roles using documented evidence, conflict checks, approved policy, and qualified HR/legal review when required by jurisdiction.

#### PPL-07.07 — A person MUST be able to correct factual errors…

**Requirement:** A person MUST be able to correct factual errors and request review of a consequential decision through a route not controlled solely by the original decision-maker where practical. Reviewers MUST consider evidence and policy consistently and protect against retaliation.

#### PPL-07.08 — Performance notes, compensation, health, accommodation…

**Requirement:** Performance notes, compensation, health, accommodation, and corrective-action records MUST have restricted access, retention and deletion rules, and data minimization. Use aggregate team-level learning without exposing individual details unnecessarily.

#### PPL-07.09 — The responsible owner MUST periodically review whether expectations…

**Requirement:** The responsible owner MUST periodically review whether expectations, metrics, workload, opportunity, feedback, and outcomes produce unexplained disparities or perverse incentives. Investigate concerns and update the process based on evidence without assuming a metric alone proves discrimination or fairness.

### SEC-13 — Physical Workspace Remote Work and Asset Protection

*Domain:* [security](../../../torviello-enforcer/security/README.md) · *Rule:* [13-physical-workspace-remote-work-and-asset-protection.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/security/rules/13-physical-workspace-remote-work-and-asset-protection.md)

**Control objective.** People must protect devices, conversations, records, and physical access wherever work occurs. Controls must match the sensitivity of information and actual threat, remain usable for the workforce, and respect jurisdictional privacy and workplace rules.

#### SEC-13.01 — The organization MUST define requirements for office, home…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST define requirements for office, home, travel, coworking, and other work locations based on information sensitivity, device type, local law, and customer/contract conditions. Restrictions or exceptions MUST be stated, approved, and supported with practical alternatives.

#### SEC-13.02 — Work devices MUST use approved authentication, encryption…

**Requirement:** Work devices MUST use approved authentication, encryption, screen lock, supported security updates, endpoint protection where available, and remote lock/wipe capability proportionate to risk. Removable media MUST be restricted, encrypted, inventoried, and sanitized before reuse or disposal.

#### SEC-13.03 — Workers MUST position screens, control printing, secure…

**Requirement:** Workers MUST position screens, control printing, secure paper records, and avoid discussing confidential work where unauthorized people can see or hear it. Use privacy filters, locked storage, secure disposal, or private rooms where sensitivity warrants.

#### SEC-13.04 — Work locations that host sensitive systems, records…

**Requirement:** Work locations that host sensitive systems, records, or equipment MUST identify authorized access, visitor escort/record requirements, key/card custody, alarm response, and lost credential escalation. Do not leave visitors unattended in restricted areas.

#### SEC-13.05 — When working while traveling, protect devices from theft…

**Requirement:** When working while traveling, protect devices from theft, shoulder surfing, tampering, and unattended access; use approved network protections and avoid sensitive work where privacy cannot be assured. Report loss, suspected tampering, border seizure, or exposure promptly.

#### SEC-13.06 — Loss, theft, damage, unexpected access…

**Requirement:** Loss, theft, damage, unexpected access, fire, flood, power failure, or environmental hazard affecting work assets MUST be reported through a defined route. The owner MUST assess credential revocation, data exposure, business continuity, recovery, and notification needs.

#### SEC-13.07 — Before transfer, return, resale, or disposal…

**Requirement:** Before transfer, return, resale, or disposal, remove organizational accounts and securely erase storage under an approved method; recover licenses and accessories; verify disposal for sensitive media; and record custody transfer where required.

#### SEC-13.08 — People MUST receive concise guidance for the locations…

**Requirement:** People MUST receive concise guidance for the locations and devices they use, including reporting lost assets, safe travel, remote meetings, printing, and household/shared-device boundaries. Training MUST NOT rely on surveillance of personal spaces.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

The following conditions are explicitly prohibited by the mapped controls. Any one of them is sufficient to fail the requirement.

- [PPL-03] Do not retaliate or permit informal punishment for raising a concern or requesting an accommodation.
- [PPL-03] Do not promise absolute confidentiality or conduct an investigation beyond the handler’s authority and competence.
- [PPL-03] Do not treat a hostile or inaccessible work environment as an individual resilience problem.
- [PPL-05] Do not submit knowingly false time, expense, leave, or reimbursement records.
- [PPL-05] Do not use personal payment or accounts to evade approval, procurement, tax, or security controls.
- [PPL-05] Do not disclose private employment or financial details to establish routine operational coverage.
- [PPL-06] Do not ask candidates to provide confidential information from a current or former employer.
- [PPL-06] Do not promise employment, compensation, access, remote-work terms, or product authority without approval.
- [PPL-06] Do not use candidate data, automated tools, or screening results outside the disclosed and approved process.
- [PPL-07] Do not use a single productivity metric, commit count, hours online, or model-generated score as a complete measure of a person’s contribution.
- [PPL-07] Do not surprise a person with a consequential decision based on expectations they were not given.
- [PPL-07] Do not retaliate against a person for protected reporting, accommodation requests, or raising a good-faith concern.
- [SEC-13] Do not store sensitive work on unmanaged personal devices or personal cloud accounts without an approved exception.
- [SEC-13] Do not expose customer data, credentials, unreleased product information, or confidential conversations in public spaces.
- [SEC-13] Do not install intrusive monitoring in personal workspaces without lawful authority, necessity, transparency, and privacy review.
