# ENG-008 — Synthesize speech with bounded streaming and deterministic selection

**Requirement ID:** ENG-008  
**Priority:** MUST  
**Source:** [engine-runtime.md](../engine-runtime.md)  
**Acceptance checks:** [AC-ENG-14](../../../torviello-engine/specifications/checks/AC-ENG-14.md)  
**Rule controls:** API-02, PERF-05, API-03

## 1. Normative requirement

The engine MUST validate TTS text and parameters, select an engine and voice deterministically, return playable full audio or framed PCM streaming audio, bound gaps between chunks, and report engine and voice metadata without silent fallback.

The capitalized keywords MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are normative in the sense of BCP 14. Where a clause below is conditional, the stated condition determines applicability.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

Speech output is consumed by realtime callers that cannot wait for a complete file. The reference implementation supports full WAV synthesis and sentence-level PCM streaming, clamps speed and pitch, rejects empty and oversized text, applies per-chunk timeouts, lists engines and voices, and exposes TTS health and statistics.

Torviello requires those behaviors to be consistent across aliases and to be observable to clients: the selected engine, the applied parameters, the sample format, and a terminal error when a stream cannot continue. Deterministic selection prevents a missing voice from silently changing the product's voice identity.

## 3. Priority and interpretation

This requirement is **MUST**. A MUST requirement is necessary for the stated engine behavior and for a safe release; an unmet MUST blocks release unless an approved exception with compensating measures, an owner, and an expiry exists. A SHOULD requirement is expected unless a documented tradeoff justifies deferral; the tradeoff must still name the affected checks and the residual risk.

Priority applies to the behavior observed through the shipping engine, not to the presence of code, a design document, or a test double. Passing evidence must exercise the same boundary that carries the risk.

## 4. Scope and applicability

Applies to `/tts/synthesize`, `/tts/stream`, their versioned aliases, audio synthesis aliases, and the TTS WebSocket path. It covers validation, engine and voice selection, parameter clamping, full and streamed output, timeouts, and metadata. It does not define model quality evaluation.

The requirement applies to new and materially changed behavior in the declared profiles. A legacy path becomes subject to it when changed, when it participates in a claimed capability, or when a migration explicitly brings it into scope. Behavior outside the declared platform matrix may be excluded only when the exclusion is published and the unsupported path fails clearly.

## 5. Detailed obligations

The following obligations refine the normative statement for design, implementation, and review.

- Reject empty text and enforce a documented maximum text length before invoking an engine.
- Resolve the requested engine and voice or use documented defaults; reject unknown identifiers instead of falling back silently.
- Clamp speed and pitch to supported ranges and report the applied values.
- Return a valid WAV container for full synthesis and raw PCM with an explicit sample rate, channel count, and sample format for streaming.
- Bound the first chunk and the gap between subsequent chunks with configured deadlines and terminate the stream on timeout.
- Emit ordered audio chunks and stop cleanly on client disconnect.
- Report engine, voice, sample rate, duration when known, and postprocessing state in response metadata.

### 5.2 Highest-standard assurance

This requirement is assessed at **HPA-4**, the Highest Practical Assurance level defined in [assurance-standard.md](../assurance-standard.md). The following obligations are additional to section 5 and equally normative.

**Applicable standards:** ISO/IEC 25010:2023 (performance efficiency, functional correctness), OWASP ASVS 5.0.0 Level 3 (input validation), OpenTelemetry 1.44.0, IETF BCP 14.

- The response MUST declare sample rate, channel count, encoding, byte order, and stream framing; a header MUST agree with the emitted bytes.
- Frame order MUST be preserved and timestamps or sequence numbers MUST be monotonic; a stream MUST NOT emit an unmarked gap or reordered chunk.
- Synthesis limits for text length, speed, pitch, format, and requested duration MUST be enforced before model execution.
- Client disconnect, deadline expiry, and worker failure MUST cancel the producer and release the model reference and stream slot.

A claim of conformance to an external standard is prohibited unless the complete control-by-control evidence exists; citing the standard is not evidence.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

### 6.0 HPA-4 acceptance rule

Acceptance is binary and evidence-gated by [`acceptance-criteria.md`](../../../torviello-engine/specifications/acceptance-criteria.md). The check-specific threshold in section 3 of that document is normative; the outcomes below are necessary but not sufficient. One occurrence of any failure criterion in section 4 of that document fails acceptance, and no partial or conditional pass is permitted.


The requirement is satisfied when all of the following observable outcomes hold on a stated revision and environment.

- Full synthesis returns audio/wav with a valid header at the documented rate and layout.
- Streaming synthesis returns audio/pcm chunks in order with the documented encoding.
- Empty text returns 400 tts.empty_text without engine invocation.
- Out-of-range speed and pitch are clamped and reported.
- A stalled engine ends the stream with tts.chunk_timeout.
- An unknown engine or voice returns 404 and no fallback runs.

Acceptance checks: [AC-ENG-14](../../../torviello-engine/specifications/checks/AC-ENG-14.md).

### 6.1 Highest-standard gates

These gates are additional to the outcomes in section 6. A gate that cannot be executed is a blocked check, not a pass.

- A property test MUST prove that framing, declared metadata, and decoded duration agree for randomized text and parameter sets.
- Long-stream tests MUST include slow consumers, mid-stream disconnect, deadline expiry, and backpressure at the bounded channel.
- The audio decoder and text/parameter parser MUST be fuzzed for at least 24 CPU-hours with no panic, hang, or unbounded allocation.

All gates MUST run against the shipping artifact and configuration, with retained, reproducible evidence.

## 7. Implementation guidance and assessment determination

Create a TTS capability interface that returns either a complete audio buffer or a bounded chunk receiver. Resolve engine and voice through a catalog before synthesis. Wrap the receiver in a timeout-aware stream that distinguishes first-chunk and steady-state deadlines. Build metadata from the same resolved selection used to synthesize.

A determination of **pass** requires that every applicable MUST clause is implemented at the authoritative boundary, that each acceptance check has current reproducible evidence, and that no prohibited condition occurs. A determination of **fail** applies when implementation is absent, behavior contradicts the requirement, evidence is missing or stale, or the result cannot be reproduced. **Not applicable** requires a stated deployment or platform condition and reviewer rationale; it cannot be used to excuse an unimplemented capability that the profile claims to support.

Assessment combines examination, interview where an operational procedure is involved, and test. Examination confirms that the design and source address the boundary. Test confirms observable behavior, including at least one negative or boundary case per limit, failure mode, or security decision. Documentation alone does not establish effectiveness.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

- Any failure criterion in [`acceptance-criteria.md`](../../../torviello-engine/specifications/acceptance-criteria.md) section 4 is also prohibited and fatal.


- Do not stream arbitrary container bytes under an audio/pcm label.
- Do not silently substitute a different engine or voice.
- Do not hold a connection open indefinitely when an engine stalls.
- Do not emit out-of-order or duplicated chunks.
- Do not log synthesized text or raw audio content.

Additional control prohibitions apply where the mapped clauses state them. A prohibited condition is a failure even if a happy-path scenario passes.

## 9. Verification procedure

- 1. Synthesize a short text in full and streaming modes and inspect container and chunk headers.
- 2. Submit empty and long texts and inspect validation responses.
- 3. Submit extreme speed and pitch values and inspect metadata.
- 4. Use a stub engine that delays beyond the chunk timeout.
- 5. Request an unknown engine and voice.
- 6. Record request, output inspection, and timing.

The procedure must be executed through the shipping path. A unit-only substitute does not establish that the public boundary enforces the requirement. Record skipped steps and their reason; a skipped check is not passing evidence.

### 9.1 Highest-standard procedure

In addition to the steps above:

- 9. Validate PCM and WAV output with an independent decoder and compare declared and observed format.
- 10. Replay the long-stream and disconnect cases and inspect producer termination.

Each step MUST retain its command, input digests, environment, and observed result. A skipped step is recorded as skipped and does not count toward a pass.

## 10. Required evidence

- WAV header inspection and streaming chunk trace.
- Validation responses for empty and oversized text.
- Metadata showing clamped parameters.
- Timeout terminal event and connection close.
- Unknown identifier error responses.

Evidence must identify the revision, environment, configuration, fixture, command or inspection, observed result, and any limitation. Evidence that predates a relevant change is stale.

### 10.1 Highest-standard evidence

Retain the following with the evidence bundle described in [assurance-standard.md](../assurance-standard.md):

- Audio validation reports for every supported format.
- A stream timeline showing monotonic frames and terminal behavior.
- Cancellation evidence for disconnect and deadline expiry.

Evidence MUST record the source revision, build inputs, toolchain, hardware, configuration, model digests, command, observed result, threshold, and limitations. A summary without retained measurements is not evidence.

## 11. Failure conditions

- Full output is not a valid WAV or streamed framing is undocumented.
- A missing engine or voice silently uses a default.
- A stalled engine holds the stream open.
- Chunks arrive out of order or duplicated.
- Text or audio content appears in logs.

A failure also exists when an approved exception is expired, lacks compensating measures, or is not linked from the affected check.

### 11.1 Highest-standard failure conditions

- HPA-4 assurance evidence is missing, stale, unsigned where required, or below the recorded threshold.

- Declared sample rate, encoding, or channel count differs from the emitted audio.
- A stream reorders, drops, or interleaves frames without a documented terminal event.
- A disconnected stream continues consuming model or worker resources.
