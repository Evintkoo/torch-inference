# ORG-024 — Meetings and synchronous collaboration shall have a purpose, necessary participants, decisions/actions, and durable record when they create commitments or change direction

**Requirement ID:** ORG-024  
**Priority:** MUST  
**Source:** [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md) — Planning, collaboration, and knowledge  
**Status:** Active requirement; no implementation or evaluation evidence is recorded in this repository.  
**Rule controls:** COL-01, COL-02  
**Acceptance checks:** None recorded  
**Open decisions:** None recorded

## 1. Normative requirement

Meetings and synchronous collaboration shall have a purpose, necessary participants, decisions/actions, and durable record when they create commitments or change direction.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

This requirement is part of **Planning, collaboration, and knowledge** in [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md). It is traced to 2 rule control(s): COL-01, COL-02. Those controls state the following purpose:

- **COL-01 — Communication Meetings Reviews and Handoffs:** Communication must preserve context, ownership, confidentiality, and actionability across people, teams, time zones, and tools. Synchronous conversations are for interaction; authoritative decisions and commitments must remain findable after participants leave.
- **COL-02 — Organizational Records Knowledge and Information Lifecycle:** Information created during work must have a source of truth, owner, audience, access level, retention, and update path. The organization must preserve what future workers need while minimizing personal, confidential, and duplicate records.

Acceptance is not satisfied by citing these controls. The implementation must demonstrate the observable result in section 6, and the mapped rule clauses in section 5 define what must hold at the enforcement boundary.

## 3. Priority and interpretation

This requirement has **MUST** priority under the repository's requirement conventions. A MUST requirement is needed for the stated product direction or a safe release. A SHOULD requirement is expected unless a documented tradeoff justifies deferral. A COULD requirement is a candidate capability that requires product or technical validation. Acceptance describes observable evidence; it does not mandate a specific implementation unless the requirement says so.

## 5. Detailed obligations

Each subsection below is the complete normative content of a mapped control. A requirement is not satisfied by selecting one convenient clause; every applicable clause must hold, including its negative and boundary behavior.

### COL-01 — Communication Meetings Reviews and Handoffs

*Domain:* [collaboration-and-communication](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/README.md) · *Rule:* [01-communication-meetings-reviews-and-handoffs.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/rules/01-communication-meetings-reviews-and-handoffs.md)

**Control objective.** Communication must preserve context, ownership, confidentiality, and actionability across people, teams, time zones, and tools. Synchronous conversations are for interaction; authoritative decisions and commitments must remain findable after participants leave.

#### COL-01.01 — The sender MUST use an approved channel appropriate…

**Requirement:** The sender MUST use an approved channel appropriate to urgency, audience, confidentiality, retention, accessibility, and audit needs. Sensitive information MUST use an access-controlled approved system; urgent incidents MUST use the defined escalation route rather than waiting for ordinary asynchronous response.

#### COL-01.02 — A work or decision request MUST state context…

**Requirement:** A work or decision request MUST state context, desired outcome, owner/recipient, decision deadline, impact of delay, supporting links, and response needed. If the recipient lacks authority or context, they MUST redirect or escalate rather than implying approval.

#### COL-01.03 — A meeting MUST have a clear purpose, expected…

**Requirement:** A meeting MUST have a clear purpose, expected decision/output, necessary participants, and accessible material. The facilitator MUST distinguish information sharing, consultation, decision, and approval; attendance or silence MUST NOT be treated as consent.

#### COL-01.04 — A meeting or review that changes scope, risk…

**Requirement:** A meeting or review that changes scope, risk acceptance, product behavior, resource commitment, external messaging, or ownership MUST record decision, rationale, approver, dissent/conditions where material, action owner, and due/review date in the authoritative record.

#### COL-01.05 — A handoff MUST include current state, owner…

**Requirement:** A handoff MUST include current state, owner, context, links/artifacts, user impact, dependencies, deadlines, risks, pending decisions, and next action. High-risk handoffs MUST be explicitly accepted; define escalation if acknowledgment does not arrive.

#### COL-01.06 — Teams MUST provide enough written context and reasonable response…

**Requirement:** Teams MUST provide enough written context and reasonable response expectations for people not present in a live meeting. Critical decisions MUST not be made solely in a private channel inaccessible to impacted owners or reviewers.

#### COL-01.07 — When owners disagree, restate the decision to be…

**Requirement:** When owners disagree, restate the decision to be made, evidence and constraints, applicable control, options, and accountable decision-maker. Preserve respectful conduct; escalate unresolved high-impact conflicts to the named authority and record the result.

#### COL-01.08 — Recipients MUST respect channel membership and classification, verify…

**Requirement:** Recipients MUST respect channel membership and classification, verify recipients before sending sensitive material, and correct misdelivery through the incident process. Forwarding, screenshots, transcripts, recordings, or AI summaries MUST follow the applicable consent, privacy, retention, and access rules.

### COL-02 — Organizational Records Knowledge and Information Lifecycle

*Domain:* [collaboration-and-communication](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/README.md) · *Rule:* [02-organizational-records-knowledge-and-information-lifecycle.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/rules/02-organizational-records-knowledge-and-information-lifecycle.md)

**Control objective.** Information created during work must have a source of truth, owner, audience, access level, retention, and update path. The organization must preserve what future workers need while minimizing personal, confidential, and duplicate records.

#### COL-02.01 — A record owner MUST identify purpose, sensitivity…

**Requirement:** A record owner MUST identify purpose, sensitivity, authoritative status, audience, access, retention trigger, and applicable legal hold for material decisions, contracts, incidents, support cases, releases, training, and procedures. Apply approved classifications and data minimization rules.

#### COL-02.02 — For each process or decision, designate the authoritative…

**Requirement:** For each process or decision, designate the authoritative record and link secondary discussion to it. Copies and summaries MUST identify their source and date; where systems conflict, the record owner MUST resolve and announce the canonical version.

#### COL-02.03 — Operational, security, support, and recovery procedures…

**Requirement:** Operational, security, support, and recovery procedures MUST identify prerequisites, role, safe steps, expected outcome, failure/escalation, last review, and verification method. Test high-impact procedures periodically and after material change or incident.

#### COL-02.04 — When a role, owner, vendor…

**Requirement:** When a role, owner, vendor, or product area changes, transfer decision context, undocumented constraints, credentials via approved secret management, open risks, and current work to an authorized successor. Do not place secrets in documentation or handoff notes.

#### COL-02.05 — Authoritative records MUST have access appropriate to sensitivity…

**Requirement:** Authoritative records MUST have access appropriate to sensitivity, identifiable owners, version/change history proportionate to importance, and recovery from accidental deletion or corruption. Changes to policy, approvals, or audit evidence MUST preserve who changed what and when.

#### COL-02.06 — Retain records only for the approved business, legal…

**Requirement:** Retain records only for the approved business, legal, safety, security, or contractual purpose. At the retention trigger, delete or archive through the approved process; legal holds suspend ordinary deletion only for the scoped records and duration.

#### COL-02.07 — For critical capabilities and procedures, identify single-person dependency…

**Requirement:** For critical capabilities and procedures, identify single-person dependency, recovery time, and backup competence. Mitigate through cross-training, tested documentation, successor assignment, vendor transition, or explicit accepted risk.

#### COL-02.08 — Workers MUST be able to locate current procedures…

**Requirement:** Workers MUST be able to locate current procedures, decisions, ownership, escalation contacts, and approved templates without relying on private introductions. Keep material readable and accessible to intended users and provide controlled alternatives when a person cannot use the default channel.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

The following conditions are explicitly prohibited by the mapped controls. Any one of them is sufficient to fail the requirement.

- [COL-01] Do not treat a reaction, meeting attendance, or lack of response as formal approval.
- [COL-01] Do not store the only copy of a business-critical decision in personal chat or email.
- [COL-01] Do not include sensitive personnel, customer, or security details in broad channels.
- [COL-02] Do not place credentials, private keys, personal data, or customer payloads in general knowledge bases.
- [COL-02] Do not leave a material decision in a personal inbox, draft, or tool without an authoritative record.
- [COL-02] Do not preserve duplicate records indefinitely without a purpose, owner, and retention rule.
