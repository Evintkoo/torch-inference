# ORG-002 — Decision authority shall be proportional to impact, reversibility, exposure, and affected stakeholders

**Requirement ID:** ORG-002  
**Priority:** MUST  
**Source:** [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md) — Authority, roles, and decisions  
**Status:** Active requirement; no implementation or evaluation evidence is recorded in this repository.  
**Rule controls:** COL-03, ORG-01, ORG-04, PPL-02, PPL-04, PPL-06, EXT-03  
**Acceptance checks:** None recorded  
**Open decisions:** None recorded

## 1. Normative requirement

Decision authority shall be proportional to impact, reversibility, exposure, and affected stakeholders.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

This requirement is part of **Authority, roles, and decisions** in [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md). It is traced to 7 rule control(s): COL-03, ORG-01, ORG-04, PPL-02, PPL-04, PPL-06, EXT-03. Those controls state the following purpose:

- **COL-03 — Cross-Team Coordination Review and Escalation:** Work that crosses domain, team, or organizational boundaries must identify interface owners, dependencies, decisions, risks, and review points. A handoff is complete only when the receiving owner can act and the sending owner’s remaining accountability is clear.
- **ORG-01 — Functional Roles Accountability and Decision Rights:** People must know which outcomes they own, what authority they can exercise, who must review a decision, and who accepts residual risk. Clear authority is required for routine work, exceptions, emergencies, external commitments, and succession.
- **ORG-04 — Spending Procurement and Organizational Commitments:** People must not create financial, contractual, data, service, or operational commitments without an authorized sponsor and a review proportionate to lifecycle cost and risk. Procurement and commitments include subscriptions, infrastructure, software, datasets, models, contractors, marketing, public promises, and renewals.
- **PPL-02 — Competence Training Supervision and Delegation:** People may perform work only within their competence, authorization, and supervision. Training and qualification must be specific to the decisions and risks a person is expected to handle, and must be refreshed when systems or duties change.
- **PPL-04 — Work Intake Prioritization and Individual Execution:** Every work action must have a clear purpose, owner, authorized scope, completion condition, and escalation path. Teams must balance planned work, incidents, maintenance, learning, and stakeholder commitments without relying on hidden queues or personal memory.
- **PPL-06 — Recruitment Selection and Hiring Decisions:** Recruitment and selection decisions must be job-related, fair, authorized, privacy-preserving, and supported by consistent evidence. The hiring process must identify the capability needed and select for that capability without promising terms or collecting information beyond lawful business need.
- **EXT-03 — Public Statements Product Claims and Marketing Content:** External-facing claims shape user decisions and organizational obligations. Product, security, privacy, licensing, performance, accessibility, model, and commercial statements must be accurate, scoped, evidenced, approved, and corrected when they become stale.

Acceptance is not satisfied by citing these controls. The implementation must demonstrate the observable result in section 6, and the mapped rule clauses in section 5 define what must hold at the enforcement boundary.

## 3. Priority and interpretation

This requirement has **MUST** priority under the repository's requirement conventions. A MUST requirement is needed for the stated product direction or a safe release. A SHOULD requirement is expected unless a documented tradeoff justifies deferral. A COULD requirement is a candidate capability that requires product or technical validation. Acceptance describes observable evidence; it does not mandate a specific implementation unless the requirement says so.

## 5. Detailed obligations

Each subsection below is the complete normative content of a mapped control. A requirement is not satisfied by selecting one convenient clause; every applicable clause must hold, including its negative and boundary behavior.

### COL-03 — Cross-Team Coordination Review and Escalation

*Domain:* [collaboration-and-communication](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/README.md) · *Rule:* [03-cross-team-coordination-review-and-escalation.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/rules/03-cross-team-coordination-review-and-escalation.md)

**Control objective.** Work that crosses domain, team, or organizational boundaries must identify interface owners, dependencies, decisions, risks, and review points. A handoff is complete only when the receiving owner can act and the sending owner’s remaining accountability is clear.

#### COL-03.01 — A change affecting shared APIs, data, security…

**Requirement:** A change affecting shared APIs, data, security boundaries, deployment, costs, documentation, customer support, legal terms, or release schedules MUST identify impacted owners and required reviews before the commitment becomes difficult to reverse.

#### COL-03.02 — Cross-team work MUST define inputs, outputs, responsibility…

**Requirement:** Cross-team work MUST define inputs, outputs, responsibility boundaries, response expectations, version/compatibility, failure escalation, and acceptance evidence. Each team MUST know what it provides, consumes, and owns after handoff.

#### COL-03.03 — A dependency MUST have a provider owner, consumer…

**Requirement:** A dependency MUST have a provider owner, consumer owner, expected date/contract, status, failure consequence, and fallback or escalation. Critical-path changes MUST be re-evaluated when assumptions or delivery dates change.

#### COL-03.04 — Reviewers MUST identify blocking correctness, safety, privacy…

**Requirement:** Reviewers MUST identify blocking correctness, safety, privacy, security, legal, compatibility, or operability concerns distinctly from recommendations and style preferences. Authors MUST resolve or explicitly document disposition; reviewers MUST not use personal preference as an undisclosed mandatory standard.

#### COL-03.05 — Participants MUST state the decision, available evidence…

**Requirement:** Participants MUST state the decision, available evidence, requirements, controls, risks, alternatives, and accountable decider. If evidence is insufficient, define a safe experiment or time-bounded discovery task rather than converting an assumption into policy.

#### COL-03.06 — When an owner is unavailable or teams disagree under…

**Requirement:** When an owner is unavailable or teams disagree under a deadline, escalate to the delegated authority and identify what can safely proceed. Do not bypass a required security, privacy, financial, release, or external-commitment approval to preserve schedule.

#### COL-03.07 — The coordinating owner MUST confirm the receiver accepted…

**Requirement:** The coordinating owner MUST confirm the receiver accepted the work, documentation and records are updated, remaining risks and actions have owners, and each team knows whether support ends or continues.

### ORG-01 — Functional Roles Accountability and Decision Rights

*Domain:* [organization](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/README.md) · *Rule:* [01-functional-roles-accountability-and-decision-rights.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/rules/01-functional-roles-accountability-and-decision-rights.md)

**Control objective.** People must know which outcomes they own, what authority they can exercise, who must review a decision, and who accepts residual risk. Clear authority is required for routine work, exceptions, emergencies, external commitments, and succession.

#### ORG-01.01 — The organization MUST maintain a current map of functions…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST maintain a current map of functions that perform product, engineering, security, privacy, legal/compliance coordination, finance/procurement, people operations, release, operations, support, and external communications as applicable. For each material workflow, identify one accountable owner, responsible performer(s), required consulted roles, informed parties, and delegate/escalation path; do not require a dedicated job title for every function.

#### ORG-01.02 — For architecture, security/privacy risk, release, data…

**Requirement:** For architecture, security/privacy risk, release, data use, spending, contracts, customer promises, public claims, policy exceptions, and incident disclosure, define who proposes, reviews, approves, executes, and verifies the decision. Set thresholds using impact, irreversibility, exposure, cost, and affected parties; high-impact decisions MUST receive independent review where practical.

#### ORG-01.03 — A delegation MUST state scope, limits, start/end…

**Requirement:** A delegation MUST state scope, limits, start/end, records required, subdelegation rules, and conditions for escalation. The delegator remains accountable unless the governing policy explicitly transfers accountability; informal availability or seniority MUST NOT be treated as unlimited authority.

#### ORG-01.04 — Where one person can initiate, approve, execute…

**Requirement:** Where one person can initiate, approve, execute, and attest to the same high-impact transaction, the workflow MUST use independent approval, automated safeguards, retrospective review, or another documented compensating control. Apply separation proportionate to actual fraud, error, privacy, security, and release risk.

#### ORG-01.05 — When schedule, cost, quality, security…

**Requirement:** When schedule, cost, quality, security, privacy, accessibility, and customer impact conflict, the decision record MUST state the tradeoff, evidence, affected stakeholders, residual risk, accountable approver, and rejected alternatives. The most senior person’s preference alone is not a substitute for the required decision evidence.

#### ORG-01.06 — Every material responsibility MUST have an escalation route…

**Requirement:** Every material responsibility MUST have an escalation route for blocked decisions, unavailable owners, suspected wrongdoing, unsafe instructions, or urgent user impact. The route MUST include a delegate or coverage procedure; an unresolved escalation MUST not be silently treated as approval.

#### ORG-01.07 — Role changes, departures, new products, new…

**Requirement:** Role changes, departures, new products, new jurisdictions, material incidents, and changed risk MUST trigger review of affected authority, access, approval thresholds, and succession. Remove stale owner names and privileges promptly.

### ORG-04 — Spending Procurement and Organizational Commitments

*Domain:* [organization](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/README.md) · *Rule:* [04-spending-procurement-and-organizational-commitments.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/rules/04-spending-procurement-and-organizational-commitments.md)

**Control objective.** People must not create financial, contractual, data, service, or operational commitments without an authorized sponsor and a review proportionate to lifecycle cost and risk. Procurement and commitments include subscriptions, infrastructure, software, datasets, models, contractors, marketing, public promises, and renewals.

#### ORG-04.01 — The accountable business owner MUST define spending and commitment…

**Requirement:** The accountable business owner MUST define spending and commitment classes, approval limits, budget authority, required finance/legal/security/privacy/procurement review, evidence, and renewal authority. Thresholds MUST consider total lifecycle cost, data access, lock-in, user impact, and exit cost rather than initial price alone.

#### ORG-04.02 — Before a material purchase or external commitment…

**Requirement:** Before a material purchase or external commitment, the sponsor MUST state the business/user need, expected outcome, constraints, alternatives including no purchase, evaluation criteria, full cost, risks, and measurable acceptance conditions. Selection rationale MUST be retained.

#### ORG-04.03 — Before granting a supplier or contractor sensitive information…

**Requirement:** Before granting a supplier or contractor sensitive information, production access, code contribution, model/data access, or operational responsibility, review capability, security/privacy controls, licensing, subcontractors, continuity, incident duties, data return/deletion, and termination rights as applicable.

#### ORG-04.04 — Only designated roles MAY sign agreements, accept terms…

**Requirement:** Only designated roles MAY sign agreements, accept terms, quote prices, promise features or service levels, or represent a roadmap as committed. Review obligations, implied warranties, data rights, support burden, accessibility, export/retention, indemnity, renewal, and termination before acceptance.

#### ORG-04.05 — A service or commitment owner MUST track usage…

**Requirement:** A service or commitment owner MUST track usage, total spend, renewals, budget thresholds, owner, business value, and cost anomalies. Unused or over-provisioned resources MUST be removed or justified; cost-saving actions MUST be checked for security, reliability, and user impact.

#### ORG-04.06 — Material scope, price, data, access…

**Requirement:** Material scope, price, data, access, subprocessor, support, jurisdiction, or contract changes MUST trigger reassessment and approval before taking effect. Renewal decisions MUST compare current value and alternatives and include a tested exit or export path when lock-in risk is material.

#### ORG-04.07 — At contract or project end, revoke access…

**Requirement:** At contract or project end, revoke access, return or delete information, recover assets, stop recurring charges, retain required records, transfer ownership, and verify closure. Record any retained data or continuing duty and its owner.

### PPL-02 — Competence Training Supervision and Delegation

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [02-competence-training-supervision-and-delegation.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/02-competence-training-supervision-and-delegation.md)

**Control objective.** People may perform work only within their competence, authorization, and supervision. Training and qualification must be specific to the decisions and risks a person is expected to handle, and must be refreshed when systems or duties change.

#### PPL-02.01 — For work that can affect security, privacy…

**Requirement:** For work that can affect security, privacy, safety, finances, customer commitments, production availability, model quality, or legal rights, define the knowledge and demonstrated capability required. A credential alone MUST NOT substitute for role-specific competence evidence.

#### PPL-02.02 — Before independent performance of a high-risk task…

**Requirement:** Before independent performance of a high-risk task, the person MUST complete relevant instruction and demonstrate the necessary procedure or judgment. Training MUST cover escalation, failure handling, data protection, tool access, and prohibited actions as applicable.

#### PPL-02.03 — When a person is learning, working outside routine…

**Requirement:** When a person is learning, working outside routine competence, or handling an unusual high-risk task, assign a qualified reviewer/supervisor, define the allowed scope, and require checkpoints before irreversible or externally visible action.

#### PPL-02.04 — Material policy, threat, system, legal…

**Requirement:** Material policy, threat, system, legal, incident, or role changes MUST trigger an assessment of training impact. Retrain or requalify affected roles before they perform newly risky actions independently.

#### PPL-02.05 — A person delegating work MUST confirm the recipient’s authority…

**Requirement:** A person delegating work MUST confirm the recipient’s authority, competence, context, access, expected result, deadline, escalation, and review point. Delegation MUST not transfer an obligation the delegator lacks authority to transfer.

#### PPL-02.06 — Performance feedback MUST use role-relevant expectations and evidence…

**Requirement:** Performance feedback MUST use role-relevant expectations and evidence, provide a path to improve, and distinguish system/process failures from individual actions. Consequential employment decisions MUST follow the approved, jurisdiction-reviewed people policy.

#### PPL-02.07 — Training and qualification evidence MUST record role, subject/version…

**Requirement:** Training and qualification evidence MUST record role, subject/version, completion or assessment result, owner, and refresh trigger. Keep records proportionate and protect them as personnel data.

### PPL-04 — Work Intake Prioritization and Individual Execution

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [04-work-intake-prioritization-and-individual-execution.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/04-work-intake-prioritization-and-individual-execution.md)

**Control objective.** Every work action must have a clear purpose, owner, authorized scope, completion condition, and escalation path. Teams must balance planned work, incidents, maintenance, learning, and stakeholder commitments without relying on hidden queues or personal memory.

#### PPL-04.01 — A request for product, engineering, operations…

**Requirement:** A request for product, engineering, operations, support, research, compliance, or administrative work MUST be captured in an approved system with requester, purpose, affected users, urgency, expected outcome, owner, and relevant evidence. Emergency actions MAY start immediately but MUST be recorded promptly afterward.

#### PPL-04.02 — A responsible owner MUST compare user harm/value, security/legal…

**Requirement:** A responsible owner MUST compare user harm/value, security/legal obligations, service impact, dependencies, urgency, effort, opportunity cost, and reversibility. Record deferral rationale for material risks or commitments; do not let loudness, status, or recency alone determine priority.

#### PPL-04.03 — Accepted work MUST state outcomes, boundaries, dependencies…

**Requirement:** Accepted work MUST state outcomes, boundaries, dependencies, decision owner, review needs, validation, and closure evidence proportionate to risk. For software feature work, follow the specification → technical requirements → plan → implementation sequence required by project rules; use a proportionate documented path for routine maintenance.

#### PPL-04.04 — Team leads SHOULD make active work and blocked work…

**Requirement:** Team leads SHOULD make active work and blocked work visible, limit avoidable concurrent commitments, and identify the decision needed to unblock progress. Do not claim capacity for work without accounting for on-call, review, support, security, and maintenance duties.

#### PPL-04.05 — The owner MUST update status when scope, estimate…

**Requirement:** The owner MUST update status when scope, estimate, dependency, risk, or expected delivery changes. A blocked or unsafe task MUST be escalated with the reason, impact, next action, and decision needed; silence MUST NOT be interpreted as acceptance.

#### PPL-04.06 — Completion MUST be checked against acceptance evidence by…

**Requirement:** Completion MUST be checked against acceptance evidence by the owner or an appropriate reviewer. Work that affects production, user data, public commitments, money, security, or irreversible state MUST receive independent review/approval where practical.

#### PPL-04.07 — When work is complete, cancelled, superseded…

**Requirement:** When work is complete, cancelled, superseded, or transferred, update status, artifacts, owners, follow-up tasks, stakeholders, and any residual risk. Unfinished work MUST not remain hidden in personal notes, private messages, or unowned branches.

### PPL-06 — Recruitment Selection and Hiring Decisions

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [06-recruitment-selection-and-hiring-decisions.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/06-recruitment-selection-and-hiring-decisions.md)

**Control objective.** Recruitment and selection decisions must be job-related, fair, authorized, privacy-preserving, and supported by consistent evidence. The hiring process must identify the capability needed and select for that capability without promising terms or collecting information beyond lawful business need.

#### PPL-06.01 — Before recruitment begins, an accountable owner MUST approve…

**Requirement:** Before recruitment begins, an accountable owner MUST approve the role purpose, outcomes, essential competencies, reporting/delegation boundary, budget, employment or contractor form, and decision authority. Do not recruit for an undefined title or imply a role grants unapproved product, spending, or access authority.

#### PPL-06.02 — Selection criteria and interview/assessment methods MUST relate to actual…

**Requirement:** Selection criteria and interview/assessment methods MUST relate to actual role outcomes and be consistently applied to candidates. Define essential versus trainable capabilities, accessible participation, scoring or evidence method, and decision threshold before evaluating applicants.

#### PPL-06.03 — Interviewers MUST disclose personal, financial, or other…

**Requirement:** Interviewers MUST disclose personal, financial, or other conflicts and recuse or use independent review where appropriate. Hiring decisions MUST be based on documented job-related evidence, not protected characteristics, affinity, prestige, personal similarity, or undisclosed criteria, subject to applicable law.

#### PPL-06.04 — Collect only candidate information needed for the approved process…

**Requirement:** Collect only candidate information needed for the approved process; provide required notices and consent where applicable; restrict access; secure interview notes and assessments; set retention/deletion triggers; and use approved systems. Sensitive screening MUST be lawful, proportionate, and performed by authorized roles.

#### PPL-06.05 — Reference checks, background checks, work samples…

**Requirement:** Reference checks, background checks, work samples, psychometric tests, and automated ranking MUST have an authorized purpose, valid criteria, proportionality, transparency where required, appropriate human review, and a challenge/correction path. An automated score MUST NOT be the sole basis for a consequential decision without an approved lawful process.

#### PPL-06.06 — Only an authorized role MAY make or change…

**Requirement:** Only an authorized role MAY make or change an offer, compensation term, work location, start date, confidentiality/IP term, or contractual commitment. Candidate communications MUST be timely, accurate, respectful, accessible, and consistent with approved terms.

#### PPL-06.07 — The owner MUST record the selection rationale and approvals…

**Requirement:** The owner MUST record the selection rationale and approvals in protected records, notify candidates appropriately, dispose of data under retention rules, and hand the approved role, duties, conditions, training, and access requirements to the onboarding owner.

### EXT-03 — Public Statements Product Claims and Marketing Content

*Domain:* [stakeholder-relations](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/README.md) · *Rule:* [03-public-statements-product-claims-and-marketing-content.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/rules/03-public-statements-product-claims-and-marketing-content.md)

**Control objective.** External-facing claims shape user decisions and organizational obligations. Product, security, privacy, licensing, performance, accessibility, model, and commercial statements must be accurate, scoped, evidenced, approved, and corrected when they become stale.

#### EXT-03.01 — Each public channel and content type MUST have…

**Requirement:** Each public channel and content type MUST have an owner, authorized publisher, subject-matter reviewers, approval path, and correction process. Material claims involving security, privacy, legal rights, license, availability, support, or commercial terms MUST receive relevant specialist review.

#### EXT-03.02 — A claim MUST identify the product/profile/version, tested conditions…

**Requirement:** A claim MUST identify the product/profile/version, tested conditions, limitations, date/evidence, and source owner. Performance, accuracy, hardware support, model quality, security, accessibility, privacy, and reliability claims MUST match reproducible evidence and actual supported behavior.

#### EXT-03.03 — Reviewers MUST assess likely user interpretation, omitted conditions…

**Requirement:** Reviewers MUST assess likely user interpretation, omitted conditions, comparison baseline, target audience, and whether a prototype or aspiration could be mistaken for a shipped guarantee. Disclaimers MUST not contradict a headline or obscure a material limitation.

#### EXT-03.04 — Use of customer names, testimonials, partner marks…

**Requirement:** Use of customer names, testimonials, partner marks, certification symbols, standards references, model/provider names, or endorsements MUST have permission, accurate context, rights review, and current verification. Never imply certification or independent validation that has not occurred.

#### EXT-03.05 — Pricing, licensing, warranty, support level…

**Requirement:** Pricing, licensing, warranty, support level, privacy notice, data use, service level, roadmap, and legal terms MUST come from an approved source and authorized owner. Content editors MUST not improvise an enforceable commitment.

#### EXT-03.06 — Assign each material page, release note, demo…

**Requirement:** Assign each material page, release note, demo, social post, and sales artifact an owner and review trigger. Update or remove stale claims when the product, model, license, vulnerability status, supported profile, or commitment changes.

#### EXT-03.07 — When a published statement is materially false or misleading…

**Requirement:** When a published statement is materially false or misleading, notify the accountable owner, assess affected users and obligations, correct the source and downstream copies, and communicate the correction through a proportionate channel. Preserve evidence of what was changed and when.

#### EXT-03.08 — Before publishing, verify rights and approvals for code…

**Requirement:** Before publishing, verify rights and approvals for code, imagery, user stories, screenshots, telemetry, support cases, employee identities, and customer data. Redact secrets and identifying data; use explicit consent and approved retention for testimonials or case studies.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

The following conditions are explicitly prohibited by the mapped controls. Any one of them is sufficient to fail the requirement.

- [COL-03] Do not assume a shared repository, channel, or manager means shared accountability.
- [COL-03] Do not merge unresolved blocking review concerns into a vague “follow up later” without risk approval.
- [COL-03] Do not create hidden inter-team dependencies through undocumented data formats or privileged access.
- [ORG-01] Do not infer authority from job title, tenure, chat participation, or access to a tool.
- [ORG-01] Do not allow a person to approve their own material exception without a documented independent compensating review.
