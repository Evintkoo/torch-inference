# ORG-011 — Join, role-change, leave, and separation workflows shall update responsibilities, access, credentials, equipment, records, and knowledge handoff

**Requirement ID:** ORG-011  
**Priority:** MUST  
**Source:** [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md) — Workforce lifecycle and capability  
**Status:** Active requirement; no implementation or evaluation evidence is recorded in this repository.  
**Rule controls:** OPS-06, PPL-01, PPL-05, SEC-12, SEC-13  
**Acceptance checks:** None recorded  
**Open decisions:** None recorded

## 1. Normative requirement

Join, role-change, leave, and separation workflows shall update responsibilities, access, credentials, equipment, records, and knowledge handoff.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

This requirement is part of **Workforce lifecycle and capability** in [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md). It is traced to 5 rule control(s): OPS-06, PPL-01, PPL-05, SEC-12, SEC-13. Those controls state the following purpose:

- **OPS-06 — Operational Roles Coverage Handover and Human Continuity:** Operational resilience depends on people knowing who is on duty, what they may do, how to hand work over, and how the service remains safe when a key person or supplier is unavailable. Coverage must match the service commitment without inventing unsupported staffing promises.
- **PPL-01 — Joining Role Change Leave and Separation:** Every person’s organizational relationship and duties must move through controlled entry, change, absence, return, and exit processes. Workforce workflows coordinate identity, authority, access, equipment, knowledge, privacy, and continuity without assuming one employment model or jurisdiction.
- **PPL-05 — Work Time Availability Leave and Expense Integrity:** People must follow approved work-time, availability, leave, expense, and resource-use policies with accurate records and safe escalation. These controls require a policy owner and lawful, transparent procedures; they do not define a universal schedule, compensation model, or employment arrangement.
- **SEC-12 — Personnel Security and Human Access Lifecycle:** Human and agent-operated identities are security principals. Identity proofing, role authorization, training, privileged activity, transfer, emergency access, and separation must follow a traceable lifecycle tied to job function and risk.
- **SEC-13 — Physical Workspace Remote Work and Asset Protection:** People must protect devices, conversations, records, and physical access wherever work occurs. Controls must match the sensitivity of information and actual threat, remain usable for the workforce, and respect jurisdictional privacy and workplace rules.

Acceptance is not satisfied by citing these controls. The implementation must demonstrate the observable result in section 6, and the mapped rule clauses in section 5 define what must hold at the enforcement boundary.

## 3. Priority and interpretation

This requirement has **MUST** priority under the repository's requirement conventions. A MUST requirement is needed for the stated product direction or a safe release. A SHOULD requirement is expected unless a documented tradeoff justifies deferral. A COULD requirement is a candidate capability that requires product or technical validation. Acceptance describes observable evidence; it does not mandate a specific implementation unless the requirement says so.

### OPS-06 — Operational Roles Coverage Handover and Human Continuity

*Domain:* [operations](../../../torviello-enforcer/operations/README.md) · *Rule:* [06-operational-roles-coverage-handover-and-human-continuity.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/operations/rules/06-operational-roles-coverage-handover-and-human-continuity.md)

**Control objective.** Operational resilience depends on people knowing who is on duty, what they may do, how to hand work over, and how the service remains safe when a key person or supplier is unavailable. Coverage must match the service commitment without inventing unsupported staffing promises.

#### OPS-06.01 — Each production profile MUST identify service owner, incident…

**Requirement:** Each production profile MUST identify service owner, incident lead, deploy/release authority, security escalation, data/persistence owner, customer communication owner, and backup/delegate as applicable. State whether each function is staffed continuously, during defined hours, or on best-effort basis.

#### OPS-06.02 — For supported operational channels, state hours, severity…

**Requirement:** For supported operational channels, state hours, severity classes, response targets, escalation contacts, and fallback when the primary owner is unreachable. Public service promises MUST match actual staffing, monitoring, and recovery capability.

#### OPS-06.03 — An operational handover MUST include current service health…

**Requirement:** An operational handover MUST include current service health, open incidents/changes, customer impact, risks, pending actions, access used, deadlines, and next decision. The receiving owner MUST acknowledge material risk and understand their authority.

#### OPS-06.04 — Coverage MUST use named identities and pre-authorized delegates…

**Requirement:** Coverage MUST use named identities and pre-authorized delegates with time-bounded access. Never share accounts or keys to cover an absent operator; emergency access uses the security break-glass process and post-use review.

#### OPS-06.05 — For critical operations, identify single-person and supplier dependencies…

**Requirement:** For critical operations, identify single-person and supplier dependencies, recovery documentation, alternate contacts, and minimum competent coverage. Exercise absence, simultaneous incident, and unavailability of a key vendor at a frequency proportional to impact.

#### OPS-06.06 — Operational processes MUST allow responders to escalate unsafe workload…

**Requirement:** Operational processes MUST allow responders to escalate unsafe workload, conflicting incident tasks, or insufficient authority. Rotate or hand off duties as needed under approved work policies; do not use undocumented availability expectations as a service guarantee.

#### OPS-06.07 — Changes to service hours, product scope, team…

**Requirement:** Changes to service hours, product scope, team structure, vendor, incident severity, or recovery process MUST trigger review of roles, contacts, runbooks, training, and support commitments.

### PPL-01 — Joining Role Change Leave and Separation

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [01-joining-role-change-leave-and-separation.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/01-joining-role-change-leave-and-separation.md)

**Control objective.** Every person’s organizational relationship and duties must move through controlled entry, change, absence, return, and exit processes. Workforce workflows coordinate identity, authority, access, equipment, knowledge, privacy, and continuity without assuming one employment model or jurisdiction.

#### PPL-01.01 — Before an employee, contractor, intern, advisor…

**Requirement:** Before an employee, contractor, intern, advisor, agent operator, or other person begins work, an accountable sponsor MUST define relationship, scope, duration, supervisor, permitted systems/data, confidentiality/IP terms, required screening lawful for the jurisdiction, and end/renewal trigger.

#### PPL-01.02 — Before production, source, model, customer-data…

**Requirement:** Before production, source, model, customer-data, administrative, finance, or public-communication access, verify identity through approved means, accept applicable policy/agreements, complete required role training, assign a sponsor, and grant only the approved role set. Exceptions require an owner and expiration.

#### PPL-01.03 — A role, team, contract, or project…

**Requirement:** A role, team, contract, or project change MUST trigger review of current entitlements, data access, approvals, delegated authority, equipment, training, and outstanding work. Remove access and authority no longer needed; do not wait for the next periodic review when the change is material.

#### PPL-01.04 — For planned or unexpected absence, the manager…

**Requirement:** For planned or unexpected absence, the manager or delegate MUST identify critical duties, authority suspension or delegation, work queues, customer/incident coverage, and return/reassignment steps. Do not share credentials to provide coverage.

#### PPL-01.05 — At separation or contract end, a responsible owner…

**Requirement:** At separation or contract end, a responsible owner MUST promptly disable identities, sessions, tokens, keys, devices, recovery methods, admin roles, and external accounts; recover assets; transfer owned work and records; and remove public/financial authority. Timing MUST reflect risk and legal constraints.

#### PPL-01.06 — Personnel records, screening results, performance, health…

**Requirement:** Personnel records, screening results, performance, health, and investigation information MUST be limited to an authorized purpose, least-privilege access, approved retention, and applicable law. Security controls MUST NOT become a pretext for collecting unrelated personal information.

#### PPL-01.07 — The sponsor or delegate MUST confirm access closure…

**Requirement:** The sponsor or delegate MUST confirm access closure, asset return, knowledge handoff, outstanding commitments, legal holds, and record ownership. Any delayed removal MUST name a risk owner, compensating control, and deadline; overdue items escalate.

### PPL-05 — Work Time Availability Leave and Expense Integrity

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [05-work-time-availability-leave-and-expense-integrity.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/05-work-time-availability-leave-and-expense-integrity.md)

**Control objective.** People must follow approved work-time, availability, leave, expense, and resource-use policies with accurate records and safe escalation. These controls require a policy owner and lawful, transparent procedures; they do not define a universal schedule, compensation model, or employment arrangement.

#### PPL-05.01 — Each role MUST have documented expectations for availability…

**Requirement:** Each role MUST have documented expectations for availability, on-call duty, response windows, work location, time recording if required, and escalation. Expectations MUST be consistent with the applicable agreement, service promise, and jurisdiction-specific policy; informal messages MUST NOT create hidden permanent obligations.

#### PPL-05.02 — Where time or activity records are required…

**Requirement:** Where time or activity records are required, the person MUST report actual work, leave, on-call, or expense information accurately and promptly. Managers MUST correct process or workload issues rather than pressure people to falsify records or work off the record.

#### PPL-05.03 — Leave and absence MUST use the approved confidential process…

**Requirement:** Leave and absence MUST use the approved confidential process, identify only information required by policy, and trigger appropriate duty coverage and access/delegation review. Managers MUST not disclose sensitive leave information to coworkers beyond operational need.

#### PPL-05.04 — A person requesting reimbursement or using organizational funds MUST…

**Requirement:** A person requesting reimbursement or using organizational funds MUST state business purpose, amount, date, recipient, and evidence required by policy, disclose conflicts, and use approved categories. Approvers MUST verify legitimacy, policy, budget, and separation-of-duties controls.

#### PPL-05.05 — Cards, accounts, credits, cloud budgets…

**Requirement:** Cards, accounts, credits, cloud budgets, travel booking, and subscriptions MUST be assigned to an authorized person, purpose, limit, and reconciliation owner. Lost instruments, suspicious charges, duplicate claims, and out-of-policy purchases MUST be reported promptly.

#### PPL-05.06 — People MUST be able to report fatigue, excessive…

**Requirement:** People MUST be able to report fatigue, excessive workload, unsafe work hours, conflicting on-call assignments, or inability to meet response commitments without retaliation. The owner MUST rebalance work, change coverage, or reduce the service commitment rather than conceal the risk.

#### PPL-05.07 — Time, leave, payroll, reimbursement…

**Requirement:** Time, leave, payroll, reimbursement, and expense evidence MUST be access-controlled and retained only according to approved legal, financial, employment, and audit requirements. Product or engineering repositories MUST NOT become shadow HR or finance databases.

### SEC-12 — Personnel Security and Human Access Lifecycle

*Domain:* [security](../../../torviello-enforcer/security/README.md) · *Rule:* [12-personnel-security-and-human-access-lifecycle.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/security/rules/12-personnel-security-and-human-access-lifecycle.md)

**Control objective.** Human and agent-operated identities are security principals. Identity proofing, role authorization, training, privileged activity, transfer, emergency access, and separation must follow a traceable lifecycle tied to job function and risk.

#### SEC-12.01 — Every person and human-operated agent identity with access…

**Requirement:** Every person and human-operated agent identity with access to organizational systems MUST use an individually attributable account or a controlled service identity with an accountable human owner. Shared accounts are prohibited except for a documented technical necessity with compensating attribution and rotation.

#### SEC-12.02 — Access MUST be based on a current role/task need…

**Requirement:** Access MUST be based on a current role/task need, approved by the resource owner, scoped to least privilege, and time-bounded where practical. Authentication strength and screening MUST reflect data sensitivity, privilege, exposure, and applicable law.

#### SEC-12.03 — Administrative roles MUST be distinct from everyday accounts where…

**Requirement:** Administrative roles MUST be distinct from everyday accounts where supported, protected by strong authentication, and used only for authorized administrative tasks. High-impact actions MUST be logged and independently approved or reviewed according to risk.

#### SEC-12.04 — Access MUST be reviewed at role change, contract…

**Requirement:** Access MUST be reviewed at role change, contract end, incident, privilege elevation, system ownership change, and periodic intervals based on risk. Remove dormant, excessive, orphaned, and conflicting access; record reviewer, scope, decision, and closure.

#### SEC-12.05 — Break-glass access MUST have an incident or change reference…

**Requirement:** Break-glass access MUST have an incident or change reference, narrow scope, strong authentication, alerting, expiry, and post-use independent review. Temporary grants MUST expire automatically or have an owner and tested revocation deadline.

#### SEC-12.06 — At separation or loss of authorization, disable accounts…

**Requirement:** At separation or loss of authorization, disable accounts, keys, tokens, active sessions, recovery methods, device certificates, signing credentials, and external access within risk-based deadlines. Confirm revocation across federated and supplier systems.

#### SEC-12.07 — Passwords, recovery codes, private keys…

**Requirement:** Passwords, recovery codes, private keys, and tokens MUST use approved secret-management and delivery paths. Never send credentials through ordinary chat/email or retain them in handoff, support, or personnel records.

#### SEC-12.08 — Identity and privileged activity records MUST allow attribution…

**Requirement:** Identity and privileged activity records MUST allow attribution, preserve integrity, alert on high-risk anomalies, and avoid recording authentication secrets. Investigate unexplained access or failed offboarding through the security incident process.

### SEC-13 — Physical Workspace Remote Work and Asset Protection

*Domain:* [security](../../../torviello-enforcer/security/README.md) · *Rule:* [13-physical-workspace-remote-work-and-asset-protection.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/security/rules/13-physical-workspace-remote-work-and-asset-protection.md)

**Control objective.** People must protect devices, conversations, records, and physical access wherever work occurs. Controls must match the sensitivity of information and actual threat, remain usable for the workforce, and respect jurisdictional privacy and workplace rules.

#### SEC-13.01 — The organization MUST define requirements for office, home…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST define requirements for office, home, travel, coworking, and other work locations based on information sensitivity, device type, local law, and customer/contract conditions. Restrictions or exceptions MUST be stated, approved, and supported with practical alternatives.

#### SEC-13.02 — Work devices MUST use approved authentication, encryption…

**Requirement:** Work devices MUST use approved authentication, encryption, screen lock, supported security updates, endpoint protection where available, and remote lock/wipe capability proportionate to risk. Removable media MUST be restricted, encrypted, inventoried, and sanitized before reuse or disposal.

#### SEC-13.03 — Workers MUST position screens, control printing, secure…

**Requirement:** Workers MUST position screens, control printing, secure paper records, and avoid discussing confidential work where unauthorized people can see or hear it. Use privacy filters, locked storage, secure disposal, or private rooms where sensitivity warrants.

#### SEC-13.04 — Work locations that host sensitive systems, records…

**Requirement:** Work locations that host sensitive systems, records, or equipment MUST identify authorized access, visitor escort/record requirements, key/card custody, alarm response, and lost credential escalation. Do not leave visitors unattended in restricted areas.

#### SEC-13.05 — When working while traveling, protect devices from theft…

**Requirement:** When working while traveling, protect devices from theft, shoulder surfing, tampering, and unattended access; use approved network protections and avoid sensitive work where privacy cannot be assured. Report loss, suspected tampering, border seizure, or exposure promptly.

#### SEC-13.06 — Loss, theft, damage, unexpected access…

**Requirement:** Loss, theft, damage, unexpected access, fire, flood, power failure, or environmental hazard affecting work assets MUST be reported through a defined route. The owner MUST assess credential revocation, data exposure, business continuity, recovery, and notification needs.

#### SEC-13.07 — Before transfer, return, resale, or disposal…

**Requirement:** Before transfer, return, resale, or disposal, remove organizational accounts and securely erase storage under an approved method; recover licenses and accessories; verify disposal for sensitive media; and record custody transfer where required.

#### SEC-13.08 — People MUST receive concise guidance for the locations…

**Requirement:** People MUST receive concise guidance for the locations and devices they use, including reporting lost assets, safe travel, remote meetings, printing, and household/shared-device boundaries. Training MUST NOT rely on surveillance of personal spaces.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

The following conditions are explicitly prohibited by the mapped controls. Any one of them is sufficient to fail the requirement.

- [OPS-06] Do not promise continuous support based on monitoring alone.
- [OPS-06] Do not make one person the only holder of critical recovery knowledge or credentials.
- [OPS-06] Do not leave an incident active without a named lead and next update/decision owner.
- [PPL-01] Do not grant broad access because a person is trusted or because role setup is inconvenient.
- [PPL-01] Do not share or transfer a departing person’s credentials to preserve continuity.
- [PPL-01] Do not collect or retain sensitive personnel information without a defined lawful purpose and access owner.
- [PPL-05] Do not submit knowingly false time, expense, leave, or reimbursement records.
- [PPL-05] Do not use personal payment or accounts to evade approval, procurement, tax, or security controls.
- [PPL-05] Do not disclose private employment or financial details to establish routine operational coverage.
- [SEC-12] Do not grant access based only on a manager’s informal message when the resource owner’s approval is required.
- [SEC-12] Do not leave an identity active because its owner is temporarily absent or the access inventory is unclear.
- [SEC-12] Do not bypass access review by using a contractor, personal account, or machine credential without an accountable owner.
- [SEC-13] Do not store sensitive work on unmanaged personal devices or personal cloud accounts without an approved exception.
- [SEC-13] Do not expose customer data, credentials, unreleased product information, or confidential conversations in public spaces.
- [SEC-13] Do not install intrusive monitoring in personal workspaces without lawful authority, necessity, transparency, and privacy review.
