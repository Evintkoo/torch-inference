# Agents: requirements/items

Scope: one document per requirement ID.

## Instructions

- Keep one document per stable requirement ID; do not merge or renumber IDs.
- Keep the normative statement and acceptance criteria identical to the authoritative source table.
- Keep rule-control and acceptance-check mappings current; every requirement must map to at least one rule control or record an approved unmapped decision.
- Do not weaken a requirement or its acceptance criteria to make a change pass; that requires explicit human authorization.
