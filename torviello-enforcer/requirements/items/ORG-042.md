# ORG-042 — Material incidents, complaints, audit findings, near misses, and repeated workflow failures shall result in owned corrective action and effectiveness review

**Requirement ID:** ORG-042  
**Priority:** MUST  
**Source:** [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md) — Governance, compliance, and improvement  
**Status:** Active requirement; no implementation or evaluation evidence is recorded in this repository.  
**Rule controls:** COL-03, OPS-06, ORG-03, PPL-03, PPL-07, EXT-02, EXT-04  
**Acceptance checks:** None recorded  
**Open decisions:** None recorded

## 1. Normative requirement

Material incidents, complaints, audit findings, near misses, and repeated workflow failures shall result in owned corrective action and effectiveness review.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

This requirement is part of **Governance, compliance, and improvement** in [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md). It is traced to 7 rule control(s): COL-03, OPS-06, ORG-03, PPL-03, PPL-07, EXT-02, EXT-04. Those controls state the following purpose:

- **COL-03 — Cross-Team Coordination Review and Escalation:** Work that crosses domain, team, or organizational boundaries must identify interface owners, dependencies, decisions, risks, and review points. A handoff is complete only when the receiving owner can act and the sending owner’s remaining accountability is clear.
- **OPS-06 — Operational Roles Coverage Handover and Human Continuity:** Operational resilience depends on people knowing who is on duty, what they may do, how to hand work over, and how the service remains safe when a key person or supplier is unavailable. Coverage must match the service commitment without inventing unsupported staffing promises.
- **ORG-03 — Compliance Risk and Obligation Management:** Organizational decisions must account for applicable law, contract, policy, security, safety, privacy, and product risk. Owners must identify obligations and uncertainty early, seek qualified advice where needed, and turn findings into controlled, verifiable actions.
- **PPL-03 — Respectful Accessible and Safe Work:** Work practices must protect people’s safety, dignity, accessibility, and ability to raise concerns. The organization must maintain qualified, jurisdiction-aware policies and fair response processes instead of relying on informal norms or individual goodwill.
- **PPL-07 — Performance Development Recognition and Corrective Action:** People and teams need clear expectations, useful feedback, development support, and fair accountability. Performance processes must improve outcomes and capability without reducing complex work to a single metric or bypassing approved employment policies.
- **EXT-02 — Feedback Complaints Accessibility and Dispute Handling:** Complaints and feedback are controlled signals about unmet expectations, harm, defects, accessibility barriers, and trust. The process must be easy to find, impartial, timely, reviewable, and linked to improvement while respecting customer rights and privacy.
- **EXT-04 — External Incident and Stakeholder Communications:** External incident communications must be coordinated, truthful, timely, privacy-preserving, and consistent with legal, contractual, and user-safety obligations. Technical investigation, customer service, legal review, and public communication must have distinct responsibilities that coordinate under one incident authority.

Acceptance is not satisfied by citing these controls. The implementation must demonstrate the observable result in section 6, and the mapped rule clauses in section 5 define what must hold at the enforcement boundary.

This requirement has **MUST** priority under the repository's requirement conventions. A MUST requirement is needed for the stated product direction or a safe release. ## 5. Detailed obligations

Each subsection below is the complete normative content of a mapped control. A requirement is not satisfied by selecting one convenient clause; every applicable clause must hold, including its negative and boundary behavior.

### COL-03 — Cross-Team Coordination Review and Escalation

*Domain:* [collaboration-and-communication](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/README.md) · *Rule:* [03-cross-team-coordination-review-and-escalation.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/rules/03-cross-team-coordination-review-and-escalation.md)

**Control objective.** Work that crosses domain, team, or organizational boundaries must identify interface owners, dependencies, decisions, risks, and review points. A handoff is complete only when the receiving owner can act and the sending owner’s remaining accountability is clear.

#### COL-03.01 — A change affecting shared APIs, data, security…

**Requirement:** A change affecting shared APIs, data, security boundaries, deployment, costs, documentation, customer support, legal terms, or release schedules MUST identify impacted owners and required reviews before the commitment becomes difficult to reverse.

#### COL-03.02 — Cross-team work MUST define inputs, outputs, responsibility…

**Requirement:** Cross-team work MUST define inputs, outputs, responsibility boundaries, response expectations, version/compatibility, failure escalation, and acceptance evidence. Each team MUST know what it provides, consumes, and owns after handoff.

#### COL-03.03 — A dependency MUST have a provider owner, consumer…

**Requirement:** A dependency MUST have a provider owner, consumer owner, expected date/contract, status, failure consequence, and fallback or escalation. Critical-path changes MUST be re-evaluated when assumptions or delivery dates change.

#### COL-03.04 — Reviewers MUST identify blocking correctness, safety, privacy…

**Requirement:** Reviewers MUST identify blocking correctness, safety, privacy, security, legal, compatibility, or operability concerns distinctly from recommendations and style preferences. Authors MUST resolve or explicitly document disposition; reviewers MUST not use personal preference as an undisclosed mandatory standard.

#### COL-03.05 — Participants MUST state the decision, available evidence…

**Requirement:** Participants MUST state the decision, available evidence, requirements, controls, risks, alternatives, and accountable decider. If evidence is insufficient, define a safe experiment or time-bounded discovery task rather than converting an assumption into policy.

#### COL-03.06 — When an owner is unavailable or teams disagree under…

**Requirement:** When an owner is unavailable or teams disagree under a deadline, escalate to the delegated authority and identify what can safely proceed. Do not bypass a required security, privacy, financial, release, or external-commitment approval to preserve schedule.

#### COL-03.07 — The coordinating owner MUST confirm the receiver accepted…

**Requirement:** The coordinating owner MUST confirm the receiver accepted the work, documentation and records are updated, remaining risks and actions have owners, and each team knows whether support ends or continues.

### OPS-06 — Operational Roles Coverage Handover and Human Continuity

*Domain:* [operations](../../../torviello-enforcer/operations/README.md) · *Rule:* [06-operational-roles-coverage-handover-and-human-continuity.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/operations/rules/06-operational-roles-coverage-handover-and-human-continuity.md)

**Control objective.** Operational resilience depends on people knowing who is on duty, what they may do, how to hand work over, and how the service remains safe when a key person or supplier is unavailable. Coverage must match the service commitment without inventing unsupported staffing promises.

#### OPS-06.01 — Each production profile MUST identify service owner, incident…

**Requirement:** Each production profile MUST identify service owner, incident lead, deploy/release authority, security escalation, data/persistence owner, customer communication owner, and backup/delegate as applicable. State whether each function is staffed continuously, during defined hours, or on best-effort basis.

#### OPS-06.02 — For supported operational channels, state hours, severity…

**Requirement:** For supported operational channels, state hours, severity classes, response targets, escalation contacts, and fallback when the primary owner is unreachable. Public service promises MUST match actual staffing, monitoring, and recovery capability.

#### OPS-06.03 — An operational handover MUST include current service health…

**Requirement:** An operational handover MUST include current service health, open incidents/changes, customer impact, risks, pending actions, access used, deadlines, and next decision. The receiving owner MUST acknowledge material risk and understand their authority.

#### OPS-06.04 — Coverage MUST use named identities and pre-authorized delegates…

**Requirement:** Coverage MUST use named identities and pre-authorized delegates with time-bounded access. Never share accounts or keys to cover an absent operator; emergency access uses the security break-glass process and post-use review.

#### OPS-06.05 — For critical operations, identify single-person and supplier dependencies…

**Requirement:** For critical operations, identify single-person and supplier dependencies, recovery documentation, alternate contacts, and minimum competent coverage. Exercise absence, simultaneous incident, and unavailability of a key vendor at a frequency proportional to impact.

#### OPS-06.06 — Operational processes MUST allow responders to escalate unsafe workload…

**Requirement:** Operational processes MUST allow responders to escalate unsafe workload, conflicting incident tasks, or insufficient authority. Rotate or hand off duties as needed under approved work policies; do not use undocumented availability expectations as a service guarantee.

#### OPS-06.07 — Changes to service hours, product scope, team…

**Requirement:** Changes to service hours, product scope, team structure, vendor, incident severity, or recovery process MUST trigger review of roles, contacts, runbooks, training, and support commitments.

### ORG-03 — Compliance Risk and Obligation Management

*Domain:* [organization](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/README.md) · *Rule:* [03-compliance-risk-and-obligation-management.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/rules/03-compliance-risk-and-obligation-management.md)

**Control objective.** Organizational decisions must account for applicable law, contract, policy, security, safety, privacy, and product risk. Owners must identify obligations and uncertainty early, seek qualified advice where needed, and turn findings into controlled, verifiable actions.

#### ORG-03.01 — For each product/service activity and operating jurisdiction…

**Requirement:** For each product/service activity and operating jurisdiction, an assigned owner MUST identify relevant legal, regulatory, contractual, license, privacy, accessibility, security, safety, and customer obligations. Record source, scope, applicability rationale, control owner, evidence, review trigger, and qualified adviser where interpretation is uncertain.

#### ORG-03.02 — Employees and agents MUST NOT make authoritative legal conclusions…

**Requirement:** Employees and agents MUST NOT make authoritative legal conclusions from a general rule, internet summary, or model output. When a decision affects employment, privacy, data residency, export controls, consumer rights, intellectual property, licensing, breach notification, or regulated use, route it to qualified counsel or an authorized specialist before commitment.

#### ORG-03.03 — A risk review MUST identify asset/person/stakeholder, threat…

**Requirement:** A risk review MUST identify asset/person/stakeholder, threat or failure scenario, likelihood and impact, exposure, existing safeguards, residual risk, treatment owner, approval authority, and review trigger. The depth of review MUST reflect harm, irreversibility, scale, and uncertainty.

#### ORG-03.04 — For each material risk, the owner MUST choose…

**Requirement:** For each material risk, the owner MUST choose avoid, reduce, transfer, or accept; state why, due date, interim safeguards, monitoring, and evidence. Risk acceptance MUST be explicit, time-bounded or trigger-bounded, and authorized by the owner accountable for the affected impact.

#### ORG-03.05 — A material change in jurisdiction, data use…

**Requirement:** A material change in jurisdiction, data use, product purpose, supplier, public claim, contract, deployment model, or user population MUST trigger an obligation and risk impact review before the change is made effective.

#### ORG-03.06 — A material incident, complaint, audit finding…

**Requirement:** A material incident, complaint, audit finding, near miss, or repeated exception MUST be analyzed for process, authority, competence, incentive, tooling, and control causes. Corrective actions MUST identify owners, dates, control updates, effectiveness checks, and residual risks.

#### ORG-03.07 — Compliance, risk, investigation, and corrective-action records…

**Requirement:** Compliance, risk, investigation, and corrective-action records MUST have restricted access, retention and deletion rules, integrity protection proportionate to their importance, and a legal-hold process. Personal data in those records MUST be minimized and handled under the approved privacy policy.

### PPL-03 — Respectful Accessible and Safe Work

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [03-respectful-accessible-and-safe-work.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/03-respectful-accessible-and-safe-work.md)

**Control objective.** Work practices must protect people’s safety, dignity, accessibility, and ability to raise concerns. The organization must maintain qualified, jurisdiction-aware policies and fair response processes instead of relying on informal norms or individual goodwill.

#### PPL-03.01 — A designated owner MUST maintain current, accessible policies…

**Requirement:** A designated owner MUST maintain current, accessible policies for respectful conduct, discrimination/harassment prevention, accessibility accommodations, workplace health/safety relevant to the work, acceptable technology use, and non-retaliation. Policy scope, reporting routes, responsible handlers, and applicable jurisdictions MUST be stated.

#### PPL-03.02 — People MUST have an accessible reporting route for unsafe…

**Requirement:** People MUST have an accessible reporting route for unsafe, discriminatory, harassing, retaliatory, or coercive behavior and an alternative when the normal manager or handler is implicated. Emergency or immediate physical danger MUST have a direct urgent response route.

#### PPL-03.03 — Reports MUST be acknowledged, risk-triaged, assigned…

**Requirement:** Reports MUST be acknowledged, risk-triaged, assigned to a trained impartial handler, and handled with access limited to need-to-know. The process MUST state confidentiality limits, evidence preservation, protective measures, fair response, escalation, and closure.

#### PPL-03.04 — The organization MUST prohibit retaliation for good-faith reporting…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST prohibit retaliation for good-faith reporting, requesting accommodation, participating in an investigation, or raising a safety/quality concern. Monitor for adverse treatment after reports and provide a separate escalation path.

#### PPL-03.05 — Work tools, documentation, meetings, training…

**Requirement:** Work tools, documentation, meetings, training, and reporting channels SHOULD be usable by people with differing access needs. When an accommodation or accessibility barrier is raised, assign an owner, assess confidentiality and safety, and provide an effective alternative while resolving the barrier.

#### PPL-03.06 — Investigations and performance decisions MUST distinguish observed facts…

**Requirement:** Investigations and performance decisions MUST distinguish observed facts, inferences, and unverified claims; provide affected people a fair opportunity to respond when appropriate; and follow qualified HR/legal advice for regulated or sensitive cases.

#### PPL-03.07 — Aggregate trends in reports, safety issues, accommodations…

**Requirement:** Aggregate trends in reports, safety issues, accommodations, and recurring barriers to improve systems. Share only the minimum personal detail needed for action; protect source identity and records under approved retention and legal-hold rules.

### PPL-07 — Performance Development Recognition and Corrective Action

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [07-performance-development-recognition-and-corrective-action.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/07-performance-development-recognition-and-corrective-action.md)

**Control objective.** People and teams need clear expectations, useful feedback, development support, and fair accountability. Performance processes must improve outcomes and capability without reducing complex work to a single metric or bypassing approved employment policies.

#### PPL-07.01 — Each role MUST have current expectations tied to responsibilities…

**Requirement:** Each role MUST have current expectations tied to responsibilities, authority, quality, collaboration, safety, and outcome measures relevant to the work. Expectations MUST be communicated before an evaluation period and revised when scope changes.

#### PPL-07.02 — Managers or designated reviewers MUST provide feedback based on…

**Requirement:** Managers or designated reviewers MUST provide feedback based on observed work and agreed expectations, explain evidence and impact, invite context, and identify actionable next steps. Material feedback MUST not be delivered for the first time as an undocumented consequential decision.

#### PPL-07.03 — Evaluation MUST consider outcome quality, user and team…

**Requirement:** Evaluation MUST consider outcome quality, user and team impact, role scope, constraints, collaboration, incident learning, and opportunities provided. Metrics MUST be contextual and MUST NOT be the sole basis for pay, promotion, discipline, or separation decisions.

#### PPL-07.04 — Before attributing failure to an individual, review workload…

**Requirement:** Before attributing failure to an individual, review workload, unclear authority, missing training, unsafe incentives, tools, dependencies, staffing, and process design. People remain accountable for intentional or negligent conduct under policy, but systemic contributors MUST be addressed as well.

#### PPL-07.05 — When capability gaps are remediable, define support…

**Requirement:** When capability gaps are remediable, define support, mentoring/training, time, expected improvement evidence, and review date. Promotion and recognition criteria MUST be role-related, consistently applied, conflict-reviewed, and accessible under applicable policy.

#### PPL-07.06 — Compensation, promotion, discipline, performance plans…

**Requirement:** Compensation, promotion, discipline, performance plans, termination, or other consequential employment decisions MUST be made by authorized roles using documented evidence, conflict checks, approved policy, and qualified HR/legal review when required by jurisdiction.

#### PPL-07.07 — A person MUST be able to correct factual errors…

**Requirement:** A person MUST be able to correct factual errors and request review of a consequential decision through a route not controlled solely by the original decision-maker where practical. Reviewers MUST consider evidence and policy consistently and protect against retaliation.

#### PPL-07.08 — Performance notes, compensation, health, accommodation…

**Requirement:** Performance notes, compensation, health, accommodation, and corrective-action records MUST have restricted access, retention and deletion rules, and data minimization. Use aggregate team-level learning without exposing individual details unnecessarily.

#### PPL-07.09 — The responsible owner MUST periodically review whether expectations…

**Requirement:** The responsible owner MUST periodically review whether expectations, metrics, workload, opportunity, feedback, and outcomes produce unexplained disparities or perverse incentives. Investigate concerns and update the process based on evidence without assuming a metric alone proves discrimination or fairness.

### EXT-02 — Feedback Complaints Accessibility and Dispute Handling

*Domain:* [stakeholder-relations](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/README.md) · *Rule:* [02-feedback-complaints-accessibility-and-dispute-handling.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/rules/02-feedback-complaints-accessibility-and-dispute-handling.md)

**Control objective.** Complaints and feedback are controlled signals about unmet expectations, harm, defects, accessibility barriers, and trust. The process must be easy to find, impartial, timely, reviewable, and linked to improvement while respecting customer rights and privacy.

#### EXT-02.01 — Publish how users, customers, and partners can…

**Requirement:** Publish how users, customers, and partners can provide feedback or complain, what information is useful, expected acknowledgment/update, available accessibility alternatives, escalation, and any external dispute or regulator route required by applicable terms/law.

#### EXT-02.02 — On receipt, assign an owner, case ID…

**Requirement:** On receipt, assign an owner, case ID, acknowledgment, category, severity, affected people, urgency, and next update. Assess actual or potential harm, privacy/security, accessibility, financial impact, repeated pattern, and whether the complainant needs an immediate protective action.

#### EXT-02.03 — The investigator MUST be independent of the disputed decision…

**Requirement:** The investigator MUST be independent of the disputed decision where practical, preserve relevant records, hear the complainant and affected parties fairly, distinguish facts from assumptions, and identify applicable product, policy, contract, and legal requirements.

#### EXT-02.04 — The decision-maker MUST state findings, rationale, corrective…

**Requirement:** The decision-maker MUST state findings, rationale, corrective action or remedy, owner, timeline, and review/appeal route. A responder MUST not promise refund, service credit, admission, legal position, or roadmap change outside delegated authority.

#### EXT-02.05 — Provide status and outcome within the stated timeframe…

**Requirement:** Provide status and outcome within the stated timeframe and confidentiality limits. If delayed, explain the reason, owner, next update date, and escalation route; do not leave the complainant to repeatedly chase an unowned case.

#### EXT-02.06 — When a complaint concerns legal rights, safety…

**Requirement:** When a complaint concerns legal rights, safety, discrimination, security, privacy, or a high-impact product decision, route it to the appropriate specialist process. Internal complaint handling MUST not block a user from applicable external rights or remedy.

#### EXT-02.07 — Review recurring themes by product, version, user…

**Requirement:** Review recurring themes by product, version, user group, workflow, and impact. Assign corrective action to root causes; verify later whether complaint recurrence, defect, or barrier decreased, and communicate material product changes.

### EXT-04 — External Incident and Stakeholder Communications

*Domain:* [stakeholder-relations](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/README.md) · *Rule:* [04-external-incident-and-stakeholder-communications.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/rules/04-external-incident-and-stakeholder-communications.md)

**Control objective.** External incident communications must be coordinated, truthful, timely, privacy-preserving, and consistent with legal, contractual, and user-safety obligations. Technical investigation, customer service, legal review, and public communication must have distinct responsibilities that coordinate under one incident authority.

#### EXT-04.01 — Each supported service profile MUST identify who declares…

**Requirement:** Each supported service profile MUST identify who declares an incident, approves customer notices, handles media/regulator contact, prepares technical facts, and tracks affected users. Include deputies and a channel to reach them outside ordinary work hours where the service promise requires it.

#### EXT-04.02 — For suspected security, privacy, safety, data…

**Requirement:** For suspected security, privacy, safety, data loss, or widespread availability incidents, the incident lead MUST identify affected products, users, data, jurisdictions, contracts, evidence, and potential notification deadlines. Route to qualified counsel/privacy/security owners immediately; do not wait for full root-cause certainty to begin obligation assessment.

#### EXT-04.03 — Every update MUST distinguish confirmed facts, active investigation…

**Requirement:** Every update MUST distinguish confirmed facts, active investigation, unknowns, user actions, containment, support path, next update, and timestamp. Do not speculate about cause, scope, attribution, or resolution; correct earlier statements when evidence changes.

#### EXT-04.04 — Notices MUST avoid exposing credentials, exploit detail that…

**Requirement:** Notices MUST avoid exposing credentials, exploit detail that increases harm, personal data of other people, confidential customer information, or evidence that would compromise response. Provide specific mitigations users can safely follow and an accessible contact route.

#### EXT-04.05 — Use approved channels to reach directly affected customers…

**Requirement:** Use approved channels to reach directly affected customers, public users, partners, regulators, and internal responders as applicable. Maintain a canonical notice; ensure support, status page, account contacts, and public statements do not contradict it.

#### EXT-04.06 — The incident lead MUST set the next update time…

**Requirement:** The incident lead MUST set the next update time based on impact and obligations even if investigation is ongoing. Closure communication MUST state resolved status, remaining risk, user action, remediation/support path, and where a later post-incident report will appear.

#### EXT-04.07 — After incident closure, assess timeliness, accuracy…

**Requirement:** After incident closure, assess timeliness, accuracy, audience reach, accessibility, privacy, support readiness, and approval bottlenecks. Correct any affected claim and update contact lists, playbooks, training, controls, and contracts.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

The following conditions are explicitly prohibited by the mapped controls. Any one of them is sufficient to fail the requirement.

- [COL-03] Do not assume a shared repository, channel, or manager means shared accountability.
- [COL-03] Do not merge unresolved blocking review concerns into a vague “follow up later” without risk approval.
- [COL-03] Do not create hidden inter-team dependencies through undocumented data formats or privileged access.
- [OPS-06] Do not promise continuous support based on monitoring alone.
- [OPS-06] Do not make one person the only holder of critical recovery knowledge or credentials.
- [OPS-06] Do not leave an incident active without a named lead and next update/decision owner.
- [ORG-03] Do not treat absence of a known regulation or complaint as evidence that an activity is compliant or safe.
- [ORG-03] Do not accept material residual risk on behalf of a user, customer, or data subject without authority.
- [ORG-03] Do not publish legal or compliance claims without evidence, scope, and qualified review when required.
- [PPL-03] Do not retaliate or permit informal punishment for raising a concern or requesting an accommodation.
- [PPL-03] Do not promise absolute confidentiality or conduct an investigation beyond the handler’s authority and competence.
