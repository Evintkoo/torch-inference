# ENG-015 — Bound requests and degrade predictably under pressure

**Requirement ID:** ENG-015  
**Priority:** MUST  
**Source:** [engine-runtime.md](../engine-runtime.md)  
**Acceptance checks:** [AC-ENG-21](../../torviello-engine/specifications/checks/AC-ENG-21.md)  
**Rule controls:** PERF-02, SEC-10, API-02

## 1. Normative requirement

Every inbound boundary MUST enforce a configured limit, every dependency call MUST have a deadline, overload MUST produce an explicit retryable or non-retryable status, and failure isolation MUST prevent one failing backend from consuming all capacity.

The capitalized keywords MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, and MAY are normative in the sense of BCP 14. Where a clause below is conditional, the stated condition determines applicability.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

Inference servers fail in characteristic ways: a large body exhausts memory, a stuck model holds every worker, a failing GPU turns every request into a slow 500, and a burst of clients creates an unbounded queue. The reference implementation includes body and per-item limits, request and chunk timeouts, a per-IP rate limiter, bulkhead permits, circuit breakers, and retry policy components.

Torviello specifies how those pieces compose into client-visible behavior. Limits must be enforced at ingress rather than after allocation; timeouts must release workers; circuit and bulkhead outcomes must be distinguishable; and rate limiting must not throttle probes.

## 3. Priority and interpretation

This requirement is **MUST**. A MUST requirement is necessary for the stated engine behavior and for a safe release; an unmet MUST blocks release unless an approved exception with compensating measures, an owner, and an expiry exists. A SHOULD requirement is expected unless a documented tradeoff justifies deferral; the tradeoff must still name the affected checks and the residual risk.

Priority applies to the behavior observed through the shipping engine, not to the presence of code, a design document, or a test double. Passing evidence must exercise the same boundary that carries the risk.

## 4. Scope and applicability

Applies to all HTTP and WebSocket ingress, model execution, optional upstream calls, and internal queues. It covers body and item limits, concurrency admission, deadlines, circuit breaking, bulkhead saturation, rate limiting, and retry guidance.

The requirement applies to new and materially changed behavior in the declared profiles. A legacy path becomes subject to it when changed, when it participates in a claimed capability, or when a migration explicitly brings it into scope. Behavior outside the declared platform matrix may be excluded only when the exclusion is published and the unsupported path fails clearly.

## 5. Detailed obligations

The following obligations refine the normative statement for design, implementation, and review.

- Enforce JSON body and per-item limits while reading, before decoding or allocating beyond the bound.
- Apply a deadline to every model inference and upstream call and release the worker or permit on expiry.
- Use a file-size and decoded-dimension gate for uploads before full decode.
- Open a circuit after the configured failure threshold and reject fast with a retryable status during the open interval.
- Probe recovery explicitly and close the circuit only after a successful probe or documented policy.
- Reject when concurrency permits are exhausted rather than growing an unbounded queue.
- Exempt health and metrics probes from inference rate limiting and include Retry-After when retry can succeed.

#### PERF-02.01 — Bound request concurrency, queue depth, batch size, model count

The project MUST bound request concurrency, queue depth, batch size, model count, model memory, download size, temporary disk, and request execution time. Defaults MUST be safe on the minimum supported machine.

#### PERF-02.02 — Make tradeoffs between throughput, tail latency, memory, CPU load

The project MUST make tradeoffs between throughput, tail latency, memory, CPU load, GPU utilization, energy use, and idle power explicit in profiles or configuration.

#### PERF-02.03 — Maintain a backend matrix for CPU, CUDA, Metal, Vulkan

The project MUST maintain a backend matrix for CPU, CUDA, Metal, Vulkan, and AMD paths. Each claimed combination lists provider/runtime/driver versions, hardware, build flags, support level, and known limits.

#### PERF-02.04 — Backend selection MUST be observable and deterministic

Backend selection MUST be observable and deterministic. Fallback MUST be allowed only when configured and MUST report why the requested provider was unavailable.

#### PERF-02.05 — Use load shedding and backpressure rather than allowing unbounded

The project MUST use load shedding and backpressure rather than allowing unbounded queues. Overload has a documented client response, metric, and recovery condition.

#### PERF-02.06 — Preserve a CPU-only baseline and validate inference correctness on

The project MUST preserve a CPU-only baseline and validate inference correctness on every advertised backend before performance tuning.

#### SEC-10.01 — Every security-relevant setting MUST have a safe default…

**Requirement:** Every security-relevant setting MUST have a safe default for its deployment profile and a validation rule preventing known-dangerous combinations.

#### SEC-10.02 — Authentication MUST be required for remote administrative and persistent…

**Requirement:** Authentication MUST be required for remote administrative and persistent user-data operations. Local account-free operation MUST be restricted to a clearly defined local exposure boundary.

#### SEC-10.03 — Authentication and authorization failures MUST not disclose credential validity…

**Requirement:** Authentication and authorization failures MUST not disclose credential validity, protected object existence, tenant membership, or internal policy details beyond what the caller needs to remediate.

#### SEC-10.04 — Expensive operations MUST enforce per-actor and global resource budgets…

**Requirement:** Expensive operations MUST enforce per-actor and global resource budgets, deadline, queue admission, cancellation, and retry ceilings; budgets MUST apply to preprocessing, model download, inference, and response serialization.

#### SEC-10.05 — Resource exhaustion MUST degrade predictably: reject new work…

**Requirement:** Resource exhaustion MUST degrade predictably: reject new work, protect existing work according to policy, preserve essential health/administrative recovery, and avoid process-wide failure from one request.

#### SEC-10.06 — Abuse detection MUST correlate repeated authentication failures, model-download…

**Requirement:** Abuse detection MUST correlate repeated authentication failures, model-download abuse, sensitive business flow automation, and unusual resource consumption with bounded signals and privacy-safe retention.

#### SEC-10.07 — Security controls MUST fail closed when their required key…

**Requirement:** Security controls MUST fail closed when their required key, policy, validator, or authorization dependency is unavailable; availability exceptions require a named decision and a bounded degraded mode.

#### API-02.01 — Treat every request field, header, URL, filename, archive entry

The project MUST treat every request field, header, URL, filename, archive entry, image, audio stream, and model input as untrusted. Parse into validated domain types before expensive work.

#### API-02.02 — Bound request bytes, multipart part count, image dimensions, audio

The project MUST bound request bytes, multipart part count, image dimensions, audio duration, decompressed size, queue wait, inference time, concurrent work, and response size. Limits MUST be configurable within safe hard bounds.

#### API-02.03 — Reject invalid content types, unsupported encodings, malformed multipart boundaries

The project MUST reject invalid content types, unsupported encodings, malformed multipart boundaries, path traversal, unexpected archive paths, and values outside schema constraints with safe client errors.

#### API-02.04 — Apply authentication and authorization to each operation

The project MUST apply authentication and authorization to each operation. Health endpoints disclose only their intended readiness state; administrative operations require explicit roles/scopes.

#### API-02.05 — Streaming routes MUST define cancellation, client disconnect handling, backpressure

Streaming routes MUST define cancellation, client disconnect handling, backpressure, timeout, partial result, and cleanup behavior. The project MUST NOT retain unbounded output in memory for a slow consumer.

#### API-02.06 — Use rate limits and concurrency controls at the API

The project MUST use rate limits and concurrency controls at the API boundary for expensive or abuse-sensitive operations. The project MUST also return a documented overload response and expose a bounded metric.

### 5.2 Highest-standard assurance

This requirement is assessed at **HPA-4**, the Highest Practical Assurance level defined in [assurance-standard.md](../assurance-standard.md). The following obligations are additional to section 5 and equally normative.

**Applicable standards:** ISO/IEC 25010:2023 (performance efficiency, reliability), Google SRE monitoring and load practice, OpenTelemetry 1.44.0, IETF BCP 14.

- Admission limits MUST be enforced before body buffering, decode, queue insertion, or model dispatch, and every rejection MUST name the limit and scope.
- Queues, channels, retry budgets, and per-caller concurrency MUST be bounded; saturation MUST produce 429 or 503 with retry guidance, never unbounded growth or 500.
- Deadlines MUST propagate through queue wait, execution, upstream calls, and streaming, and every expiry MUST release its permit.
- Liveness, readiness, and metrics MUST remain available when inference admission is saturated or a circuit is open.

A claim of conformance to an external standard is prohibited unless the complete control-by-control evidence exists; citing the standard is not evidence.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

### 6.0 HPA-4 acceptance rule

Acceptance is binary and evidence-gated by [`acceptance-criteria.md`](../../torviello-engine/specifications/acceptance-criteria.md). The check-specific threshold in section 3 of that document is normative; the outcomes below are necessary but not sufficient. One occurrence of any failure criterion in section 4 of that document fails acceptance, and no partial or conditional pass is permitted.


The requirement is satisfied when all of the following observable outcomes hold on a stated revision and environment.

- An oversized JSON body returns 413 without full buffering.
- An item above its per-item cap returns 413 naming the item.
- An inference exceeding its deadline returns 504 and releases the worker.
- Repeated backend failure opens the circuit and returns 503 while open.
- A successful probe closes the circuit and restores traffic.
- Exhausted permits return 429 engine.busy; rate-limited clients receive Retry-After; probes remain exempt.

Acceptance checks: [AC-ENG-21](../../torviello-engine/specifications/checks/AC-ENG-21.md).

### 6.1 Highest-standard gates

These gates are additional to the outcomes in section 6. A gate that cannot be executed is a blocked check, not a pass.

- Load and soak tests MUST produce a saturation curve with error rate, latency percentiles, queue depth, and recovery time on named hardware.
- Fault injection MUST cover deadline expiry, worker exhaustion, circuit opening and closing, and burst traffic.
- Property tests MUST verify that every configured limit is either enforced at the declared boundary or explicitly disabled.

All gates MUST run against the shipping artifact and configuration, with retained, reproducible evidence.

## 7. Implementation guidance and assessment determination

Enforce limits in transport extractors and streaming readers. Wrap backend calls in a timeout that owns the permit lifetime. Keep circuit and bulkhead state in shared guards and map their outcomes in one error translation layer. Rate limiting should run after correlation assignment and before handlers, with an explicit probe allowlist.

A determination of **pass** requires that every applicable MUST clause is implemented at the authoritative boundary, that each acceptance check has current reproducible evidence, and that no prohibited condition occurs. A determination of **fail** applies when implementation is absent, behavior contradicts the requirement, evidence is missing or stale, or the result cannot be reproduced. **Not applicable** requires a stated deployment or platform condition and reviewer rationale; it cannot be used to excuse an unimplemented capability that the profile claims to support.

Assessment combines examination, interview where an operational procedure is involved, and test. Examination confirms that the design and source address the boundary. Test confirms observable behavior, including at least one negative or boundary case per limit, failure mode, or security decision. Documentation alone does not establish effectiveness.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

- Any failure criterion in [`acceptance-criteria.md`](../../torviello-engine/specifications/acceptance-criteria.md) section 4 is also prohibited and fatal.


- Do not parse or decode a body before enforcing its size limit.
- Do not let a timed-out request retain a worker or permit.
- Do not return 500 for capacity or dependency exhaustion that has a specific status.
- Do not allow unlimited queue growth under saturation.
- Do not rate-limit liveness, readiness, or metrics probes.

Additional control prohibitions apply where the mapped clauses state them. A prohibited condition is a failure even if a happy-path scenario passes.

## 9. Verification procedure

- 1. Send bodies and items above each configured limit and inspect memory and status.
- 2. Use a stub backend that exceeds its deadline and verify worker release.
- 3. Drive repeated backend failures and observe circuit transitions and statuses.
- 4. Saturate permits and send additional requests.
- 5. Exceed the per-IP budget and call health and metrics.
- 6. Record configuration, load, statuses, and resource observations.

The procedure must be executed through the shipping path. A unit-only substitute does not establish that the public boundary enforces the requirement. Record skipped steps and their reason; a skipped check is not passing evidence.

### 9.1 Highest-standard procedure

In addition to the steps above:

- 9. Run the load, soak, and burst suites and retain the saturation curve and recovery timeline.
- 10. Inject deadline, worker, and circuit failures and verify probe availability and status mapping.

Each step MUST retain its command, input digests, environment, and observed result. A skipped step is recorded as skipped and does not count toward a pass.

## 10. Required evidence

- Limit rejection responses and evidence of early enforcement.
- Timeout status and worker-release observation.
- Circuit state transitions and response codes.
- Saturation and rate-limit responses with headers.
- Probe exemption evidence.

Evidence must identify the revision, environment, configuration, fixture, command or inspection, observed result, and any limitation. Evidence that predates a relevant change is stale.

### 10.1 Highest-standard evidence

Retain the following with the evidence bundle described in [assurance-standard.md](../assurance-standard.md):

- The load/soak report with hardware, configuration, and percentiles.
- The saturation and recovery timeline.
- The limit-enforcement matrix and fault-injection records.

Evidence MUST record the source revision, build inputs, toolchain, hardware, configuration, model digests, command, observed result, threshold, and limitations. A summary without retained measurements is not evidence.

## 11. Failure conditions

- Oversized input is fully allocated before rejection.
- A timeout leaves a worker or permit occupied.
- Capacity failures surface as generic 500s.
- Queues grow without bound.
- Probes are throttled by inference limits.

A failure also exists when an approved exception is expired, lacks compensating measures, or is not linked from the affected check.

### 11.1 Highest-standard failure conditions

- HPA-4 assurance evidence is missing, stale, unsigned where required, or below the recorded threshold.

- A queue or retry budget grows without bound.
- Overload returns 500 or blocks probes.
- A deadline expires without releasing its permit or model reference.
