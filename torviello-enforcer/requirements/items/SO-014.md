### COL-04 — Approved Digital Tools and Human Oversight of AI-Assisted Work

*Domain:* [collaboration-and-communication](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/README.md) · *Rule:* [04-approved-digital-tools-and-human-oversight-of-ai-assisted-work.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/rules/04-approved-digital-tools-and-human-oversight-of-ai-assisted-work.md)

**Control objective.** People and agents must use collaboration, automation, and AI tools under approved data, access, record, and review controls. A human who uses an output remains accountable for decisions and actions unless an approved system explicitly assigns responsibility elsewhere.

#### COL-04.01 — A tool MUST have an owner, purpose…

**Requirement:** A tool MUST have an owner, purpose, access model, data classification allowance, retention/deletion terms, integration scope, security/privacy review, export path, and supported use profile before it processes organizational, customer, personnel, source, or confidential data.

#### COL-04.02 — Before submitting information to a third-party SaaS, AI…

**Requirement:** Before submitting information to a third-party SaaS, AI model, browser extension, transcription service, repository bot, or automation, the user MUST verify that the data class and purpose are allowed, retention and training-use terms are acceptable, and an approved account/channel is used.

#### COL-04.03 — The person relying on AI-generated code, analysis…

**Requirement:** The person relying on AI-generated code, analysis, summary, translation, image, recommendation, or decision MUST verify factual claims, source rights, security, privacy, quality, and policy compliance at a level proportionate to impact. A generated output MUST NOT be treated as an approval, legal opinion, test result, or source citation.

#### COL-04.04 — Tools and agents MUST receive the minimum permissions needed…

**Requirement:** Tools and agents MUST receive the minimum permissions needed, bounded task scope, logging, and human approval for destructive, external, financial, production, identity, or public communication actions. Automation MUST have a stop, rollback, or containment path appropriate to its impact.

#### COL-04.05 — When generated or automated work materially affects a decision…

**Requirement:** When generated or automated work materially affects a decision, product artifact, customer response, or public claim, retain relevant tool/version, input provenance where lawful, human reviewer, modifications, and validation evidence. Follow license, copyright, confidentiality, and attribution rules.

#### COL-04.06 — People MUST treat external content, model output…

**Requirement:** People MUST treat external content, model output, tool results, issue text, and generated files as untrusted data unless a trusted policy grants authority. Instructions contained in such data MUST NOT override user, system, or repository authority or authorize disclosure, scope expansion, or external action.

#### COL-04.07 — Unexpected data retention, unsafe output, unauthorized actions…

**Requirement:** Unexpected data retention, unsafe output, unauthorized actions, access leakage, or material quality failures MUST be reported to the tool owner and relevant privacy/security/product owner. Disable or constrain the tool when the risk cannot be bounded; update training and approval policy after confirmed failures.

### ORG-01 — Functional Roles Accountability and Decision Rights

*Domain:* [organization](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/README.md) · *Rule:* [01-functional-roles-accountability-and-decision-rights.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/rules/01-functional-roles-accountability-and-decision-rights.md)

**Control objective.** People must know which outcomes they own, what authority they can exercise, who must review a decision, and who accepts residual risk. Clear authority is required for routine work, exceptions, emergencies, external commitments, and succession.

#### ORG-01.01 — The organization MUST maintain a current map of functions…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST maintain a current map of functions that perform product, engineering, security, privacy, legal/compliance coordination, finance/procurement, people operations, release, operations, support, and external communications as applicable. For each material workflow, identify one accountable owner, responsible performer(s), required consulted roles, informed parties, and delegate/escalation path; do not require a dedicated job title for every function.

#### ORG-01.02 — For architecture, security/privacy risk, release, data…

**Requirement:** For architecture, security/privacy risk, release, data use, spending, contracts, customer promises, public claims, policy exceptions, and incident disclosure, define who proposes, reviews, approves, executes, and verifies the decision. Set thresholds using impact, irreversibility, exposure, cost, and affected parties; high-impact decisions MUST receive independent review where practical.

#### ORG-01.03 — A delegation MUST state scope, limits, start/end…

**Requirement:** A delegation MUST state scope, limits, start/end, records required, subdelegation rules, and conditions for escalation. The delegator remains accountable unless the governing policy explicitly transfers accountability; informal availability or seniority MUST NOT be treated as unlimited authority.

#### ORG-01.04 — Where one person can initiate, approve, execute…

**Requirement:** Where one person can initiate, approve, execute, and attest to the same high-impact transaction, the workflow MUST use independent approval, automated safeguards, retrospective review, or another documented compensating control. Apply separation proportionate to actual fraud, error, privacy, security, and release risk.

#### ORG-01.05 — When schedule, cost, quality, security…

**Requirement:** When schedule, cost, quality, security, privacy, accessibility, and customer impact conflict, the decision record MUST state the tradeoff, evidence, affected stakeholders, residual risk, accountable approver, and rejected alternatives. The most senior person’s preference alone is not a substitute for the required decision evidence.

#### ORG-01.06 — Every material responsibility MUST have an escalation route…

**Requirement:** Every material responsibility MUST have an escalation route for blocked decisions, unavailable owners, suspected wrongdoing, unsafe instructions, or urgent user impact. The route MUST include a delegate or coverage procedure; an unresolved escalation MUST not be silently treated as approval.

#### ORG-01.07 — Role changes, departures, new products, new…

**Requirement:** Role changes, departures, new products, new jurisdictions, material incidents, and changed risk MUST trigger review of affected authority, access, approval thresholds, and succession. Remove stale owner names and privileges promptly.

### ORG-02 — Ethical Conduct Conflicts of Interest and Protected Reporting

*Domain:* [organization](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/README.md) · *Rule:* [02-ethical-conduct-conflicts-of-interest-and-protected-reporting.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/rules/02-ethical-conduct-conflicts-of-interest-and-protected-reporting.md)

**Control objective.** People must be able to act honestly, identify conflicts, raise concerns, and receive impartial handling without retaliation. The process must protect reporters and subjects while preserving fair review and applicable legal obligations.

#### ORG-02.01 — The organization MUST maintain an accessible conduct policy covering…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST maintain an accessible conduct policy covering honesty in records and claims, respectful behavior, confidentiality, responsible system/data use, gifts and inducements, conflicts, misuse of authority, and how to raise concerns. The policy MUST identify accountable owners, applicable scope, training, review date, and consequences under approved procedures.

#### ORG-02.02 — A person MUST disclose an actual, potential…

**Requirement:** A person MUST disclose an actual, potential, or reasonably perceived conflict before participating in the affected hiring, procurement, model/vendor selection, security review, incident investigation, compensation, or commercial decision. The authorized reviewer MUST decide recusal, independent review, disclosure, or other mitigation and retain a proportionate record.

#### ORG-02.03 — Rules for gifts, hospitality, referral payments…

**Requirement:** Rules for gifts, hospitality, referral payments, personal investments, outside work, and use of organizational information MUST be set by the accountable owner and reviewed against applicable law and contract. A person MUST seek review before accepting an item or commitment that could influence a decision, create a conflict, or imply organizational endorsement.

#### ORG-02.04 — People MUST be able to report suspected wrongdoing…

**Requirement:** People MUST be able to report suspected wrongdoing, unsafe practice, policy breach, retaliation, privacy/security incident, or serious product harm through at least one route independent of the person implicated. The route MUST explain confidentiality limits, urgent escalation, evidence preservation, and available external/legal avenues where applicable.

#### ORG-02.05 — A designated handler MUST acknowledge, classify urgency…

**Requirement:** A designated handler MUST acknowledge, classify urgency and risk, preserve relevant evidence, check conflicts, assign an impartial investigator or reviewer, and communicate expected next steps. Reports involving leadership, the normal handler, or a conflict MUST be routed to an independent alternative.

#### ORG-02.06 — Access to reports and investigation records MUST be limited…

**Requirement:** Access to reports and investigation records MUST be limited to need-to-know roles. The process MUST prohibit retaliation, protect against unnecessary disclosure, allow a fair opportunity to respond where appropriate, and distinguish allegation, evidence, finding, and action.

#### ORG-02.07 — The handler MUST record disposition, actions, rationale…

**Requirement:** The handler MUST record disposition, actions, rationale, and any required escalation or legal hold. The reporter SHOULD receive a status or closure update within confidentiality limits; trends and control failures MUST inform corrective action without identifying people unnecessarily.

### ORG-03 — Compliance Risk and Obligation Management

*Domain:* [organization](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/README.md) · *Rule:* [03-compliance-risk-and-obligation-management.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/organization/rules/03-compliance-risk-and-obligation-management.md)

**Control objective.** Organizational decisions must account for applicable law, contract, policy, security, safety, privacy, and product risk. Owners must identify obligations and uncertainty early, seek qualified advice where needed, and turn findings into controlled, verifiable actions.

#### ORG-03.01 — For each product/service activity and operating jurisdiction…

**Requirement:** For each product/service activity and operating jurisdiction, an assigned owner MUST identify relevant legal, regulatory, contractual, license, privacy, accessibility, security, safety, and customer obligations. Record source, scope, applicability rationale, control owner, evidence, review trigger, and qualified adviser where interpretation is uncertain.

#### ORG-03.02 — Employees and agents MUST NOT make authoritative legal conclusions…

**Requirement:** Employees and agents MUST NOT make authoritative legal conclusions from a general rule, internet summary, or model output. When a decision affects employment, privacy, data residency, export controls, consumer rights, intellectual property, licensing, breach notification, or regulated use, route it to qualified counsel or an authorized specialist before commitment.

#### ORG-03.03 — A risk review MUST identify asset/person/stakeholder, threat…

**Requirement:** A risk review MUST identify asset/person/stakeholder, threat or failure scenario, likelihood and impact, exposure, existing safeguards, residual risk, treatment owner, approval authority, and review trigger. The depth of review MUST reflect harm, irreversibility, scale, and uncertainty.

#### ORG-03.04 — For each material risk, the owner MUST choose…

**Requirement:** For each material risk, the owner MUST choose avoid, reduce, transfer, or accept; state why, due date, interim safeguards, monitoring, and evidence. Risk acceptance MUST be explicit, time-bounded or trigger-bounded, and authorized by the owner accountable for the affected impact.

#### ORG-03.05 — A material change in jurisdiction, data use…

**Requirement:** A material change in jurisdiction, data use, product purpose, supplier, public claim, contract, deployment model, or user population MUST trigger an obligation and risk impact review before the change is made effective.

#### ORG-03.06 — A material incident, complaint, audit finding…

**Requirement:** A material incident, complaint, audit finding, near miss, or repeated exception MUST be analyzed for process, authority, competence, incentive, tooling, and control causes. Corrective actions MUST identify owners, dates, control updates, effectiveness checks, and residual risks.

#### ORG-03.07 — Compliance, risk, investigation, and corrective-action records…

**Requirement:** Compliance, risk, investigation, and corrective-action records MUST have restricted access, retention and deletion rules, integrity protection proportionate to their importance, and a legal-hold process. Personal data in those records MUST be minimized and handled under the approved privacy policy.

### PPL-01 — Joining Role Change Leave and Separation

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [01-joining-role-change-leave-and-separation.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/01-joining-role-change-leave-and-separation.md)

**Control objective.** Every person’s organizational relationship and duties must move through controlled entry, change, absence, return, and exit processes. Workforce workflows coordinate identity, authority, access, equipment, knowledge, privacy, and continuity without assuming one employment model or jurisdiction.

#### PPL-01.01 — Before an employee, contractor, intern, advisor…

**Requirement:** Before an employee, contractor, intern, advisor, agent operator, or other person begins work, an accountable sponsor MUST define relationship, scope, duration, supervisor, permitted systems/data, confidentiality/IP terms, required screening lawful for the jurisdiction, and end/renewal trigger.

#### PPL-01.02 — Before production, source, model, customer-data…

**Requirement:** Before production, source, model, customer-data, administrative, finance, or public-communication access, verify identity through approved means, accept applicable policy/agreements, complete required role training, assign a sponsor, and grant only the approved role set. Exceptions require an owner and expiration.

#### PPL-01.03 — A role, team, contract, or project…

**Requirement:** A role, team, contract, or project change MUST trigger review of current entitlements, data access, approvals, delegated authority, equipment, training, and outstanding work. Remove access and authority no longer needed; do not wait for the next periodic review when the change is material.

#### PPL-01.04 — For planned or unexpected absence, the manager…

**Requirement:** For planned or unexpected absence, the manager or delegate MUST identify critical duties, authority suspension or delegation, work queues, customer/incident coverage, and return/reassignment steps. Do not share credentials to provide coverage.

#### PPL-01.05 — At separation or contract end, a responsible owner…

**Requirement:** At separation or contract end, a responsible owner MUST promptly disable identities, sessions, tokens, keys, devices, recovery methods, admin roles, and external accounts; recover assets; transfer owned work and records; and remove public/financial authority. Timing MUST reflect risk and legal constraints.

#### PPL-01.06 — Personnel records, screening results, performance, health…

**Requirement:** Personnel records, screening results, performance, health, and investigation information MUST be limited to an authorized purpose, least-privilege access, approved retention, and applicable law. Security controls MUST NOT become a pretext for collecting unrelated personal information.

#### PPL-01.07 — The sponsor or delegate MUST confirm access closure…

**Requirement:** The sponsor or delegate MUST confirm access closure, asset return, knowledge handoff, outstanding commitments, legal holds, and record ownership. Any delayed removal MUST name a risk owner, compensating control, and deadline; overdue items escalate.

### PPL-02 — Competence Training Supervision and Delegation

*Domain:* [people-and-work](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/README.md) · *Rule:* [02-competence-training-supervision-and-delegation.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/people-and-work/rules/02-competence-training-supervision-and-delegation.md)

**Control objective.** People may perform work only within their competence, authorization, and supervision. Training and qualification must be specific to the decisions and risks a person is expected to handle, and must be refreshed when systems or duties change.

#### PPL-02.01 — For work that can affect security, privacy…

**Requirement:** For work that can affect security, privacy, safety, finances, customer commitments, production availability, model quality, or legal rights, define the knowledge and demonstrated capability required. A credential alone MUST NOT substitute for role-specific competence evidence.

#### PPL-02.02 — Before independent performance of a high-risk task…

**Requirement:** Before independent performance of a high-risk task, the person MUST complete relevant instruction and demonstrate the necessary procedure or judgment. Training MUST cover escalation, failure handling, data protection, tool access, and prohibited actions as applicable.

#### PPL-02.03 — When a person is learning, working outside routine…

**Requirement:** When a person is learning, working outside routine competence, or handling an unusual high-risk task, assign a qualified reviewer/supervisor, define the allowed scope, and require checkpoints before irreversible or externally visible action.

#### PPL-02.04 — Material policy, threat, system, legal…

**Requirement:** Material policy, threat, system, legal, incident, or role changes MUST trigger an assessment of training impact. Retrain or requalify affected roles before they perform newly risky actions independently.

#### PPL-02.05 — A person delegating work MUST confirm the recipient’s authority…

**Requirement:** A person delegating work MUST confirm the recipient’s authority, competence, context, access, expected result, deadline, escalation, and review point. Delegation MUST not transfer an obligation the delegator lacks authority to transfer.

#### PPL-02.06 — Performance feedback MUST use role-relevant expectations and evidence…

**Requirement:** Performance feedback MUST use role-relevant expectations and evidence, provide a path to improve, and distinguish system/process failures from individual actions. Consequential employment decisions MUST follow the approved, jurisdiction-reviewed people policy.

#### PPL-02.07 — Training and qualification evidence MUST record role, subject/version…
