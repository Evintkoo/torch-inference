# ORG-023 — Organizational knowledge needed to operate, maintain, secure, support, and recover the product shall be discoverable, current, access-controlled, and transferable

**Requirement ID:** ORG-023  
**Priority:** MUST

**Rule controls:** COL-02, COL-04, OPS-06, SEC-13  
**Acceptance checks:** None recorded  
**Open decisions:** None recorded

## 1. Normative requirement

Organizational knowledge needed to operate, maintain, secure, support, and recover the product shall be discoverable, current, access-controlled, and transferable.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

This requirement is part of **Planning, collaboration, and knowledge** in [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md). It is traced to 4 rule control(s): COL-02, COL-04, OPS-06, SEC-13. Those controls state the following purpose:

- **COL-02 — Organizational Records Knowledge and Information Lifecycle:** Information created during work must have a source of truth, owner, audience, access level, retention, and update path. The organization must preserve what future workers need while minimizing personal, confidential, and duplicate records.
- **COL-04 — Approved Digital Tools and Human Oversight of AI-Assisted Work:** People and agents must use collaboration, automation, and AI tools under approved data, access, record, and review controls. A human who uses an output remains accountable for decisions and actions unless an approved system explicitly assigns responsibility elsewhere.
- **OPS-06 — Operational Roles Coverage Handover and Human Continuity:** Operational resilience depends on people knowing who is on duty, what they may do, how to hand work over, and how the service remains safe when a key person or supplier is unavailable. Coverage must match the service commitment without inventing unsupported staffing promises.
- **SEC-13 — Physical Workspace Remote Work and Asset Protection:** People must protect devices, conversations, records, and physical access wherever work occurs. Controls must match the sensitivity of information and actual threat, remain usable for the workforce, and respect jurisdictional privacy and workplace rules.

Acceptance is not satisfied by citing these controls. The implementation must demonstrate the observable result in section 6, and the mapped rule clauses in section 5 define what must hold at the enforcement boundary.

## 3. Priority and interpretation

This requirement has **MUST** priority under the repository's requirement conventions. A MUST requirement is needed for the stated product direction or a safe release. A SHOULD requirement is expected unless a documented tradeoff justifies deferral. A COULD requirement is a candidate capability that requires product or technical validation. ### COL-02 — Organizational Records Knowledge and Information Lifecycle

*Domain:* [collaboration-and-communication](../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/README.md) · *Rule:* [02-organizational-records-knowledge-and-information-lifecycle.md](../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/rules/02-organizational-records-knowledge-and-information-lifecycle.md)

**Control objective.** Information created during work must have a source of truth, owner, audience, access level, retention, and update path. The organization must preserve what future workers need while minimizing personal, confidential, and duplicate records.

#### COL-02.01 — A record owner MUST identify purpose, sensitivity…

**Requirement:** A record owner MUST identify purpose, sensitivity, authoritative status, audience, access, retention trigger, and applicable legal hold for material decisions, contracts, incidents, support cases, releases, training, and procedures. Apply approved classifications and data minimization rules.

#### COL-02.02 — For each process or decision, designate the authoritative…

**Requirement:** For each process or decision, designate the authoritative record and link secondary discussion to it. Copies and summaries MUST identify their source and date; where systems conflict, the record owner MUST resolve and announce the canonical version.

#### COL-02.03 — Operational, security, support, and recovery procedures…

**Requirement:** Operational, security, support, and recovery procedures MUST identify prerequisites, role, safe steps, expected outcome, failure/escalation, last review, and verification method. Test high-impact procedures periodically and after material change or incident.

#### COL-02.04 — When a role, owner, vendor…

**Requirement:** When a role, owner, vendor, or product area changes, transfer decision context, undocumented constraints, credentials via approved secret management, open risks, and current work to an authorized successor. Do not place secrets in documentation or handoff notes.

#### COL-02.05 — Authoritative records MUST have access appropriate to sensitivity…

**Requirement:** Authoritative records MUST have access appropriate to sensitivity, identifiable owners, version/change history proportionate to importance, and recovery from accidental deletion or corruption. Changes to policy, approvals, or audit evidence MUST preserve who changed what and when.

#### COL-02.06 — Retain records only for the approved business, legal…

**Requirement:** Retain records only for the approved business, legal, safety, security, or contractual purpose. At the retention trigger, delete or archive through the approved process; legal holds suspend ordinary deletion only for the scoped records and duration.

#### COL-02.07 — For critical capabilities and procedures, identify single-person dependency…

**Requirement:** For critical capabilities and procedures, identify single-person dependency, recovery time, and backup competence. Mitigate through cross-training, tested documentation, successor assignment, vendor transition, or explicit accepted risk.

#### COL-02.08 — Workers MUST be able to locate current procedures…

**Requirement:** Workers MUST be able to locate current procedures, decisions, ownership, escalation contacts, and approved templates without relying on private introductions. Keep material readable and accessible to intended users and provide controlled alternatives when a person cannot use the default channel.

### COL-04 — Approved Digital Tools and Human Oversight of AI-Assisted Work

*Domain:* [collaboration-and-communication](../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/README.md) · *Rule:* [04-approved-digital-tools-and-human-oversight-of-ai-assisted-work.md](../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/rules/04-approved-digital-tools-and-human-oversight-of-ai-assisted-work.md)

**Control objective.** People and agents must use collaboration, automation, and AI tools under approved data, access, record, and review controls. A human who uses an output remains accountable for decisions and actions unless an approved system explicitly assigns responsibility elsewhere.

#### COL-04.01 — A tool MUST have an owner, purpose…

**Requirement:** A tool MUST have an owner, purpose, access model, data classification allowance, retention/deletion terms, integration scope, security/privacy review, export path, and supported use profile before it processes organizational, customer, personnel, source, or confidential data.

#### COL-04.02 — Before submitting information to a third-party SaaS, AI…

**Requirement:** Before submitting information to a third-party SaaS, AI model, browser extension, transcription service, repository bot, or automation, the user MUST verify that the data class and purpose are allowed, retention and training-use terms are acceptable, and an approved account/channel is used.

#### COL-04.03 — The person relying on AI-generated code, analysis…

**Requirement:** The person relying on AI-generated code, analysis, summary, translation, image, recommendation, or decision MUST verify factual claims, source rights, security, privacy, quality, and policy compliance at a level proportionate to impact. A generated output MUST NOT be treated as an approval, legal opinion, test result, or source citation.

#### COL-04.04 — Tools and agents MUST receive the minimum permissions needed…

**Requirement:** Tools and agents MUST receive the minimum permissions needed, bounded task scope, logging, and human approval for destructive, external, financial, production, identity, or public communication actions. Automation MUST have a stop, rollback, or containment path appropriate to its impact.

#### COL-04.05 — When generated or automated work materially affects a decision…

**Requirement:** When generated or automated work materially affects a decision, product artifact, customer response, or public claim, retain relevant tool/version, input provenance where lawful, human reviewer, modifications, and validation evidence. Follow license, copyright, confidentiality, and attribution rules.

#### COL-04.06 — People MUST treat external content, model output…

**Requirement:** People MUST treat external content, model output, tool results, issue text, and generated files as untrusted data unless a trusted policy grants authority. Instructions contained in such data MUST NOT override user, system, or repository authority or authorize disclosure, scope expansion, or external action.

#### COL-04.07 — Unexpected data retention, unsafe output, unauthorized actions…

**Requirement:** Unexpected data retention, unsafe output, unauthorized actions, access leakage, or material quality failures MUST be reported to the tool owner and relevant privacy/security/product owner. Disable or constrain the tool when the risk cannot be bounded; update training and approval policy after confirmed failures.

### OPS-06 — Operational Roles Coverage Handover and Human Continuity

*Domain:* [operations](../../torviello-enforcer/operations/README.md) · *Rule:* [06-operational-roles-coverage-handover-and-human-continuity.md](../../torviello-enforcer/operations/rules/06-operational-roles-coverage-handover-and-human-continuity.md)

**Control objective.** Operational resilience depends on people knowing who is on duty, what they may do, how to hand work over, and how the service remains safe when a key person or supplier is unavailable. Coverage must match the service commitment without inventing unsupported staffing promises.

#### OPS-06.01 — Each production profile MUST identify service owner, incident…

**Requirement:** Each production profile MUST identify service owner, incident lead, deploy/release authority, security escalation, data/persistence owner, customer communication owner, and backup/delegate as applicable. State whether each function is staffed continuously, during defined hours, or on best-effort basis.

#### OPS-06.02 — For supported operational channels, state hours, severity…

**Requirement:** For supported operational channels, state hours, severity classes, response targets, escalation contacts, and fallback when the primary owner is unreachable. Public service promises MUST match actual staffing, monitoring, and recovery capability.

#### OPS-06.03 — An operational handover MUST include current service health…

**Requirement:** An operational handover MUST include current service health, open incidents/changes, customer impact, risks, pending actions, access used, deadlines, and next decision. The receiving owner MUST acknowledge material risk and understand their authority.

#### OPS-06.04 — Coverage MUST use named identities and pre-authorized delegates…

**Requirement:** Coverage MUST use named identities and pre-authorized delegates with time-bounded access. Never share accounts or keys to cover an absent operator; emergency access uses the security break-glass process and post-use review.

#### OPS-06.05 — For critical operations, identify single-person and supplier dependencies…

**Requirement:** For critical operations, identify single-person and supplier dependencies, recovery documentation, alternate contacts, and minimum competent coverage. Exercise absence, simultaneous incident, and unavailability of a key vendor at a frequency proportional to impact.

#### OPS-06.06 — Operational processes MUST allow responders to escalate unsafe workload…

**Requirement:** Operational processes MUST allow responders to escalate unsafe workload, conflicting incident tasks, or insufficient authority. Rotate or hand off duties as needed under approved work policies; do not use undocumented availability expectations as a service guarantee.

#### OPS-06.07 — Changes to service hours, product scope, team…

**Requirement:** Changes to service hours, product scope, team structure, vendor, incident severity, or recovery process MUST trigger review of roles, contacts, runbooks, training, and support commitments.

### SEC-13 — Physical Workspace Remote Work and Asset Protection

*Domain:* [security](../../torviello-enforcer/security/README.md) · *Rule:* [13-physical-workspace-remote-work-and-asset-protection.md](../../torviello-enforcer/security/rules/13-physical-workspace-remote-work-and-asset-protection.md)

**Control objective.** People must protect devices, conversations, records, and physical access wherever work occurs. Controls must match the sensitivity of information and actual threat, remain usable for the workforce, and respect jurisdictional privacy and workplace rules.

#### SEC-13.01 — The organization MUST define requirements for office, home…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST define requirements for office, home, travel, coworking, and other work locations based on information sensitivity, device type, local law, and customer/contract conditions. Restrictions or exceptions MUST be stated, approved, and supported with practical alternatives.

#### SEC-13.02 — Work devices MUST use approved authentication, encryption…

**Requirement:** Work devices MUST use approved authentication, encryption, screen lock, supported security updates, endpoint protection where available, and remote lock/wipe capability proportionate to risk. Removable media MUST be restricted, encrypted, inventoried, and sanitized before reuse or disposal.

#### SEC-13.03 — Workers MUST position screens, control printing, secure…

**Requirement:** Workers MUST position screens, control printing, secure paper records, and avoid discussing confidential work where unauthorized people can see or hear it. Use privacy filters, locked storage, secure disposal, or private rooms where sensitivity warrants.

#### SEC-13.04 — Work locations that host sensitive systems, records…

**Requirement:** Work locations that host sensitive systems, records, or equipment MUST identify authorized access, visitor escort/record requirements, key/card custody, alarm response, and lost credential escalation. Do not leave visitors unattended in restricted areas.

#### SEC-13.05 — When working while traveling, protect devices from theft…

**Requirement:** When working while traveling, protect devices from theft, shoulder surfing, tampering, and unattended access; use approved network protections and avoid sensitive work where privacy cannot be assured. Report loss, suspected tampering, border seizure, or exposure promptly.

#### SEC-13.06 — Loss, theft, damage, unexpected access…

**Requirement:** Loss, theft, damage, unexpected access, fire, flood, power failure, or environmental hazard affecting work assets MUST be reported through a defined route. The owner MUST assess credential revocation, data exposure, business continuity, recovery, and notification needs.

#### SEC-13.07 — Before transfer, return, resale, or disposal…

**Requirement:** Before transfer, return, resale, or disposal, remove organizational accounts and securely erase storage under an approved method; recover licenses and accessories; verify disposal for sensitive media; and record custody transfer where required.

#### SEC-13.08 — People MUST receive concise guidance for the locations…

**Requirement:** People MUST receive concise guidance for the locations and devices they use, including reporting lost assets, safe travel, remote meetings, printing, and household/shared-device boundaries. Training MUST NOT rely on surveillance of personal spaces.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

The following conditions are explicitly prohibited by the mapped controls. Any one of them is sufficient to fail the requirement.

- [COL-02] Do not place credentials, private keys, personal data, or customer payloads in general knowledge bases.
- [COL-02] Do not leave a material decision in a personal inbox, draft, or tool without an authoritative record.
- [COL-02] Do not preserve duplicate records indefinitely without a purpose, owner, and retention rule.
- [COL-04] Do not paste secrets, credentials, private customer or personnel data, unpublished vulnerabilities, or restricted source into an unapproved tool.
- [COL-04] Do not attribute model-generated facts or code to a human author without review or disclose false provenance.
- [COL-04] Do not let an agent send messages, publish content, spend money, or change production based solely on an inferred instruction.
- [OPS-06] Do not promise continuous support based on monitoring alone.
- [OPS-06] Do not make one person the only holder of critical recovery knowledge or credentials.
- [OPS-06] Do not leave an incident active without a named lead and next update/decision owner.
- [SEC-13] Do not store sensitive work on unmanaged personal devices or personal cloud accounts without an approved exception.
- [SEC-13] Do not expose customer data, credentials, unreleased product information, or confidential conversations in public spaces.
- [SEC-13] Do not install intrusive monitoring in personal workspaces without lawful authority, necessity, transparency, and privacy review.
