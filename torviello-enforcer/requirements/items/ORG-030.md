# ORG-030 — External statements and commitments shall be made only by authorized roles using verified, current, and appropriately qualified information

**Requirement ID:** ORG-030  
**Priority:** MUST  
**Source:** [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md) — External commitments and stakeholder trust  
**Status:** Active requirement; no implementation or evaluation evidence is recorded in this repository.  
**Rule controls:** EXT-01, EXT-03, EXT-04  
**Acceptance checks:** None recorded  
**Open decisions:** None recorded

## 1. Normative requirement

External statements and commitments shall be made only by authorized roles using verified, current, and appropriately qualified information.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

This requirement is part of **External commitments and stakeholder trust** in [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md). It is traced to 3 rule control(s): EXT-01, EXT-03, EXT-04. Those controls state the following purpose:

- **EXT-01 — Customer User and Community Support Operations:** Every user-facing request must reach an authorized and competent responder, receive an accurate acknowledgment, and be resolved, escalated, or explicitly bounded. Support must protect user privacy and feed product, security, accessibility, and reliability learning back to owners.
- **EXT-03 — Public Statements Product Claims and Marketing Content:** External-facing claims shape user decisions and organizational obligations. Product, security, privacy, licensing, performance, accessibility, model, and commercial statements must be accurate, scoped, evidenced, approved, and corrected when they become stale.
- **EXT-04 — External Incident and Stakeholder Communications:** External incident communications must be coordinated, truthful, timely, privacy-preserving, and consistent with legal, contractual, and user-safety obligations. Technical investigation, customer service, legal review, and public communication must have distinct responsibilities that coordinate under one incident authority.

Acceptance is not satisfied by citing these controls. The implementation must demonstrate the observable result in section 6, and the mapped rule clauses in section 5 define what must hold at the enforcement boundary.

## 3. Priority and interpretation

This requirement has **MUST** priority under the repository's requirement conventions. A MUST requirement is needed for the stated product direction or a safe release. A SHOULD requirement is expected unless a documented tradeoff justifies deferral. A COULD requirement is a candidate capability that requires product or technical validation. Acceptance describes observable evidence; it does not mandate a specific implementation unless the requirement says so.

### EXT-01 — Customer User and Community Support Operations

*Domain:* [stakeholder-relations](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/README.md) · *Rule:* [01-customer-user-and-community-support-operations.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/rules/01-customer-user-and-community-support-operations.md)

**Control objective.** Every user-facing request must reach an authorized and competent responder, receive an accurate acknowledgment, and be resolved, escalated, or explicitly bounded. Support must protect user privacy and feed product, security, accessibility, and reliability learning back to owners.

#### EXT-01.01 — For each supported product profile, state supported audiences…

**Requirement:** For each supported product profile, state supported audiences, support channel, hours/response expectations, languages/accessibility options as applicable, supported versions, escalation boundaries, and what the channel cannot provide. Do not imply 24/7 or guaranteed response absent an approved staffed commitment.

#### EXT-01.02 — A support owner MUST capture a case identifier…

**Requirement:** A support owner MUST capture a case identifier, minimal contact/context, product/version/profile, impact, urgency, category, consent/data handling where relevant, owner, status, and next update. Classify security, privacy, safety, accessibility, billing, data-loss, outage, and ordinary usage cases distinctly.

#### EXT-01.03 — The responder MUST confirm receipt, explain next steps…

**Requirement:** The responder MUST confirm receipt, explain next steps and realistic response timing, and avoid promises beyond authority. If the issue is urgent or outside support competence, route it to the designated incident, privacy, security, legal, or product owner.

#### EXT-01.04 — Request only the diagnostic information needed; prefer synthetic…

**Requirement:** Request only the diagnostic information needed; prefer synthetic or redacted data. Provide a secure transfer path for necessary sensitive files, restrict case access, set retention, and never ask users to send passwords, private keys, or unnecessary inference content.

#### EXT-01.05 — Support responses MUST distinguish verified facts, hypotheses…

**Requirement:** Support responses MUST distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, workarounds, and commitments; explain limitations plainly; preserve user dignity; and provide accessible alternatives where a product barrier blocks use.

#### EXT-01.06 — The case owner MUST record diagnosis, workaround/fix…

**Requirement:** The case owner MUST record diagnosis, workaround/fix, affected versions, user communication, residual risk, escalation, and closure. For unresolved issues, state owner, next update, and safe workaround; closure MUST NOT mean merely that a ticket was transferred.

#### EXT-01.07 — Repeated complaints, confusing behavior, support workarounds…

**Requirement:** Repeated complaints, confusing behavior, support workarounds, accessibility barriers, unsafe use, and common failures MUST be summarized for accountable product and engineering owners. Convert recurring risks into requirements, defects, documentation, controls, or product decisions with traceable follow-up.

#### EXT-01.08 — Support measures MAY include response and resolution times…

**Requirement:** Support measures MAY include response and resolution times, backlog age, reopen rate, user impact, and satisfaction, but MUST be interpreted with case complexity, safety, fairness, and quality. Do not reward fast closure at the expense of accurate diagnosis or user outcome.

### EXT-03 — Public Statements Product Claims and Marketing Content

*Domain:* [stakeholder-relations](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/README.md) · *Rule:* [03-public-statements-product-claims-and-marketing-content.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/rules/03-public-statements-product-claims-and-marketing-content.md)

**Control objective.** External-facing claims shape user decisions and organizational obligations. Product, security, privacy, licensing, performance, accessibility, model, and commercial statements must be accurate, scoped, evidenced, approved, and corrected when they become stale.

#### EXT-03.01 — Each public channel and content type MUST have…

**Requirement:** Each public channel and content type MUST have an owner, authorized publisher, subject-matter reviewers, approval path, and correction process. Material claims involving security, privacy, legal rights, license, availability, support, or commercial terms MUST receive relevant specialist review.

#### EXT-03.02 — A claim MUST identify the product/profile/version, tested conditions…

**Requirement:** A claim MUST identify the product/profile/version, tested conditions, limitations, date/evidence, and source owner. Performance, accuracy, hardware support, model quality, security, accessibility, privacy, and reliability claims MUST match reproducible evidence and actual supported behavior.

#### EXT-03.03 — Reviewers MUST assess likely user interpretation, omitted conditions…

**Requirement:** Reviewers MUST assess likely user interpretation, omitted conditions, comparison baseline, target audience, and whether a prototype or aspiration could be mistaken for a shipped guarantee. Disclaimers MUST not contradict a headline or obscure a material limitation.

#### EXT-03.04 — Use of customer names, testimonials, partner marks…

**Requirement:** Use of customer names, testimonials, partner marks, certification symbols, standards references, model/provider names, or endorsements MUST have permission, accurate context, rights review, and current verification. Never imply certification or independent validation that has not occurred.

#### EXT-03.05 — Pricing, licensing, warranty, support level…

**Requirement:** Pricing, licensing, warranty, support level, privacy notice, data use, service level, roadmap, and legal terms MUST come from an approved source and authorized owner. Content editors MUST not improvise an enforceable commitment.

#### EXT-03.06 — Assign each material page, release note, demo…

**Requirement:** Assign each material page, release note, demo, social post, and sales artifact an owner and review trigger. Update or remove stale claims when the product, model, license, vulnerability status, supported profile, or commitment changes.

#### EXT-03.07 — When a published statement is materially false or misleading…

**Requirement:** When a published statement is materially false or misleading, notify the accountable owner, assess affected users and obligations, correct the source and downstream copies, and communicate the correction through a proportionate channel. Preserve evidence of what was changed and when.

#### EXT-03.08 — Before publishing, verify rights and approvals for code…

**Requirement:** Before publishing, verify rights and approvals for code, imagery, user stories, screenshots, telemetry, support cases, employee identities, and customer data. Redact secrets and identifying data; use explicit consent and approved retention for testimonials or case studies.

### EXT-04 — External Incident and Stakeholder Communications

*Domain:* [stakeholder-relations](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/README.md) · *Rule:* [04-external-incident-and-stakeholder-communications.md](../../../torviello-enforcer/stakeholder-relations/rules/04-external-incident-and-stakeholder-communications.md)

**Control objective.** External incident communications must be coordinated, truthful, timely, privacy-preserving, and consistent with legal, contractual, and user-safety obligations. Technical investigation, customer service, legal review, and public communication must have distinct responsibilities that coordinate under one incident authority.

#### EXT-04.01 — Each supported service profile MUST identify who declares…

**Requirement:** Each supported service profile MUST identify who declares an incident, approves customer notices, handles media/regulator contact, prepares technical facts, and tracks affected users. Include deputies and a channel to reach them outside ordinary work hours where the service promise requires it.

#### EXT-04.02 — For suspected security, privacy, safety, data…

**Requirement:** For suspected security, privacy, safety, data loss, or widespread availability incidents, the incident lead MUST identify affected products, users, data, jurisdictions, contracts, evidence, and potential notification deadlines. Route to qualified counsel/privacy/security owners immediately; do not wait for full root-cause certainty to begin obligation assessment.

#### EXT-04.03 — Every update MUST distinguish confirmed facts, active investigation…

**Requirement:** Every update MUST distinguish confirmed facts, active investigation, unknowns, user actions, containment, support path, next update, and timestamp. Do not speculate about cause, scope, attribution, or resolution; correct earlier statements when evidence changes.

#### EXT-04.04 — Notices MUST avoid exposing credentials, exploit detail that…

**Requirement:** Notices MUST avoid exposing credentials, exploit detail that increases harm, personal data of other people, confidential customer information, or evidence that would compromise response. Provide specific mitigations users can safely follow and an accessible contact route.

#### EXT-04.05 — Use approved channels to reach directly affected customers…

**Requirement:** Use approved channels to reach directly affected customers, public users, partners, regulators, and internal responders as applicable. Maintain a canonical notice; ensure support, status page, account contacts, and public statements do not contradict it.

#### EXT-04.06 — The incident lead MUST set the next update time…

**Requirement:** The incident lead MUST set the next update time based on impact and obligations even if investigation is ongoing. Closure communication MUST state resolved status, remaining risk, user action, remediation/support path, and where a later post-incident report will appear.

#### EXT-04.07 — After incident closure, assess timeliness, accuracy…

**Requirement:** After incident closure, assess timeliness, accuracy, audience reach, accessibility, privacy, support readiness, and approval bottlenecks. Correct any affected claim and update contact lists, playbooks, training, controls, and contracts.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

The following conditions are explicitly prohibited by the mapped controls. Any one of them is sufficient to fail the requirement.

- [EXT-01] Do not close a case solely because ownership was transferred.
- [EXT-01] Do not request secrets, unrestricted production access, or real user data when safer diagnostic evidence is sufficient.
- [EXT-01] Do not make a support promise or product roadmap commitment without the authorized owner.
- [EXT-03] Do not state that the product is secure, compliant, certified, private, unbiased, production-ready, or supported without defined scope and evidence.
- [EXT-03] Do not present a target feature, benchmark from a different configuration, or supplier statement as a verified product guarantee.
- [EXT-03] Do not publish customer or employee information because it is already visible in an internal tool.
- [EXT-04] Do not delay legally required or user-protective notice solely to achieve certainty or reputational convenience.
- [EXT-04] Do not publish unverified root-cause or scope statements.
- [EXT-04] Do not let marketing or sales approval replace incident, privacy, security, or legal authority.
