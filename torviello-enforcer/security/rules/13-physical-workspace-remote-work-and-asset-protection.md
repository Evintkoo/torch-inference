# Physical Workspace Remote Work and Asset Protection

**Control family:** security  
**Control document:** SEC-13  
**Requirement trace:** ORG-010–ORG-013, ORG-023, SO-001–SO-005, SO-010–SO-014, RP-020–RP-024  
**Status:** Normative project control

## Control objective

People must protect devices, conversations, records, and physical access wherever work occurs. Controls must match the sensitivity of information and actual threat, remain usable for the workforce, and respect jurisdictional privacy and workplace rules.

## Applicability

This control applies whenever a person, team, contractor, coding agent, automated agent, or delegated function performs an activity in scope. Responsibilities are functional roles, not mandated job titles or headcount; one person may hold multiple roles when conflicts and approval-separation risks are controlled. Apply jurisdiction-specific employment, safety, privacy, consumer, and contract rules through an accountable qualified owner.

## Normative requirements

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" are interpreted as described by IETF BCP 14 only when they appear in all capitals.

### SEC-13.01 — The organization MUST define requirements for office, home…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST define requirements for office, home, travel, coworking, and other work locations based on information sensitivity, device type, local law, and customer/contract conditions. Restrictions or exceptions MUST be stated, approved, and supported with practical alternatives.

### SEC-13.02 — Work devices MUST use approved authentication, encryption…

**Requirement:** Work devices MUST use approved authentication, encryption, screen lock, supported security updates, endpoint protection where available, and remote lock/wipe capability proportionate to risk. Removable media MUST be restricted, encrypted, inventoried, and sanitized before reuse or disposal.

### SEC-13.03 — Workers MUST position screens, control printing, secure…

**Requirement:** Workers MUST position screens, control printing, secure paper records, and avoid discussing confidential work where unauthorized people can see or hear it. Use privacy filters, locked storage, secure disposal, or private rooms where sensitivity warrants.

### SEC-13.04 — Work locations that host sensitive systems, records…

**Requirement:** Work locations that host sensitive systems, records, or equipment MUST identify authorized access, visitor escort/record requirements, key/card custody, alarm response, and lost credential escalation. Do not leave visitors unattended in restricted areas.

### SEC-13.05 — When working while traveling, protect devices from theft…

**Requirement:** When working while traveling, protect devices from theft, shoulder surfing, tampering, and unattended access; use approved network protections and avoid sensitive work where privacy cannot be assured. Report loss, suspected tampering, border seizure, or exposure promptly.

### SEC-13.06 — Loss, theft, damage, unexpected access…

**Requirement:** Loss, theft, damage, unexpected access, fire, flood, power failure, or environmental hazard affecting work assets MUST be reported through a defined route. The owner MUST assess credential revocation, data exposure, business continuity, recovery, and notification needs.

### SEC-13.07 — Before transfer, return, resale, or disposal…

**Requirement:** Before transfer, return, resale, or disposal, remove organizational accounts and securely erase storage under an approved method; recover licenses and accessories; verify disposal for sensitive media; and record custody transfer where required.

### SEC-13.08 — People MUST receive concise guidance for the locations…

**Requirement:** People MUST receive concise guidance for the locations and devices they use, including reporting lost assets, safe travel, remote meetings, printing, and household/shared-device boundaries. Training MUST NOT rely on surveillance of personal spaces.

## Prohibited conditions

- Do not store sensitive work on unmanaged personal devices or personal cloud accounts without an approved exception.
- Do not expose customer data, credentials, unreleased product information, or confidential conversations in public spaces.
- Do not install intrusive monitoring in personal workspaces without lawful authority, necessity, transparency, and privacy review.

## Decision procedure

Choose safeguards based on data sensitivity, physical exposure, device manageability, travel, local employment/privacy law, threat model, and work accessibility. If a control is impractical, choose a safer alternative that preserves the protection and document residual risk.

## Assessment procedure

### Examine

- Sample a complete instance of the physical workspace remote work and asset protection workflow from trigger through owner, action, approval, record, user/stakeholder outcome, and closure.
- Inspect the current role map, procedure, access/records, exception, and supporting evidence; confirm the actual workflow matches the written one.

### Interview

- Interview the person performing the work and the accountable owner separately where practical. Ask how they know they are authorized, where to escalate, what happens on failure, and where the durable record lives.

### Test

Assess office, remote, travel, visitor, lost-device, and disposal scenarios. Verify approved safeguards are usable, asset custody is known, reporting works, credentials can be revoked, and sensitive media is recoverable or destroyed.

## Assessment determination

Pass requires every applicable MUST to be followed and supported by current evidence. Fail applies when authority is unclear, an action bypasses required review, a person cannot identify the safe next step, a required record or safeguard is absent, or evidence is stale. Not applicable requires a stated condition and owner rationale.

## Required evidence

- Workflow record identifying applicable SEC-13 controls, functional owners, approvals, affected requirements, and outcome.
- Relevant access, training, decision, communication, support, audit, or corrective-action evidence, minimized to the data needed to assess the control.
- Approved exception with owner, risk, compensating control, expiry/review date, and revalidation plan when the standard cannot be met.

## Exceptions and nonconformance

A MUST deviation requires approval by an owner authorized for the affected risk, with independent review where practical. Record scope, rationale, impact, interim protection, remediation owner, and expiry or review trigger. Applicable law, contractual duties, safety obligations, and user rights cannot be waived by an internal exception.
