# Recruitment Selection and Hiring Decisions

**Control family:** people-and-work  
**Control document:** PPL-06  
**Requirement trace:** ORG-010, ORG-045, ORG-001–ORG-003, ORG-013  
**Status:** Normative project control

## Control objective

Recruitment and selection decisions must be job-related, fair, authorized, privacy-preserving, and supported by consistent evidence. The hiring process must identify the capability needed and select for that capability without promising terms or collecting information beyond lawful business need.

## Applicability

This control applies whenever a person, team, contractor, coding agent, automated agent, or delegated function performs an activity in scope. Responsibilities are functional roles, not mandated job titles or headcount; one person may hold multiple roles when conflicts and approval-separation risks are controlled. Apply jurisdiction-specific employment, safety, privacy, consumer, and contract rules through an accountable qualified owner.

## Normative requirements

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" are interpreted as described by IETF BCP 14 only when they appear in all capitals.

### PPL-06.01 — Before recruitment begins, an accountable owner MUST approve…

**Requirement:** Before recruitment begins, an accountable owner MUST approve the role purpose, outcomes, essential competencies, reporting/delegation boundary, budget, employment or contractor form, and decision authority. Do not recruit for an undefined title or imply a role grants unapproved product, spending, or access authority.

### PPL-06.02 — Selection criteria and interview/assessment methods MUST relate to actual…

**Requirement:** Selection criteria and interview/assessment methods MUST relate to actual role outcomes and be consistently applied to candidates. Define essential versus trainable capabilities, accessible participation, scoring or evidence method, and decision threshold before evaluating applicants.

### PPL-06.03 — Interviewers MUST disclose personal, financial, or other…

**Requirement:** Interviewers MUST disclose personal, financial, or other conflicts and recuse or use independent review where appropriate. Hiring decisions MUST be based on documented job-related evidence, not protected characteristics, affinity, prestige, personal similarity, or undisclosed criteria, subject to applicable law.

### PPL-06.04 — Collect only candidate information needed for the approved process…

**Requirement:** Collect only candidate information needed for the approved process; provide required notices and consent where applicable; restrict access; secure interview notes and assessments; set retention/deletion triggers; and use approved systems. Sensitive screening MUST be lawful, proportionate, and performed by authorized roles.

### PPL-06.05 — Reference checks, background checks, work samples…

**Requirement:** Reference checks, background checks, work samples, psychometric tests, and automated ranking MUST have an authorized purpose, valid criteria, proportionality, transparency where required, appropriate human review, and a challenge/correction path. An automated score MUST NOT be the sole basis for a consequential decision without an approved lawful process.

### PPL-06.06 — Only an authorized role MAY make or change…

**Requirement:** Only an authorized role MAY make or change an offer, compensation term, work location, start date, confidentiality/IP term, or contractual commitment. Candidate communications MUST be timely, accurate, respectful, accessible, and consistent with approved terms.

### PPL-06.07 — The owner MUST record the selection rationale and approvals…

**Requirement:** The owner MUST record the selection rationale and approvals in protected records, notify candidates appropriately, dispose of data under retention rules, and hand the approved role, duties, conditions, training, and access requirements to the onboarding owner.

## Prohibited conditions

- Do not ask candidates to provide confidential information from a current or former employer.
- Do not promise employment, compensation, access, remote-work terms, or product authority without approval.
- Do not use candidate data, automated tools, or screening results outside the disclosed and approved process.

## Decision procedure

Define the job outcome first; choose evaluation methods that directly test essential capabilities; apply the same criteria consistently; check conflicts, accessibility, and jurisdictional requirements; then approve the decision and offer through the named authority. Seek qualified HR/legal advice for protected data, screening, accommodations, and local hiring law.

## Assessment procedure

### Examine

- Sample a complete instance of the recruitment selection and hiring decisions workflow from trigger through owner, action, approval, record, user/stakeholder outcome, and closure.
- Inspect the current role map, procedure, access/records, exception, and supporting evidence; confirm the actual workflow matches the written one.

### Interview

- Interview the person performing the work and the accountable owner separately where practical. Ask how they know they are authorized, where to escalate, what happens on failure, and where the durable record lives.

### Test

Review a role approval, interview plan, candidate record, reference/screening decision, offer approval, and rejection/retention flow. Verify criteria existed before evaluation, data was minimized, conflicts were handled, decisions are traceable, and no automated decision lacked accountable human review.

## Assessment determination

Pass requires every applicable MUST to be followed and supported by current evidence. Fail applies when authority is unclear, an action bypasses required review, a person cannot identify the safe next step, a required record or safeguard is absent, or evidence is stale. Not applicable requires a stated condition and owner rationale.

## Required evidence

- Workflow record identifying applicable PPL-06 controls, functional owners, approvals, affected requirements, and outcome.
- Relevant access, training, decision, communication, support, audit, or corrective-action evidence, minimized to the data needed to assess the control.
- Approved exception with owner, risk, compensating control, expiry/review date, and revalidation plan when the standard cannot be met.

## Exceptions and nonconformance

A MUST deviation requires approval by an owner authorized for the affected risk, with independent review where practical. Record scope, rationale, impact, interim protection, remediation owner, and expiry or review trigger. Applicable law, contractual duties, safety obligations, and user rights cannot be waived by an internal exception.
