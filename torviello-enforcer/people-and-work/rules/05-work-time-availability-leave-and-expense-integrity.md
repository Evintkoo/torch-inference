# Work Time Availability Leave and Expense Integrity

**Control family:** people-and-work  
**Control document:** PPL-05  
**Requirement trace:** ORG-011, ORG-044, ORG-010, ORG-013  
**Status:** Normative project control

## Control objective

People must follow approved work-time, availability, leave, expense, and resource-use policies with accurate records and safe escalation. These controls require a policy owner and lawful, transparent procedures; they do not define a universal schedule, compensation model, or employment arrangement.

## Applicability

This control applies whenever a person, team, contractor, coding agent, automated agent, or delegated function performs an activity in scope. Responsibilities are functional roles, not mandated job titles or headcount; one person may hold multiple roles when conflicts and approval-separation risks are controlled. Apply jurisdiction-specific employment, safety, privacy, consumer, and contract rules through an accountable qualified owner.

## Normative requirements

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" are interpreted as described by IETF BCP 14 only when they appear in all capitals.

### PPL-05.01 — Each role MUST have documented expectations for availability…

**Requirement:** Each role MUST have documented expectations for availability, on-call duty, response windows, work location, time recording if required, and escalation. Expectations MUST be consistent with the applicable agreement, service promise, and jurisdiction-specific policy; informal messages MUST NOT create hidden permanent obligations.

### PPL-05.02 — Where time or activity records are required…

**Requirement:** Where time or activity records are required, the person MUST report actual work, leave, on-call, or expense information accurately and promptly. Managers MUST correct process or workload issues rather than pressure people to falsify records or work off the record.

### PPL-05.03 — Leave and absence MUST use the approved confidential process…

**Requirement:** Leave and absence MUST use the approved confidential process, identify only information required by policy, and trigger appropriate duty coverage and access/delegation review. Managers MUST not disclose sensitive leave information to coworkers beyond operational need.

### PPL-05.04 — A person requesting reimbursement or using organizational funds MUST…

**Requirement:** A person requesting reimbursement or using organizational funds MUST state business purpose, amount, date, recipient, and evidence required by policy, disclose conflicts, and use approved categories. Approvers MUST verify legitimacy, policy, budget, and separation-of-duties controls.

### PPL-05.05 — Cards, accounts, credits, cloud budgets…

**Requirement:** Cards, accounts, credits, cloud budgets, travel booking, and subscriptions MUST be assigned to an authorized person, purpose, limit, and reconciliation owner. Lost instruments, suspicious charges, duplicate claims, and out-of-policy purchases MUST be reported promptly.

### PPL-05.06 — People MUST be able to report fatigue, excessive…

**Requirement:** People MUST be able to report fatigue, excessive workload, unsafe work hours, conflicting on-call assignments, or inability to meet response commitments without retaliation. The owner MUST rebalance work, change coverage, or reduce the service commitment rather than conceal the risk.

### PPL-05.07 — Time, leave, payroll, reimbursement…

**Requirement:** Time, leave, payroll, reimbursement, and expense evidence MUST be access-controlled and retained only according to approved legal, financial, employment, and audit requirements. Product or engineering repositories MUST NOT become shadow HR or finance databases.

## Prohibited conditions

- Do not submit knowingly false time, expense, leave, or reimbursement records.
- Do not use personal payment or accounts to evade approval, procurement, tax, or security controls.
- Do not disclose private employment or financial details to establish routine operational coverage.

## Decision procedure

Before work begins, determine which approved policy applies to the person and jurisdiction, who approves exceptions, what evidence is required, and how work coverage is maintained. For spending, assess business purpose, budget, total cost, conflict, and approval authority; escalate uncertain tax/employment treatment to the relevant qualified owner.

## Assessment procedure

### Examine

- Sample a complete instance of the work time availability leave and expense integrity workflow from trigger through owner, action, approval, record, user/stakeholder outcome, and closure.
- Inspect the current role map, procedure, access/records, exception, and supporting evidence; confirm the actual workflow matches the written one.

### Interview

- Interview the person performing the work and the accountable owner separately where practical. Ask how they know they are authorized, where to escalate, what happens on failure, and where the durable record lives.

### Test

Sample a time entry where required, leave handoff, reimbursement, recurring subscription, and workload escalation. Verify accuracy, approval, budget, privacy, reconciliation, coverage, and exception handling.

## Assessment determination

Pass requires every applicable MUST to be followed and supported by current evidence. Fail applies when authority is unclear, an action bypasses required review, a person cannot identify the safe next step, a required record or safeguard is absent, or evidence is stale. Not applicable requires a stated condition and owner rationale.

## Required evidence

- Workflow record identifying applicable PPL-05 controls, functional owners, approvals, affected requirements, and outcome.
- Relevant access, training, decision, communication, support, audit, or corrective-action evidence, minimized to the data needed to assess the control.
- Approved exception with owner, risk, compensating control, expiry/review date, and revalidation plan when the standard cannot be met.

## Exceptions and nonconformance

A MUST deviation requires approval by an owner authorized for the affected risk, with independent review where practical. Record scope, rationale, impact, interim protection, remediation owner, and expiry or review trigger. Applicable law, contractual duties, safety obligations, and user rights cannot be waived by an internal exception.
