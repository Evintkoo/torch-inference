# Respectful Accessible and Safe Work

**Control family:** people-and-work  
**Control document:** PPL-03  
**Requirement trace:** ORG-005, ORG-013, ORG-042, SO-004, ED-033  
**Status:** Normative project control

## Control objective

Work practices must protect people’s safety, dignity, accessibility, and ability to raise concerns. The organization must maintain qualified, jurisdiction-aware policies and fair response processes instead of relying on informal norms or individual goodwill.

## Applicability

This control applies whenever a person, team, contractor, coding agent, automated agent, or delegated function performs an activity in scope. Responsibilities are functional roles, not mandated job titles or headcount; one person may hold multiple roles when conflicts and approval-separation risks are controlled. Apply jurisdiction-specific employment, safety, privacy, consumer, and contract rules through an accountable qualified owner.

## Normative requirements

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" are interpreted as described by IETF BCP 14 only when they appear in all capitals.

### PPL-03.01 — A designated owner MUST maintain current, accessible policies…

**Requirement:** A designated owner MUST maintain current, accessible policies for respectful conduct, discrimination/harassment prevention, accessibility accommodations, workplace health/safety relevant to the work, acceptable technology use, and non-retaliation. Policy scope, reporting routes, responsible handlers, and applicable jurisdictions MUST be stated.

### PPL-03.02 — People MUST have an accessible reporting route for unsafe…

**Requirement:** People MUST have an accessible reporting route for unsafe, discriminatory, harassing, retaliatory, or coercive behavior and an alternative when the normal manager or handler is implicated. Emergency or immediate physical danger MUST have a direct urgent response route.

### PPL-03.03 — Reports MUST be acknowledged, risk-triaged, assigned…

**Requirement:** Reports MUST be acknowledged, risk-triaged, assigned to a trained impartial handler, and handled with access limited to need-to-know. The process MUST state confidentiality limits, evidence preservation, protective measures, fair response, escalation, and closure.

### PPL-03.04 — The organization MUST prohibit retaliation for good-faith reporting…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST prohibit retaliation for good-faith reporting, requesting accommodation, participating in an investigation, or raising a safety/quality concern. Monitor for adverse treatment after reports and provide a separate escalation path.

### PPL-03.05 — Work tools, documentation, meetings, training…

**Requirement:** Work tools, documentation, meetings, training, and reporting channels SHOULD be usable by people with differing access needs. When an accommodation or accessibility barrier is raised, assign an owner, assess confidentiality and safety, and provide an effective alternative while resolving the barrier.

### PPL-03.06 — Investigations and performance decisions MUST distinguish observed facts…

**Requirement:** Investigations and performance decisions MUST distinguish observed facts, inferences, and unverified claims; provide affected people a fair opportunity to respond when appropriate; and follow qualified HR/legal advice for regulated or sensitive cases.

### PPL-03.07 — Aggregate trends in reports, safety issues, accommodations…

**Requirement:** Aggregate trends in reports, safety issues, accommodations, and recurring barriers to improve systems. Share only the minimum personal detail needed for action; protect source identity and records under approved retention and legal-hold rules.

## Prohibited conditions

- Do not retaliate or permit informal punishment for raising a concern or requesting an accommodation.
- Do not promise absolute confidentiality or conduct an investigation beyond the handler’s authority and competence.
- Do not treat a hostile or inaccessible work environment as an individual resilience problem.

## Decision procedure

Route an issue by urgency, immediate safety, privacy, conflict of interest, legal jurisdiction, and need for independent handling. Choose interim protections that are proportionate and non-punitive, preserve evidence, and do not pre-judge findings.

## Assessment procedure

### Examine

- Sample a complete instance of the respectful accessible and safe work workflow from trigger through owner, action, approval, record, user/stakeholder outcome, and closure.
- Inspect the current role map, procedure, access/records, exception, and supporting evidence; confirm the actual workflow matches the written one.

### Interview

- Interview the person performing the work and the accountable owner separately where practical. Ask how they know they are authorized, where to escalate, what happens on failure, and where the durable record lives.

### Test

Exercise a manager-implicated complaint, an urgent safety report, a denied accessibility barrier, and a retaliation concern. Verify alternate handler, response timing, need-to-know limits, interim protection, fair process, and effectiveness review.

## Assessment determination

Pass requires every applicable MUST to be followed and supported by current evidence. Fail applies when authority is unclear, an action bypasses required review, a person cannot identify the safe next step, a required record or safeguard is absent, or evidence is stale. Not applicable requires a stated condition and owner rationale.

## Required evidence

- Workflow record identifying applicable PPL-03 controls, functional owners, approvals, affected requirements, and outcome.
- Relevant access, training, decision, communication, support, audit, or corrective-action evidence, minimized to the data needed to assess the control.
- Approved exception with owner, risk, compensating control, expiry/review date, and revalidation plan when the standard cannot be met.

## Exceptions and nonconformance

A MUST deviation requires approval by an owner authorized for the affected risk, with independent review where practical. Record scope, rationale, impact, interim protection, remediation owner, and expiry or review trigger. Applicable law, contractual duties, safety obligations, and user rights cannot be waived by an internal exception.
