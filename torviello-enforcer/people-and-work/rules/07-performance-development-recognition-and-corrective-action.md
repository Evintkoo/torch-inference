# Performance Development Recognition and Corrective Action

**Control family:** people-and-work  
**Control document:** PPL-07  
**Requirement trace:** ORG-010, ORG-013, ORG-042, ORG-046, ED-014  
**Status:** Normative project control

## Control objective

People and teams need clear expectations, useful feedback, development support, and fair accountability. Performance processes must improve outcomes and capability without reducing complex work to a single metric or bypassing approved employment policies.

## Applicability

This control applies whenever a person, team, contractor, coding agent, automated agent, or delegated function performs an activity in scope. Responsibilities are functional roles, not mandated job titles or headcount; one person may hold multiple roles when conflicts and approval-separation risks are controlled. Apply jurisdiction-specific employment, safety, privacy, consumer, and contract rules through an accountable qualified owner.

## Normative requirements

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" are interpreted as described by IETF BCP 14 only when they appear in all capitals.

### PPL-07.01 — Each role MUST have current expectations tied to responsibilities…

**Requirement:** Each role MUST have current expectations tied to responsibilities, authority, quality, collaboration, safety, and outcome measures relevant to the work. Expectations MUST be communicated before an evaluation period and revised when scope changes.

### PPL-07.02 — Managers or designated reviewers MUST provide feedback based on…

**Requirement:** Managers or designated reviewers MUST provide feedback based on observed work and agreed expectations, explain evidence and impact, invite context, and identify actionable next steps. Material feedback MUST not be delivered for the first time as an undocumented consequential decision.

### PPL-07.03 — Evaluation MUST consider outcome quality, user and team…

**Requirement:** Evaluation MUST consider outcome quality, user and team impact, role scope, constraints, collaboration, incident learning, and opportunities provided. Metrics MUST be contextual and MUST NOT be the sole basis for pay, promotion, discipline, or separation decisions.

### PPL-07.04 — Before attributing failure to an individual, review workload…

**Requirement:** Before attributing failure to an individual, review workload, unclear authority, missing training, unsafe incentives, tools, dependencies, staffing, and process design. People remain accountable for intentional or negligent conduct under policy, but systemic contributors MUST be addressed as well.

### PPL-07.05 — When capability gaps are remediable, define support…

**Requirement:** When capability gaps are remediable, define support, mentoring/training, time, expected improvement evidence, and review date. Promotion and recognition criteria MUST be role-related, consistently applied, conflict-reviewed, and accessible under applicable policy.

### PPL-07.06 — Compensation, promotion, discipline, performance plans…

**Requirement:** Compensation, promotion, discipline, performance plans, termination, or other consequential employment decisions MUST be made by authorized roles using documented evidence, conflict checks, approved policy, and qualified HR/legal review when required by jurisdiction.

### PPL-07.07 — A person MUST be able to correct factual errors…

**Requirement:** A person MUST be able to correct factual errors and request review of a consequential decision through a route not controlled solely by the original decision-maker where practical. Reviewers MUST consider evidence and policy consistently and protect against retaliation.

### PPL-07.08 — Performance notes, compensation, health, accommodation…

**Requirement:** Performance notes, compensation, health, accommodation, and corrective-action records MUST have restricted access, retention and deletion rules, and data minimization. Use aggregate team-level learning without exposing individual details unnecessarily.

### PPL-07.09 — The responsible owner MUST periodically review whether expectations…

**Requirement:** The responsible owner MUST periodically review whether expectations, metrics, workload, opportunity, feedback, and outcomes produce unexplained disparities or perverse incentives. Investigate concerns and update the process based on evidence without assuming a metric alone proves discrimination or fairness.

## Prohibited conditions

- Do not use a single productivity metric, commit count, hours online, or model-generated score as a complete measure of a person’s contribution.
- Do not surprise a person with a consequential decision based on expectations they were not given.
- Do not retaliate against a person for protected reporting, accommodation requests, or raising a good-faith concern.

## Decision procedure

Determine whether a decision is developmental, recognition-related, compensation-related, disciplinary, or separation-related; apply the matching approved policy and authority. Check evidence quality, role scope, bias/conflict, system conditions, privacy, jurisdiction, and the available review path before acting.

## Assessment procedure

### Examine

- Sample a complete instance of the performance development recognition and corrective action workflow from trigger through owner, action, approval, record, user/stakeholder outcome, and closure.
- Inspect the current role map, procedure, access/records, exception, and supporting evidence; confirm the actual workflow matches the written one.

### Interview

- Interview the person performing the work and the accountable owner separately where practical. Ask how they know they are authorized, where to escalate, what happens on failure, and where the durable record lives.

### Test

Sample routine feedback, a development plan, a promotion/recognition decision, a corrective action, and a factual correction request. Verify expectations were known, evidence is relevant, conflicts and system factors were examined, proper authority/review was used, and records are protected.

## Assessment determination

Pass requires every applicable MUST to be followed and supported by current evidence. Fail applies when authority is unclear, an action bypasses required review, a person cannot identify the safe next step, a required record or safeguard is absent, or evidence is stale. Not applicable requires a stated condition and owner rationale.

## Required evidence

- Workflow record identifying applicable PPL-07 controls, functional owners, approvals, affected requirements, and outcome.
- Relevant access, training, decision, communication, support, audit, or corrective-action evidence, minimized to the data needed to assess the control.
- Approved exception with owner, risk, compensating control, expiry/review date, and revalidation plan when the standard cannot be met.

## Exceptions and nonconformance

A MUST deviation requires approval by an owner authorized for the affected risk, with independent review where practical. Record scope, rationale, impact, interim protection, remediation owner, and expiry or review trigger. Applicable law, contractual duties, safety obligations, and user rights cannot be waived by an internal exception.
