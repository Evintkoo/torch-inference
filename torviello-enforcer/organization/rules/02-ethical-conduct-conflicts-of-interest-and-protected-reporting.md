# Ethical Conduct Conflicts of Interest and Protected Reporting

**Control family:** organization  
**Control document:** ORG-02  
**Requirement trace:** ORG-004–ORG-005, SO-001–SO-005, SO-010–SO-014, ED-014  
**Status:** Normative project control

## Control objective

People must be able to act honestly, identify conflicts, raise concerns, and receive impartial handling without retaliation. The process must protect reporters and subjects while preserving fair review and applicable legal obligations.

## Applicability

This control applies whenever a person, team, contractor, coding agent, automated agent, or delegated function performs an activity in scope. Responsibilities are functional roles, not mandated job titles or headcount; one person may hold multiple roles when conflicts and approval-separation risks are controlled. Apply jurisdiction-specific employment, safety, privacy, consumer, and contract rules through an accountable qualified owner.

## Normative requirements

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" are interpreted as described by IETF BCP 14 only when they appear in all capitals.

### ORG-02.01 — The organization MUST maintain an accessible conduct policy covering…

**Requirement:** The organization MUST maintain an accessible conduct policy covering honesty in records and claims, respectful behavior, confidentiality, responsible system/data use, gifts and inducements, conflicts, misuse of authority, and how to raise concerns. The policy MUST identify accountable owners, applicable scope, training, review date, and consequences under approved procedures.

### ORG-02.02 — A person MUST disclose an actual, potential…

**Requirement:** A person MUST disclose an actual, potential, or reasonably perceived conflict before participating in the affected hiring, procurement, model/vendor selection, security review, incident investigation, compensation, or commercial decision. The authorized reviewer MUST decide recusal, independent review, disclosure, or other mitigation and retain a proportionate record.

### ORG-02.03 — Rules for gifts, hospitality, referral payments…

**Requirement:** Rules for gifts, hospitality, referral payments, personal investments, outside work, and use of organizational information MUST be set by the accountable owner and reviewed against applicable law and contract. A person MUST seek review before accepting an item or commitment that could influence a decision, create a conflict, or imply organizational endorsement.

### ORG-02.04 — People MUST be able to report suspected wrongdoing…

**Requirement:** People MUST be able to report suspected wrongdoing, unsafe practice, policy breach, retaliation, privacy/security incident, or serious product harm through at least one route independent of the person implicated. The route MUST explain confidentiality limits, urgent escalation, evidence preservation, and available external/legal avenues where applicable.

### ORG-02.05 — A designated handler MUST acknowledge, classify urgency…

**Requirement:** A designated handler MUST acknowledge, classify urgency and risk, preserve relevant evidence, check conflicts, assign an impartial investigator or reviewer, and communicate expected next steps. Reports involving leadership, the normal handler, or a conflict MUST be routed to an independent alternative.

### ORG-02.06 — Access to reports and investigation records MUST be limited…

**Requirement:** Access to reports and investigation records MUST be limited to need-to-know roles. The process MUST prohibit retaliation, protect against unnecessary disclosure, allow a fair opportunity to respond where appropriate, and distinguish allegation, evidence, finding, and action.

### ORG-02.07 — The handler MUST record disposition, actions, rationale…

**Requirement:** The handler MUST record disposition, actions, rationale, and any required escalation or legal hold. The reporter SHOULD receive a status or closure update within confidentiality limits; trends and control failures MUST inform corrective action without identifying people unnecessarily.

## Prohibited conditions

- Do not discourage, punish, threaten, or disadvantage a person for making a good-faith report or participating in a review.
- Do not promise absolute confidentiality where disclosure may be legally or operationally required.
- Do not investigate a concern where the handler has an unmanaged conflict.

## Decision procedure

Choose reporting channel, handler, evidence scope, and escalation based on severity, urgency, subject seniority, confidentiality, applicable legal duties, and risk of retaliation or evidence loss. Separate fact-finding from decision on corrective action where feasible; document why any independence safeguard is unavailable.

## Assessment procedure

### Examine

- Sample a complete instance of the ethical conduct conflicts of interest and protected reporting workflow from trigger through owner, action, approval, record, user/stakeholder outcome, and closure.
- Inspect the current role map, procedure, access/records, exception, and supporting evidence; confirm the actual workflow matches the written one.

### Interview

- Interview the person performing the work and the accountable owner separately where practical. Ask how they know they are authorized, where to escalate, what happens on failure, and where the durable record lives.

### Test

Use scenario reviews for a concern about a manager, a security incident, a conflict in procurement, and an urgent safety issue. Verify alternate routing, restricted access, acknowledgment, impartial assignment, non-retaliation follow-up, record retention, and closure.

## Assessment determination

Pass requires every applicable MUST to be followed and supported by current evidence. Fail applies when authority is unclear, an action bypasses required review, a person cannot identify the safe next step, a required record or safeguard is absent, or evidence is stale. Not applicable requires a stated condition and owner rationale.

## Required evidence

- Workflow record identifying applicable ORG-02 controls, functional owners, approvals, affected requirements, and outcome.
- Relevant access, training, decision, communication, support, audit, or corrective-action evidence, minimized to the data needed to assess the control.
- Approved exception with owner, risk, compensating control, expiry/review date, and revalidation plan when the standard cannot be met.

## Exceptions and nonconformance

A MUST deviation requires approval by an owner authorized for the affected risk, with independent review where practical. Record scope, rationale, impact, interim protection, remediation owner, and expiry or review trigger. Applicable law, contractual duties, safety obligations, and user rights cannot be waived by an internal exception.
