# Product Discovery and Delivery Lifecycle Requirements

These requirements define how work from the manual review and later product changes moves from a reported need to an operated and evaluated outcome. They are outcome requirements, not a mandate to use one product methodology. The depth of evidence and approval must scale with user impact, uncertainty, cost, safety, security, data sensitivity, and reversibility.

| ID | Priority | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| [PDL-001](items/PDL-001.md) | MUST | Record the source, date, context, affected actors, and decision owner for each material problem or opportunity. | A reviewer can distinguish observed evidence, stakeholder opinion, and assumptions and can locate the accountable owner. |
| [PDL-002](items/PDL-002.md) | MUST | Frame the problem and desired outcome before selecting a solution. | A discovery record states current behavior, who is affected, severity/frequency, outcome measure, scope, non-goals, and what evidence would disconfirm the framing. |
| [PDL-003](items/PDL-003.md) | MUST | Gather evidence proportionate to the impact and uncertainty of the decision. | User/operator research, repository/runtime evidence, support reports, or experiments are recorded with methods, sample/context limits, consent/privacy constraints where relevant, findings, and confidence. |
| [PDL-004](items/PDL-004.md) | MUST | Identify affected and excluded user groups, workflows, accessibility needs, operating environments, and foreseeable misuse. | The specification records who benefits, who bears cost, relevant platform and accessibility constraints, and material adverse impacts. |
| [PDL-005](items/PDL-005.md) | MUST | Maintain assumptions, unknowns, constraints, risks, dependencies, and open decisions explicitly. | Each item has an owner, evidence/decision needed, due or review trigger, impact if unresolved, and status; unresolved release-critical items block commitment. |
| [PDL-006](items/PDL-006.md) | MUST | Express requirements as uniquely identified, necessary, bounded, feasible, consistent, verifiable outcomes with rationale and source. | A reviewer can trace each requirement to stakeholder need or approved constraint and identify a pass/fail verification method without interpreting intent. |
| [PDL-007](items/PDL-007.md) | MUST | Specify relevant quality attributes and adverse behavior alongside functional behavior. | Applicable security, privacy, reliability, performance, resource, portability, accessibility, operability, compatibility, and maintainability concerns have measurable scenarios or justified non-applicability. |
| [PDL-008](items/PDL-008.md) | MUST | Compare credible solution alternatives before committing to a material product, architecture, vendor, data, or workflow choice. | The decision record names constraints, alternatives including doing nothing, evaluation criteria, evidence, tradeoffs, reversibility, owner, and reconsideration triggers. |
| [PDL-009](items/PDL-009.md) | MUST | Use prototypes, spikes, benchmarks, and experiments to answer stated questions, not to imply production readiness. | Each investigation has a hypothesis/question, method, success and stop criteria, safety/data limits, owner, result, limitations, and a decision that follows from the result. |
| [PDL-010](items/PDL-010.md) | MUST | Prioritize work using explicit value, risk, urgency, dependency, effort, and opportunity-cost considerations. | Ordered work has an owner and rationale; deferrals document impact, risk acceptance authority, and revisit trigger. |
| [PDL-011](items/PDL-011.md) | MUST | Produce an implementation plan for committed work that decomposes scope into reviewable outcomes and dependencies. | The plan identifies sequence, owners/roles, estimates or uncertainty ranges, milestones, resources, verification, documentation, migration, release, rollback, and contingency for material risks. |
| [PDL-012](items/PDL-012.md) | MUST | Re-plan when evidence, scope, assumptions, dependencies, cost, or risk changes materially. | The change is assessed against approved outcomes and decisions; affected requirements, plan, estimate, stakeholders, and approvals are updated before proceeding. |
| [PDL-013](items/PDL-013.md) | MUST | Trace accepted requirements through design, implementation, verification, release, and operation. | A trace record shows requirement-to-decision/design-to-change-to-check-to-release evidence, with explicit defer, reject, or not-applicable dispositions. |
| [PDL-014](items/PDL-014.md) | MUST | Define acceptance before implementation and evaluate it against observed evidence after implementation. | Acceptance criteria state expected observable behavior, boundary/failure cases, evidence source, reviewer/decision authority, and residual limitations. |
| [PDL-015](items/PDL-015.md) | MUST | Review outcomes after release or operational adoption and feed evidence into subsequent decisions. | The owner defines an appropriate signal and review point; results, incidents, user feedback, regressions, and unmet outcomes produce a recorded continue/change/rollback/retire decision. |
| [PDL-016](items/PDL-016.md) | MUST | Preserve decision and discovery records while protecting personal, confidential, security-sensitive, and commercially sensitive information. | Records are stored in approved locations with access, retention, redaction, and publication decisions appropriate to their classification; public artifacts contain only approved content. |
| [PDL-017](items/PDL-017.md) | SHOULD | Use the lightest artifact set that still preserves traceability, reviewability, and risk control. | A lightweight change records its scope, expected behavior, relevant IDs, risks, and checks; omitted artifacts have a reason tied to low impact and reversibility. |

The discovery and planning rules are indexed in [`../torviello-enforcer/product-discovery-and-delivery/trails/00-references-trails.md`](../torviello-enforcer/product-discovery-and-delivery/trails/00-references-trails.md). All material work follows the lifecycle controls for its domain and these cross-cutting requirements.
