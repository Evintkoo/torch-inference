# Organization, People, and Stakeholder Requirements

These requirements define organization-wide operating outcomes for the people and teams that plan, build, release, operate, support, and govern Torviello. They define required functions and evidence, not a specific headcount, legal employer structure, reporting hierarchy, work schedule, or jurisdictional HR policy. Assign named owners and local policies through approved decision records before the relevant activity is performed.

## Authority, roles, and decisions

| ID | Priority | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ORG-001](items/ORG-001.md) | MUST | Every material responsibility, decision, approval, and operational duty shall have an accountable functional owner and a documented delegate or escalation path. | A role/responsibility map identifies accountable, responsible, consulted, and informed functions for material decisions and workflows; no approval depends only on an undocumented person-specific convention. |
| [ORG-002](items/ORG-002.md) | MUST | Decision authority shall be proportional to impact, reversibility, exposure, and affected stakeholders. | Decision classes define who may decide, required consultation/review, evidence, approval threshold, and escalation; high-impact or irreversible decisions receive independent review where practical. |
| [ORG-003](items/ORG-003.md) | MUST | Delegation shall have a defined scope, duration, limits, and accountability. | A delegate can identify the authority granted, the decisions excluded, the record required, and when the authority ends or returns to the delegator. |
| [ORG-004](items/ORG-004.md) | MUST | Material conflicts of interest, gifts, outside commitments, or incentives that could impair independent judgment shall be disclosed and managed. | A confidential reporting and recusal/review process exists; decisions record mitigation without exposing unnecessary personal information. |
| [ORG-005](items/ORG-005.md) | MUST | People shall have an accessible and protected path to report safety, security, privacy, compliance, quality, and conduct concerns. | Reporting channels identify receipt, impartial triage, confidentiality limits, non-retaliation expectations, escalation, closure, and appropriate records; urgent hazards have an immediate route. |

## Workforce lifecycle and capability

| ID | Priority | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ORG-010](items/ORG-010.md) | MUST | Work requiring specialized authority or access shall be assigned to people with appropriate competence and authorization. | Role profiles state required skills, training, access, supervision, and review; assignments and exceptions are recorded and reassessed when duties change. |
| [ORG-011](items/ORG-011.md) | MUST | Join, role-change, leave, and separation workflows shall update responsibilities, access, credentials, equipment, records, and knowledge handoff. | A role transition checklist has an owner, event trigger, completion evidence, revocation/transfer checks, and exception handling; personnel security controls govern access details. |
| [ORG-012](items/ORG-012.md) | MUST | Personnel shall receive practical instruction for policies and risks relevant to their duties before or when they acquire the associated authority. | Training is role-based, recorded, updated after material changes/incidents, and tested for understanding or task competence where risk requires it. |
| [ORG-013](items/ORG-013.md) | MUST | Work practices shall protect dignity, safety, fair treatment, accessibility, and freedom from retaliation, subject to applicable law and approved policy. | A current jurisdiction-reviewed policy and independent escalation route are available; reports are handled consistently, confidentially to the extent possible, and without retaliation. |

## Planning, collaboration, and knowledge

| ID | Priority | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ORG-020](items/ORG-020.md) | MUST | Work shall have a defined request, owner, priority, expected outcome, dependencies, and acceptance evidence proportionate to risk. | Teams can identify why work was accepted, who owns delivery, what is explicitly out of scope, and how completion is verified. |
| [ORG-021](items/ORG-021.md) | MUST | Decisions that affect product behavior, architecture, security, privacy, cost, support, or commitments shall be recorded with rationale and affected owners. | The decision record identifies context, alternatives, constraints, consequences, approver, affected requirements, and review triggers; transient discussion alone is not the durable record. |
| [ORG-022](items/ORG-022.md) | MUST | Handoffs between people, teams, time zones, and vendors shall preserve state, ownership, deadlines, dependencies, risks, and next actions. | A receiver can continue without relying on the sender being available; high-risk handoffs receive explicit acceptance and escalation if not acknowledged. |
| [ORG-023](items/ORG-023.md) | MUST | Organizational knowledge needed to operate, maintain, secure, support, and recover the product shall be discoverable, current, access-controlled, and transferable. | Critical procedures have an owner, source of truth, review trigger, recovery path, and backup knowledge holder or documented key-person risk. |
| [ORG-024](items/ORG-024.md) | MUST | Meetings and synchronous collaboration shall have a purpose, necessary participants, decisions/actions, and durable record when they create commitments or change direction. | Material decisions and assigned actions are recorded in the authoritative system; routine discussions do not create undocumented policy or approvals. |

## External commitments and stakeholder trust

| ID | Priority | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ORG-030](items/ORG-030.md) | MUST | External statements and commitments shall be made only by authorized roles using verified, current, and appropriately qualified information. | Approved owners exist for product, security, privacy, support, legal, and commercial statements; claims have evidence, scope, date, and review triggers. |
| [ORG-031](items/ORG-031.md) | MUST | User requests, complaints, accessibility issues, security reports, and service-impact feedback shall be received, classified, acknowledged, owned, and resolved or escalated. | A support process records case identity, impact, response target, owner, status, user communication, resolution, and trend/learning while minimizing collected data. |
| [ORG-032](items/ORG-032.md) | MUST | Customers and other affected stakeholders shall receive accurate, timely, audience-appropriate communication about material service incidents and changes. | Incident communication roles, decision authority, approved channels, update cadence, disclosure review, and closure criteria are defined for each supported service profile. |
| [ORG-033](items/ORG-033.md) | MUST | Suppliers, contractors, and other business associates shall receive only the access and information required for an approved purpose and contract. | Engagements identify sponsor, scope, data/access, security and privacy conditions, approval, monitoring, exit, and return/deletion obligations. |

## Governance, compliance, and improvement

| ID | Priority | Requirement | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ORG-040](items/ORG-040.md) | MUST | Applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and policy obligations shall be identified by jurisdiction and product activity and assigned to an accountable owner. | An obligation register links scope, applicability rationale, owner, evidence, review cadence, and changes requiring specialist advice; no rule substitutes for qualified legal advice. |
| [ORG-041](items/ORG-041.md) | MUST | Organizational risks shall be identified, evaluated, treated, accepted by an authorized owner, and reviewed when assumptions or exposure change. | Risk records state context, affected stakeholders, likelihood/impact, treatment, residual risk, owner, approval, monitoring, and expiry/review trigger. |
| [ORG-042](items/ORG-042.md) | MUST | Material incidents, complaints, audit findings, near misses, and repeated workflow failures shall result in owned corrective action and effectiveness review. | Corrective actions identify cause and contributing conditions, containment, owner, deadline, linked controls, verification, and evidence that recurrence risk decreased. |
| [ORG-043](items/ORG-043.md) | MUST | Organizational records shall have a purpose, owner, access policy, retention trigger, approved storage, and secure disposal or archival path. | Records schedules cover decisions, approvals, support, incidents, training, contracts, and audit evidence; deletion and legal-hold exceptions are authorized and evidenced. |
| [ORG-044](items/ORG-044.md) | MUST | Resource and spending authority shall be explicit for commitments that create recurring cost, legal exposure, customer dependency, or operational burden. | Procurement and commitment records identify sponsor, budget authority, alternatives, total lifecycle cost, terms, renewal/exit, and required specialist approvals. |
| [ORG-045](items/ORG-045.md) | MUST | Recruitment and selection shall use role-related criteria, authorized decisions, privacy safeguards, and fair, documented evaluation. | A hiring process defines the role need, essential criteria, interview/assessment method, conflict checks, lawful reference/screening steps, decision owner, candidate communications, and protected record retention. |
| [ORG-046](items/ORG-046.md) | MUST | Performance, development, recognition, and corrective decisions shall use defined role expectations, relevant evidence, consistent process, and a review path. | People know the expectations and feedback cadence; consequential decisions have an accountable decision owner, evidence, conflict review, applicable policy/legal review, and an appeal or correction mechanism. |
