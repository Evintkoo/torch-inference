# Agents: requirements/01-product-requirements

Scope: product requirement sets and their acceptance criteria.

## Instructions

- Keep requirement IDs unique and linked from `requirements/README.md`.
- Use measurable acceptance criteria and separate requirements from implementation choices.
- Do not weaken or delete an approved requirement to make a change pass; changes require explicit user authorization.
- Keep normative engineering rules in `torviello-enforcer/` and link to them instead of duplicating them.
