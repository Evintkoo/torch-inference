# ORG-022 — Handoffs between people, teams, time zones, and vendors shall preserve state, ownership, deadlines, dependencies, risks, and next actions

**Requirement ID:** ORG-022  
**Priority:** MUST  
**Source:** [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md) — Planning, collaboration, and knowledge  
**Status:** Active requirement; no implementation or evaluation evidence is recorded in this repository.  
**Rule controls:** COL-01, COL-03, OPS-06  
**Acceptance checks:** None recorded  
**Open decisions:** None recorded

## 1. Normative requirement

Handoffs between people, teams, time zones, and vendors shall preserve state, ownership, deadlines, dependencies, risks, and next actions.

## 2. Why this requirement exists

This requirement is part of **Planning, collaboration, and knowledge** in [organization-and-people.md](../organization-and-people.md). It is traced to 3 rule control(s): COL-01, COL-03, OPS-06. Those controls state the following purpose:

- **COL-01 — Communication Meetings Reviews and Handoffs:** Communication must preserve context, ownership, confidentiality, and actionability across people, teams, time zones, and tools. Synchronous conversations are for interaction; authoritative decisions and commitments must remain findable after participants leave.
- **COL-03 — Cross-Team Coordination Review and Escalation:** Work that crosses domain, team, or organizational boundaries must identify interface owners, dependencies, decisions, risks, and review points. A handoff is complete only when the receiving owner can act and the sending owner’s remaining accountability is clear.
- **OPS-06 — Operational Roles Coverage Handover and Human Continuity:** Operational resilience depends on people knowing who is on duty, what they may do, how to hand work over, and how the service remains safe when a key person or supplier is unavailable. Coverage must match the service commitment without inventing unsupported staffing promises.

Acceptance is not satisfied by citing these controls. The implementation must demonstrate the observable result in section 6, and the mapped rule clauses in section 5 define what must hold at the enforcement boundary.

## 3. Priority and interpretation

This requirement has **MUST** priority under the repository's requirement conventions. A MUST requirement is needed for the stated product direction or a safe release. ### COL-01 — Communication Meetings Reviews and Handoffs

*Domain:* [collaboration-and-communication](../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/README.md) · *Rule:* [01-communication-meetings-reviews-and-handoffs.md](../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/rules/01-communication-meetings-reviews-and-handoffs.md)

**Control objective.** Communication must preserve context, ownership, confidentiality, and actionability across people, teams, time zones, and tools. Synchronous conversations are for interaction; authoritative decisions and commitments must remain findable after participants leave.

#### COL-01.01 — The sender MUST use an approved channel appropriate…

**Requirement:** The sender MUST use an approved channel appropriate to urgency, audience, confidentiality, retention, accessibility, and audit needs. Sensitive information MUST use an access-controlled approved system; urgent incidents MUST use the defined escalation route rather than waiting for ordinary asynchronous response.

#### COL-01.02 — A work or decision request MUST state context…

**Requirement:** A work or decision request MUST state context, desired outcome, owner/recipient, decision deadline, impact of delay, supporting links, and response needed. If the recipient lacks authority or context, they MUST redirect or escalate rather than implying approval.

#### COL-01.03 — A meeting MUST have a clear purpose, expected…

**Requirement:** A meeting MUST have a clear purpose, expected decision/output, necessary participants, and accessible material. The facilitator MUST distinguish information sharing, consultation, decision, and approval; attendance or silence MUST NOT be treated as consent.

#### COL-01.04 — A meeting or review that changes scope, risk…

**Requirement:** A meeting or review that changes scope, risk acceptance, product behavior, resource commitment, external messaging, or ownership MUST record decision, rationale, approver, dissent/conditions where material, action owner, and due/review date in the authoritative record.

#### COL-01.05 — A handoff MUST include current state, owner…

**Requirement:** A handoff MUST include current state, owner, context, links/artifacts, user impact, dependencies, deadlines, risks, pending decisions, and next action. High-risk handoffs MUST be explicitly accepted; define escalation if acknowledgment does not arrive.

#### COL-01.06 — Teams MUST provide enough written context and reasonable response…

**Requirement:** Teams MUST provide enough written context and reasonable response expectations for people not present in a live meeting. Critical decisions MUST not be made solely in a private channel inaccessible to impacted owners or reviewers.

#### COL-01.07 — When owners disagree, restate the decision to be…

**Requirement:** When owners disagree, restate the decision to be made, evidence and constraints, applicable control, options, and accountable decision-maker. Preserve respectful conduct; escalate unresolved high-impact conflicts to the named authority and record the result.

#### COL-01.08 — Recipients MUST respect channel membership and classification, verify…

**Requirement:** Recipients MUST respect channel membership and classification, verify recipients before sending sensitive material, and correct misdelivery through the incident process. Forwarding, screenshots, transcripts, recordings, or AI summaries MUST follow the applicable consent, privacy, retention, and access rules.

### COL-03 — Cross-Team Coordination Review and Escalation

*Domain:* [collaboration-and-communication](../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/README.md) · *Rule:* [03-cross-team-coordination-review-and-escalation.md](../../torviello-enforcer/collaboration-and-communication/rules/03-cross-team-coordination-review-and-escalation.md)

**Control objective.** Work that crosses domain, team, or organizational boundaries must identify interface owners, dependencies, decisions, risks, and review points. A handoff is complete only when the receiving owner can act and the sending owner’s remaining accountability is clear.

#### COL-03.01 — A change affecting shared APIs, data, security…

**Requirement:** A change affecting shared APIs, data, security boundaries, deployment, costs, documentation, customer support, legal terms, or release schedules MUST identify impacted owners and required reviews before the commitment becomes difficult to reverse.

#### COL-03.02 — Cross-team work MUST define inputs, outputs, responsibility…

**Requirement:** Cross-team work MUST define inputs, outputs, responsibility boundaries, response expectations, version/compatibility, failure escalation, and acceptance evidence. Each team MUST know what it provides, consumes, and owns after handoff.

#### COL-03.03 — A dependency MUST have a provider owner, consumer…

**Requirement:** A dependency MUST have a provider owner, consumer owner, expected date/contract, status, failure consequence, and fallback or escalation. Critical-path changes MUST be re-evaluated when assumptions or delivery dates change.

#### COL-03.04 — Reviewers MUST identify blocking correctness, safety, privacy…

**Requirement:** Reviewers MUST identify blocking correctness, safety, privacy, security, legal, compatibility, or operability concerns distinctly from recommendations and style preferences. Authors MUST resolve or explicitly document disposition; reviewers MUST not use personal preference as an undisclosed mandatory standard.

#### COL-03.05 — Participants MUST state the decision, available evidence…

**Requirement:** Participants MUST state the decision, available evidence, requirements, controls, risks, alternatives, and accountable decider. If evidence is insufficient, define a safe experiment or time-bounded discovery task rather than converting an assumption into policy.

#### COL-03.06 — When an owner is unavailable or teams disagree under…

**Requirement:** When an owner is unavailable or teams disagree under a deadline, escalate to the delegated authority and identify what can safely proceed. Do not bypass a required security, privacy, financial, release, or external-commitment approval to preserve schedule.

#### COL-03.07 — The coordinating owner MUST confirm the receiver accepted…

**Requirement:** The coordinating owner MUST confirm the receiver accepted the work, documentation and records are updated, remaining risks and actions have owners, and each team knows whether support ends or continues.

### OPS-06 — Operational Roles Coverage Handover and Human Continuity

*Domain:* [operations](../../torviello-enforcer/operations/README.md) · *Rule:* [06-operational-roles-coverage-handover-and-human-continuity.md](../../torviello-enforcer/operations/rules/06-operational-roles-coverage-handover-and-human-continuity.md)

**Control objective.** Operational resilience depends on people knowing who is on duty, what they may do, how to hand work over, and how the service remains safe when a key person or supplier is unavailable. Coverage must match the service commitment without inventing unsupported staffing promises.

#### OPS-06.01 — Each production profile MUST identify service owner, incident…

**Requirement:** Each production profile MUST identify service owner, incident lead, deploy/release authority, security escalation, data/persistence owner, customer communication owner, and backup/delegate as applicable. State whether each function is staffed continuously, during defined hours, or on best-effort basis.

#### OPS-06.02 — For supported operational channels, state hours, severity…

**Requirement:** For supported operational channels, state hours, severity classes, response targets, escalation contacts, and fallback when the primary owner is unreachable. Public service promises MUST match actual staffing, monitoring, and recovery capability.

#### OPS-06.03 — An operational handover MUST include current service health…

**Requirement:** An operational handover MUST include current service health, open incidents/changes, customer impact, risks, pending actions, access used, deadlines, and next decision. The receiving owner MUST acknowledge material risk and understand their authority.

#### OPS-06.04 — Coverage MUST use named identities and pre-authorized delegates…

**Requirement:** Coverage MUST use named identities and pre-authorized delegates with time-bounded access. Never share accounts or keys to cover an absent operator; emergency access uses the security break-glass process and post-use review.

#### OPS-06.05 — For critical operations, identify single-person and supplier dependencies…

**Requirement:** For critical operations, identify single-person and supplier dependencies, recovery documentation, alternate contacts, and minimum competent coverage. Exercise absence, simultaneous incident, and unavailability of a key vendor at a frequency proportional to impact.

#### OPS-06.06 — Operational processes MUST allow responders to escalate unsafe workload…

**Requirement:** Operational processes MUST allow responders to escalate unsafe workload, conflicting incident tasks, or insufficient authority. Rotate or hand off duties as needed under approved work policies; do not use undocumented availability expectations as a service guarantee.

#### OPS-06.07 — Changes to service hours, product scope, team…

**Requirement:** Changes to service hours, product scope, team structure, vendor, incident severity, or recovery process MUST trigger review of roles, contacts, runbooks, training, and support commitments.

## 8. Prohibited conditions

The following conditions are explicitly prohibited by the mapped controls. Any one of them is sufficient to fail the requirement.

- [COL-01] Do not treat a reaction, meeting attendance, or lack of response as formal approval.
- [COL-01] Do not store the only copy of a business-critical decision in personal chat or email.
- [COL-01] Do not include sensitive personnel, customer, or security details in broad channels.
- [COL-03] Do not assume a shared repository, channel, or manager means shared accountability.
- [COL-03] Do not merge unresolved blocking review concerns into a vague “follow up later” without risk approval.
- [COL-03] Do not create hidden inter-team dependencies through undocumented data formats or privileged access.
- [OPS-06] Do not promise continuous support based on monitoring alone.
- [OPS-06] Do not make one person the only holder of critical recovery knowledge or credentials.
- [OPS-06] Do not leave an incident active without a named lead and next update/decision owner.
